- **Help in one piece or none is far worse than help in pieces.** Holding back every help group that couldn't cover a castle's whole lack, or that was under half the largest enemy group bound for it, took 11 of 66 from `pounce` (run 20261123-470c89's `pounce_onehelp`), though the pieces it held back died in clashes in estuary, causeway and loch. A small group that dies on the road still stops or shrinks the group it meets.
- **A neutral's payers should land together.** `pounce` paid a neutral with two castles whose groups landed 3 to 9 turns apart, and the first lost alone against the full garrison (estuary turns 34 and 40, causeway turn 37). Taking only with payers that land on one turn took 42 of 66 from `pounce` and was confirmed on the training maps (+47 games, 19 maps better, 9 worse; run 20261123-470c89's `pounce_together`).
- **Feeding a front from far full castles doesn't help `pounce` either.** Sending the spare of castles with nothing left to buy, over 6 turns from every enemy castle, to the castle nearest the enemy acted where `pounce`'s stock stood idle (loch, bight, estuary) and took exactly a third, 22 of 66, from it (run 20261123-470c89's `pounce_front`): with iteration 41's longer strikes, the second way of moving its stock to the enemy that lost.
- **One stack castle fed by all the others is a strategy of its own.** A founder on `pounce`'s code whose rear castles buy production only and send everything above their reserve to the castle nearest the enemy, which buys speed 3, took 35 of 66 from `pounce`, 36 from `batchfront_keep` and 40 from `batchfront`, but only 26 from `punisher` and 49 from `ori_mimic` (run 20261123-470c89's `hub`). The winners of real games 478, 480 and 481 played this way.
- **The upgrade that preceded a lost border castle was not always its cause.** In haven (turn 20) the only helper of the thin castle was 7 turns away at speed 1 whether or not it bought production; an upgrade that keeps what a castle within 6 turns lacks acted only on breakwater and was clearly worse in the field (−27 games; run 20261123-470c89's `pounce_upkeep`).
