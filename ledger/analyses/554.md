# Game 554: the three main mistakes

Map claw; the league bot burstguard played team 1 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 99.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 83: castle 3 left thin beside the enemy stack (decided the game)

Enemy castle 4 held 198 after sending 115 at castle 5 (12). Castle 0 (146) sent 84 to castle 5 and kept 62. Castle 3 (63, production 2, speed 1, never upgraded) is 3.2 from castle 4. Castle 4 sent 70 at castle 3 that same turn (and 6 more on turn 85) and took it on turn 86; castle 0 could only add 2 and 2. From castle 3 the enemy struck castle 0 (fell on turn 95) and castle 5 (fell on turn 98).

**The right move:** Send castle 0's remaining 62 to castle 3 as well as the 84 to castle 5: castle 3 then holds 125 against castle 4.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: `send 0->5 x84`, `send 0->3 x62` (its stack is castle 3, the castle nearest the enemy, and it is fed every turn).

## 2. Turn 66: a strike on a castle that outgrows it

Castle 5 (44) sent 30 at enemy castle 7 (4, production 4), six turns away at speed 1; castle 7 would hold 28 or more by then, and enemy castle 1 fed it 4 at a time. It sent 16, 12 and 12 more on turns 69, 71 and 73; none took castle 7. About 70 soldiers were spent, and castle 5 held 12 when castle 4's 115 came for it on turn 82, while castle 4 grew from 73 to 297.

**The right move:** No strike: send castle 5's 44 (and castle 0's 78) to castle 3, to face castle 4.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: `send 0->3 x78`, `send 5->3 x44`.

## 3. Turn 55: a capture that castle 4 took back

Castle 5 sent its 38 at enemy castle 2 (28, production 2) and took it on turn 58 with 2. Enemy castle 4 (20, with 60 landing by turn 60) is 3 away; it sent 19 on turn 61 and retook castle 2 on turn 63.

**The right move:** No strike: keep the 38 (or gather it at castle 3).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: `send 0->3 x34`, `send 5->3 x38`.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 55: `["send 0->3 x34", "send 5->3 x38"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 66: `["send 0->3 x78", "send 5->3 x44"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 83: `["send 0->5 x84", "send 0->5 x11", "send 0->3 x43"]` (avoids the mistake)
