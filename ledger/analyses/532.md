# Game 532: the three main mistakes

Map estuary; our bot batchfront, playing as team 2, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 77.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 60: castle 5 emptied for a neutral beside a stocked enemy castle

Castle 5 (53, production 4) stood 2 map units from the enemy's castle 4 (54, with 36 more landing). It sent 52 at neutral castle 7 (25) and kept 5. Castle 4 sent 30 on turn 61 and took castle 5 on turn 62; castle 5 then fed the enemy's attacks on castles 7, 1 and 3.

**The right move:** No send: keep castle 5's 53 and feed it from castles 1 and 3 (13 each).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: send 1->5 13 and 3->5 13 (castle 5 is its stack).

## 2. Turn 63: a retake of castle 5 that the enemy refills

Castle 5 had just fallen (the enemy's, 5). Castle 1 had sent 15 at it on turn 62 and castle 3 sent 12 more, each keeping 5, while the enemy's castle 4 (25), 2 map units from castle 5, sent 17 and 24 into it on turns 63 and 64. The retake failed and castles 1 and 3 stayed at 5 beside it.

**The right move:** No retake: gather castle 3 into castle 1 and keep it stocked against castle 5.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: send 3->1 13.

## 3. Turn 66: a 7-turn strike at castle 6 while castle 1 sits beside the enemy

The enemy's castle 5 held 29 (with 30 more landing) 3 turns from our castle 1 (13). Castles 1 and 3 sent 12 each at the enemy's castle 6 (2), 7 turns away, keeping 5 each, and 8 and 4 each more on turns 68 and 69; castle 4 refilled castle 6, which held. Castle 5 sent 25 at castle 1 on turn 70 (it fell on turn 72) and 42 at castle 3 on turn 72 (it fell on turn 76).

**The right move:** No strike: gather castle 3 into castle 1 against castle 5.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: send 3->1 13.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 60: `["send 1->5 x13", "send 3->5 x13"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 63: `["send 3->1 x13"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 66: `["send 3->1 x13"]` (avoids the mistake)
