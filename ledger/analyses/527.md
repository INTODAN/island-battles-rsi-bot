# Game 527: the three main mistakes

Map bight; the hosted bot was speedhelp_fullfeed, playing as team 2, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 149.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 141: castle 5 left to fall (decided the game)

The enemy had stacked castle 2 to 204 and sent 153 (speed 3) at castle 5, which held 90; it landed in 3. Castle 1 had 72 at speed 3, 2 turns away. We sent 1->5 19 only; castle 5 fell on turn 143 with 117 in it (the enemy kept 32) and castle 1 fell on turn 148.

**The right move:** Send all of castle 1 (72) to castle 5: 90 + 72 + growth holds 153. Better, feed castle 5 from castle 1 on turn 138.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: help>5 1:52 plus feed 1:20, all 72 (on turn 138 it also moves castle 1's 60 into castle 5 and buys speed there).

## 2. Turn 121: a strike on castle 3 from eight turns away

Castle 3 was the enemy's with 4; castle 5 (38, speed 2) is 7.4 from it. We sent 5->3 38 and 1->3 10; the enemy sent 14 from castle 2 on turn 122 and castle 3 was not taken. 48 soldiers were lost while the enemy fed castle 2 from 30 to 188 by turn 138.

**The right move:** No strike: feed castle 1 into castle 5 and buy speed there.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: up 5 speed and 1->5 12.

## 3. Turn 8: the first soldiers sent to the contested middle

Castle 1 had 21; neutral castle 2 (15, production 1) sits in the middle, as close to the enemy's castle 0 as to ours, and neutral castle 5 (18) is 2.8 from castle 1. We sent 1->2 19 and kept feeding castle 2 (8, 11, 10, 11) while the enemy sent its own; we took it on turn 22 with 4, lost it on turn 26, and the two middle castles changed hands more than twenty-five times after that. Castle 5 was taken only on turn 38.

**The right move:** Take castle 5 first (1->5 20), then fight for the middle with two producing castles.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: smash>5 1:20.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 8: `["send 1->5 x20"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 121: `["send 1->5 x12"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 141: `["send 1->5 x52", "send 1->5 x20"]` (avoids the mistake)
