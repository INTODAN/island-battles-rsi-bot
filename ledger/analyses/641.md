# Game 641: the three main mistakes

Map gyre; our bot batchfront_keep played as team 1 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 60.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 40: castle 4 emptied for neutral castle 9

Castle 4 (48) was our front castle, 3.6 units from enemy castle 3 (34, with 16 and 12 landing on it). It sent 40 at neutral castle 9 (25), leaving 8. Castle 3 sent 41 at castle 4 on turn 41 and castle 5 sent 20 at castle 9: castle 9 was ours for one turn (43) and castle 4 fell on turn 44. The 40 and castle 4's production 4 were lost.

**The right move:** Keep castle 4's 48 home (feed it with castle 0) and leave castle 9.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats in kind: no capture, but it gathers into castle 2, sending 4->2 48 and 0->2 9, which leaves castle 4 empty beside castle 3. `stackOf` breaks the tie (castles 2 and 4 are equally near an enemy castle) by the lowest id.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats in kind: stack@4, but it smashes neutral castle 8 (25) with 27, 1.8 units from castle 3, which could retake it at once, and keeps 21 + 9 in castle 4. Its capture pricing against nearby enemy castles (`smash`) applies only to enemy-owned targets; a neutral costs its garrison plus 2.

## 2. Turn 50: castle 0 left to fall

Castle 3 sent 34 and castle 5 14 at castle 0 (21), landing in 5. Castle 6 (21) could reach it in time with speed 2 bought. We sent nothing; castle 0 fell on turn 54 by 7 (37 against 44), and castle 6 on turn 59.

**The right move:** Buy speed 2 at castle 6 and send 8 (or more) to castle 0.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: help>0, buys speed at castle 6 and sends 6->0 8.

## 3. Turn 47: castle 0 sent 12 at castle 9

Castle 9 (6) was the enemy's, with 24 more of its soldiers on the way. Castle 0 (21) sent 12 at it, which took nothing. Kept home, those 12 would have held castle 0 on turn 54 (49 against 44). Castle 2 (30) could not be saved: 39 landed on it in 3.

**The right move:** No strike; keep castle 0's 21 home.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats in kind: no strike, but it buys speed 3 at castle 0 (20 soldiers) to send 1 soldier to castle 2, which still falls (34 against 39), and castle 0 is left with 0. In `defend`, a slow helper buys its next speed level whenever it can pay it and still give 1, without checking that what it gives covers the lack.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: the same speed 3 at castle 0 and 0->2 1; its `defend` is unchanged.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 40: `["send 0->4 x9"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 47: `["send 2->6 x30", "send 6->0 x9"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 50: `["up 6 speed", "send 6->0 x8"]` (avoids the mistake)
