# Game 602: the three main mistakes

Map claw; the league bot was fullfeed_unfreeze, playing as team 1, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 82.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 69: castle 5 left to fall

Castle 5 (12, production 4) had the enemy's 40 and 24 landing in 5. Castles 0 (16) and 3 (24) were 2.8 and 4 away; we sent 15 and 16 to castle 2 (32) instead. Castle 5 fell on turn 73 with 28 in it, and the enemy kept it.

**The right move:** Help castle 5: 0->5 16 and 3->5 17.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: exactly 0->5 16 and 3->5 17 (`defend`).

## 2. Turn 73: castle 0 emptied beside a castle about to fall

Castle 5 was falling in 1 to the enemy's 64, 2.8 from castle 0 (16); the enemy's 95 was landing on castle 2 (56) in 2 to 3. Castle 0 fed 16 into castle 2, keeping 4, and castle 3 sent 17 at castle 4 (12, fed). Castle 2 fell on turn 75 anyway; castle 5's 25 took castle 0 on turn 77 and castle 3 fell on turn 81.

**The right move:** Keep castle 0's 16 home and send castle 3's 25 into castle 2 (in time, after buying speed).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats in part: no strike, buys speed at castle 3 and helps 3->2 5, but also feeds 0->2 16, emptying castle 0. `feedStack` gives a rear castle its `safeOut`, which counts only groups bound for that castle, not the enemy's 64 that is about to take castle 5 next door.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: the feed reserve (an enemy castle that lands on castle 0 before the stack's help could) keeps castle 0's 16 home.

## 3. Turn 48: three split waves at two castles

Castle 3 (43) sent 13 at castle 2 (13) and 14 at castle 4 (8), and castle 0 12 at castle 2; the enemy answered with 25 at castle 4. Castle 2 was taken on turn 52 and changed hands four more times; castle 4 was never taken.

**The right move:** No waves: buy production at castles 3 and 5 and gather at castle 3.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: buys production 4 and speed 2 at castle 3, production 3 at castle 5, and feeds 0->3 12.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 48: `["up 3 generation_amount", "up 3 speed", "up 5 generation_amount", "send 0->3 x12"]`
- turn 69: `["send 0->5 x16", "send 3->5 x17"]`
- turn 73: `["send 0->3 x16"]`
