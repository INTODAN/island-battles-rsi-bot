# Game 595: the three main mistakes

Map estuary; our bot pounce_reach played as team 1 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost on turn 101.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 85: castle 0 short of help while we struck (decided the game)

67, 66 and 31 enemy landed on castle 0 (31) in 7 to 8 turns. Castle 2 held 100 and castle 6 48, both in time. We sent 2->0 39 but also 2->5 61 and 6->5 28 at castle 5 (41) and 6->4 19; castle 0 fell on turn 92 (34 against 20 left), and castle 5 held.

**The right move:** 2->0 100 and 6->0 48 (about 207 against 164).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: 2->0 39, 2->0 61, 6->0 48.

## 2. Turn 47: a neutral capture beside the enemy

Neutral castle 4 (25, production 1) stood 2 from the enemy's castle 5 (6, with 66 landing in 2 to 4). We sent 0->4 29, took it with 4 on turn 49, fed 21 more and lost it on turn 53.

**The right move:** No capture: keep castle 0's 48 and feed it.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: smash>4 with 27 (smash prices a neutral target at its soldiers plus 2; the enemy castles that can retake it count only for an enemy-owned target).

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: the same smash>4 27.

## 3. Turn 60: a strike at castle 5

Castle 5 (enemy, 18, production 2) was fed by castles 1 and 3. We sent 6->5 12 and 2->5 20; castle 5 held with 29.

**The right move:** No strike: 2->4 20.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: 2->4 20.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 47: `["up 6 generation_amount", "send 2->0 x10"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 60: `["send 2->4 x20", "send 6->4 x13"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 85: `["send 2->0 x100", "send 6->0 x2", "send 6->0 x46"]` (avoids the mistake)
