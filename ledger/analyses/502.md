# Game 502: the three main mistakes

Map star-of-david-duo; our bot landgrab played as team 2 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost on turn 101.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 49: a capture in the middle of the map

Castle 12 (neutral, 40, production 2) sits at the centre, as near the enemy's castles 7 and 8 (production 4 and 2) as ours. We sent 10->12, 9->12 and 11->12, 50 in all, 6 to 12 turns at speed 1 and 0.5. It took until turn 60 to capture it (with 10), and it changed hands eight times up to turn 75, with both sides feeding it every turn; meanwhile the enemy took the cheap neutrals 0 and 4.

**The right move:** No capture of castle 12: buy production and take what is on our side.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: take>12 with 10->12 26 and 9->12 16. Its neutralOption counts what the enemy castles could spare now, not what they produce during a 6 to 12 turn walk.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: the same capture.

## 2. Turn 68: feeding a lost fight

Castle 12 was the enemy's (7) with 76 enemy soldiers landing in 2 to 9 turns against our 50. We sent 10->12 8 and 11->12 8 more, leaving castles 10 and 11 with 0; on turn 68 the enemy sent 38 at castle 11, which fell on turn 73.

**The right move:** Feed one stack: 2->9 22, 10->9 8, 11->9 8.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: exactly those three feeds.

## 3. Turn 76: again at castle 12

Castle 12 (enemy, 3) had 22 enemy landing in 1 and was fed every turn after. We sent 9->12 10 and 10->12 32, then 24 more on turns 78 and 81; it stayed the enemy's. Castle 11 (taken by the enemy on turn 73) grew to 117 beside our castles 10 and 5, which fell on turns 90 and 92.

**The right move:** Gather: 2->5 54, 9->5 18, 10->5 32, with speed at castle 5.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: exactly that.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 49: `["send 10->9 x26"]`
- turn 68: `["send 2->10 x22", "send 11->10 x8"]`
- turn 76: `["up 10 speed", "send 2->10 x54", "send 5->10 x12", "send 9->10 x18"]`
