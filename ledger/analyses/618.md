# Game 618: the three main mistakes

Map archipelago; the league bot fullfeed_unfreeze played as team 1 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 73.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 47: a strike at castle 11 that never took it

Castle 6 (40) sent 28 at enemy castle 11 (6, 5 more coming), then 11 and 15 more on turns 49 and 52. Castle 11 was fed and never fell; meanwhile 20 from castle 7 took castle 10 on turn 48 and 29 took castle 4 on turn 52.

**The right move:** No strike at castle 11: keep castle 6's soldiers as the stack, or take neutral castle 8 (30), which no enemy group was heading for.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: stacks on castle 6 (0->6 8, 2->6 12, 4->6 16) and smashes neutral castle 8 with 32.

## 2. Turn 53: a retake of castle 4 that could not hold

Castle 4 was the enemy's with 9. Enemy castle 10 (10), 1.6 away, had 13 + 31 + 12 landing in it. Castle 0 (27) sent 23, then 11 more; it took castle 4 on turn 55 with 2, castle 10 sent 49 at it, castle 0 sent 42 more, and castle 4 fell again on turn 61. About 76 soldiers for six turns of a castle.

**The right move:** No retake: join castles 0, 2 and 6 into one stack.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: smash>4 with 0->4 23 (the price only counts what castle 4 holds at the landing, not castle 10's 66 beside it), and stacks on castle 0.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: no strike; it stacks on castle 2 (0->2 27, 6->2 19), as its capture price counts what enemy castle 10 will hold by the landing.

## 3. Turn 64: three castles apart beside a stack of 110

Enemy castle 10 held 110 with 44 more landing. Ours were 42 (castle 0), 34 (castle 2) and 36 (castle 6), apart. Castle 10 sent 48 at castle 2 and took it on turn 65 (38 in it), then castle 0 on turn 69 and castle 6 on turn 72.

**The right move:** Join the three in one castle (112), which none of the enemy's strikes could take.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: stacks on castle 0, 2->0 34 and 6->0 36.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 47: `["send 10->0 x8", "send 0->6 x8", "send 2->6 x12", "send 4->6 x14"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 53: `["send 0->2 x27", "send 6->2 x19"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 64: `["send 0->2 x42", "send 6->2 x36"]` (avoids the mistake)
