# Game 596: the three main mistakes

Map gyre; burstguard played as team 1 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost on turn 81.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 68: an upgrade that emptied castle 6 beside the enemy's stack (decided the game)

Castle 6 held 78, the most we had; enemy castle 9 held 92 (55 more landing), about 4 away. We bought burst potential 1 to 4 at castle 6, which kept about 26. On turn 69 castle 9 sent 65 to castle 6 and 57 to castle 4; castle 6 fell on turn 76, castle 4 on turn 78 and castle 0 on turn 80. (Castle 4 had also bought burst on turns 60 and 65.)

**The right move:** No upgrade: keep the 78 (it holds the 65), or gather it with castle 0's 20 into castle 4.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: `send 0->4 x20`, `send 6->4 x78` (stack@4); it buys no burst.

## 2. Turn 63: a strike that emptied castles 0 and 2

Enemy castle 3 held 28; castle 9 held 33 with 56 landing, 1 to 3 turns from castle 2 (18). We sent 36 from castle 0 and 14 from castle 2 to castle 3, keeping 4 in each. The strike took nothing (castle 3 had 20 on turn 65), and castle 9 sent 18 to castle 2, which fell on turn 67.

**The right move:** No strike: gather castles 0, 4 and 6 into castle 2.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: feeds castle 2 (`0->2 x36`, `4->2 x15`, `6->2 x58`).

## 3. Turn 43: castle 9 helped by half

Castle 9 (21, production 2), taken on turn 40, had 29, 20 and 1 landing from castles 5, 3 and 1 within 6 turns. We sent castle 2's 18 and kept castle 0's 39 (3 and 20 followed on turns 45 and 48). Castle 9 held that wave with little left and fell on turn 52 to the next (24, 8, 4, 4); the enemy fed it to 92 and broke us from there.

**The right move:** Help and feed together: castle 2's 18 and castle 0's 39 into castle 9.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: `send 2->9 x18` (help>9 in 6) and `send 0->9 x39` (stack@9).

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 43: `["send 2->9 x18", "send 0->4 x39"]` (still makes it)
- turn 63: `["send 0->2 x36", "send 4->2 x15", "send 6->2 x58"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 68: `["send 0->4 x20", "send 6->4 x78"]` (avoids the mistake)
