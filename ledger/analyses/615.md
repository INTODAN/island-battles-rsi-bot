# Game 615: the three main mistakes

Map archipelago; the league bot was batchfront_keep, playing as team 2, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 90.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 44: a 38-soldier strike at a castle the enemy fed

Castle 11 (47) sent 38 at castle 2 (12, production 4), after castle 1's 12 on turn 42; castle 2 was fed 18 and 8 the next turn and held. All 50 were lost.

**The right move:** No strike: gather at castle 7 or castle 11 (1->7 9, 3->7 13, 11->7 47).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: exactly those three feeds to its stack at castle 7.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats an equivalent: its stack is castle 11 and it smashes neutral castle 8 (30) with 32, keeping about 2 there 2.1 from enemy castle 6 (22) and 2.5 from castle 10 (33). `smash` prices the hold against nearby enemy castles only for an enemy-owned target; a neutral target only checks that no enemy lands first.

## 2. Turn 57: another strike at castle 2 while the enemy gathered

Castle 7 (19) sent 18 at castle 2 (8) and castle 5 8, keeping 5; the enemy's 87 was landing on castle 3 and its castles 6, 4 and 10 held 92. The next turn they sent 109 at castle 7; the strike was lost and castle 7 fell on turn 63.

**The right move:** Keep castle 7's 19 and gather at castle 3 or castle 11.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats an equivalent: no strike at castle 2, but takes neutral castle 9 (30) with 7->9 19 and 11->9 13 (about 2 left), 2.1 from enemy castle 10 (33), emptying castle 7. `neutralOption` prices the hold with `enemySpare`, which pins castle 10 against our castle 3's 84, although 87 is landing on castle 3.

**juggernaut_fix:** Repeats the same take.

## 3. Turn 59: the army trickled into a castle that could not hold

The enemy's 55, 24 and 30 were landing on castle 7 (9) in 4 to 5. We sent 12, 16 and 8 on turn 59 and 16 and 12 more by turn 62; castle 7 fell on turn 63 with 47 in it, leaving every castle at 4 to 17; castles 3, 5, 1 and 11 fell between turns 76 and 89.

**The right move:** Give castle 7 up and gather the 48 at castle 3, 1.8 away, so the enemy's capture faces a stocked castle.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: `defend` sends 3->7 17, 1->7 13, 11->7 9, 5->7 9. It sends every helper in time even when their total is below what the castle lacks.

**juggernaut_fix:** Repeats: the same help.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 44: `["up 11 speed", "send 1->11 x9", "send 3->11 x13"]`
- turn 57: `["send 5->11 x9", "send 7->11 x19"]`
- turn 59: `["send 3->7 x17", "send 1->7 x13", "send 11->7 x9", "send 5->7 x9"]`
