# Game 570: the three main mistakes

Map bight; the hosted bot was juggernaut, playing as team 1, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost (its last castle fell on turn 63, the game ended on turn 75).

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 56: the stack emptied into castle 2 beside an enemy castle of 60 (decided the game)

Castle 4, the stack (16, speed 4), sent 16 to castle 2 (4), which an enemy group of 19 was about to hit, and kept 0, with enemy castle 3 (60) 5 away. The enemy sent 55 from castle 3 at castle 4 the same turn: castles 2 and 4 fell on turn 61 (castle 4 with 28) and castle 0, our last, on turn 63.

**The right move:** Send nothing from castle 4: let castle 2 go and keep the stack against castle 3.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: feed>2 0:8 4:16. `stackOf` names castle 2 (nearest the enemy, although the 19 bound for it take it) and `feedStack` empties the old stack into it.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: sends nothing (castle 3 lands on castle 4 before help could, so castle 4 keeps its stock).

## 2. Turn 46: an 8-turn strike at castle 2

The stack (23) sent 18 at enemy castle 2 (8, production 1), 8 turns away, keeping 5, with enemy castle 3 beside both. It took castle 2 on turn 53 with 2; we fed it about 60 (14, 8, 16, 8, 8 ...) against the enemy's 19, 20, 8 and 6, and it fell on turn 61, taking the stack's soldiers with it.

**The right move:** No strike: keep the stack in castle 4 and buy nothing until it holds castle 3's stock.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: smash>2 with 18 and 0->4 8. `smash` allows walks up to `smashWalk` (12) and counts only enemy castles that land before the strike.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: its retake price counts enemy castles only within `guardWalk` (4 turns); castle 3 is farther from castle 2 at its speed.

## 3. Turn 53: speed 4 bought from the stack beside an enemy castle of 55

Castle 4 (32, speed 3) bought speed 4 for 18, leaving 14, while enemy castle 3 (55, with 26 more landing on it) stood 5 away. Three turns later the stack had 16 and was emptied by mistake 1.

**The right move:** Buy nothing: keep 32 in castle 4.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: speed4@4. `build` keeps only the stack's `guard`, which counts enemy castles within `guardWalk` (4 turns); castle 3 is 6 turns away at speed 2.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: speed4@4, for the same reason.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save).
