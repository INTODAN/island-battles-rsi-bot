# Game 600: the three main mistakes

Map gyre; the hosted bot was pounce_reach, playing as team 2 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 92.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 64: castle 8 left to fall while castles 1 and 7 sat idle

Castle 8 (31, production 3) had 46 from the enemy's castle 4 landing in 3; the opponent added 19, 15 and 26 on turns 65 to 70. We sent only 3->8 7 (castle 3 buying speed); castle 1 (36, then 52) and castle 7 (22, then 38) sent nothing to castle 8, which fell on turn 73. Castle 3 fell on turn 76, and from 5 castles we were down to 3.

**The right move:** Help and feed castle 8: 3->8 7, and 1->8 36 and 7->8 22, which land before the later waves.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: help>8 (speed 2 at castle 3, 3->8 7) and feeds castle 8 as its stack, 1->8 36 and 7->8 22; on turn 68 again 1->8 52.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats it in part: the same help, but feeds only 1->8 14 (and 1->8 35 on turn 68); castle 1 keeps 22 and castle 7 all its 22, as reserves against the enemy's castle 4, which reaches them before the slow stack's help could. By the same landings castle 8 is still about 5 short when castle 4's 26 lands.

## 2. Turn 78: a strike at castle 3 that emptied castle 5

The enemy had just taken castle 3 (3) and had 32 landing on it in 6 to 7 turns; it was also sending 66 and 21 at castle 7. We sent 1->7 19 (help) but also 1->3 19 and 5->3 20 at castle 3; they met castle 3 grown and fed to 37 and were lost. Castle 5 was left with 13; on turn 85 the opponent struck it with 46 and took it on turn 88.

**The right move:** Help castle 7 (1->7 19) and keep castle 5's 20 home, feeding it from castle 1 (1->5 19).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: exactly 1->7 19 and 1->5 19.

## 3. Turn 83: castles 1 and 5 emptied at castle 4

Castle 4 (the enemy's, 12, production 4) was 5 turns from castle 1. We sent 1->4 12 and 5->4 6, leaving castle 1 with 4 and castle 5 with 2; castle 4 grew past them. On turn 85 the opponent sent 46 at castle 5 and 33 at castle 7; castle 7 fell on turn 87, castle 5 on turn 88 and castle 1 on turn 91.

**The right move:** No strike: feed castle 5, the castle facing castle 4 (1->5 12).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: exactly 1->5 12.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 64: `["up 3 speed", "send 3->8 x7", "send 1->8 x14", "send 3->8 x13"]` (still makes it)
- turn 78: `["send 1->7 x36"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 83: `["send 1->5 x12"]` (avoids the mistake)
