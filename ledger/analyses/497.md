# Game 497: the three main mistakes

Map claw; the hosted bot was speedhelp_fullfeed, playing as team 2, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 102.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 86: castle 6 left to fall to the enemy stack (decided the game)

The enemy had fed castle 4 up to 155 (production 4, speed 2, then 3); our soldiers sat apart in castle 6 (61), castle 1 (52) and castle 7 (52). On turn 86 the enemy sent 78 from castle 4 to castle 6 (3 turns). We sent nothing; castle 6 fell on turn 88 and the enemy fed 110 more into it, then took castle 7 on turn 98 and castle 1 on turn 101.

**The right move:** Send castle 1's 52 and castle 7's 52 to castle 6 (both land in 3, with the strike); better, gather them there on turns 84 to 85.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: on turn 84 and again on turn 86 it feeds its stack at castle 6 with everything (1->6 52, 7->6 52).

## 2. Turn 91: four small sends after castle 6 fell

Castle 1 and castle 7 held 72 each; the enemy was landing 110 on castle 6 and had 55 in castle 4. We sent 7->2 21 (into castle 2, which we never took back), 7->1 19 and 1->6 13 (into the 110 landing on castle 6, lost). Castle 7 was left with 36 and fell on turn 98 to 63 + 36 + 46.

**The right move:** Gather: castle 7's 72 into castle 1 (or castle 1's into castle 7), and no send into castle 6 or castle 2.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: 7->1 72 and speed at castle 1 (the fix sends 7->1 72 without the speed).

## 3. Turn 17: a capture of castle 4 that could not hold

Castle 4 was neutral (10, production 1), 3.2 from enemy castle 3. We sent 1->4 18 (12 turns at speed 1) and 7->4 4; they took it on turn 28 with 12. On turn 24 the enemy sent 25 + 4 and took it on turn 35 with 10 left; castle 4 then became the enemy's stack, the castle every later strike came from.

**The right move:** Take castle 2 with castles 6 and 7 (as juggernaut does) and keep castle 1's soldiers home for production, leaving castle 4 until a capture can hold it.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: take>4 1:12, the same slow capture from castle 1 (beside take>2 6:9 7:4).

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats (take>4 1:12). In `neutralOption` the race check compares the enemy's nearest castle with our nearest (castle 6, 7 turns), not with the castle that pays (castle 1, 12 turns), and the hold counts what enemy castles can spare now, not what they will have gathered when the slow group lands.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 17: `[]` (still makes it)
- turn 86: `["send 1->6 x52", "send 7->6 x52"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 91: `["send 7->1 x72"]` (avoids the mistake)
