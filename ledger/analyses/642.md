# Game 642: the three main mistakes

Map gyre; the league bot batchfront_keep played team 2 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 60.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 40: the best castle emptied into a neutral (decided the game)

Castle 5 (48, production 4) sent 40 at neutral castle 8 (25) and kept 12. Enemy castle 2 (34, with 28 landing) is 3.6 away; it bought speed and sent 41 on turn 41, which took castle 5 on turn 44. Our 40 took castle 8 on turn 43 with 15, and enemy castle 4's 20 retook it on turn 44. We went from four castles to three, then to none by turn 60.

**The right move:** Keep castle 5's 48 home (it grows 4 a turn) and leave castle 8 neutral.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats in effect: `send 1->3 x9`, `send 5->3 x48`. `stackOf` ties castles 3 and 5 at the same walk from an enemy and takes the lower id; `feedStack` empties castle 5 into castle 3 (production 1), as no enemy group is bound for castle 5 yet.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids losing castle 5: stack at castle 5, `send 5->8 x27`, `send 1->5 x9`, keeping about 30 there. It still takes castle 8 with 2: its price of holding a capture counts only for an enemy target, not a neutral one, so castle 4's 23 is not counted.

## 2. Turn 47: castle 1 thinned by a strike

Castle 1 (21, production 4) sent 12 at enemy castle 8 (6, with 24 enemy soldiers landing). On turn 48 enemy castles 4 and 2 sent 48 at castle 1, which fell on turn 54 holding 37; with the 12 it would have held. The 12 took nothing.

**The right move:** Keep castle 1's 21 home.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats in effect: `up 1 speed`, `send 1->3 x1`, `send 7->3 x9`. `defend` sees castle 3 falling and, as no help lands in time, lets a helper buy its next speed level when it "can pay and still give": castle 1 pays 20 of its 21 to send 1, and is left empty.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: `up 1 speed`, `send 1->3 x1`; the fixes don't touch `defend`.

## 3. Turn 28: a production-1 neutral taken with 7

Castle 1 sent 12 and castle 5 sent 7 at neutral castle 3 (12, production 1); they took it on turn 32 with 7. Enemy castle 4 (12, production 3) sent 15 and took it on turn 36; it changed hands twice more and was lost for good on turn 49, about 50 of our soldiers spent on it.

**The right move:** Leave castle 3 alone, or take it with enough to hold against castle 4.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: `send 5->3 x14` (smash, need 14) and feeds castle 1's 13 to castle 5. `smash` prices a neutral at its soldiers plus `strikeExtra` 2.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats with the same commands; its hold price is added only when the target is an enemy castle.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 28: `["send 1->3 x13", "send 5->3 x21"]`
- turn 40: `["send 1->5 x9"]`
- turn 47: `["send 7->1 x9"]`
