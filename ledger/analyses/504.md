# Game 504: the three main mistakes

Map archipelago; the league bot was batchfront, playing as team 1, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 86.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 64: a retake of castle 6 while castle 2 was the next target (decided the game)

The enemy had just taken castle 6 and held 36 at castle 7, 31 at castle 11 and 24 at castle 5, all within reach of castle 2 (13). We already had 36 walking to castle 6 and sent 10->6 20 and 0->6 8 more. The same turn the enemy launched 88 at castle 2; our trickles (4 to 8 a turn) could not hold it and castle 2 fell on turn 70 with 47. Castle 6, retaken on turn 67, fell again on turn 72, and castle 10 on turn 78.

**The right move:** Feed castle 2 instead: 0->2 9 and 10->2 21.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: stack@2, feeds 0->2 9 and 10->2 21.

## 2. Turn 57: castle 6 emptied into a strike on castle 3

Castle 3 was the enemy's with 8, fed by castles 1, 7 and 11. We sent 6->3 18 and 4->3 8, then 10->3 16 on turn 58; castle 3 was never taken (42 soldiers lost), and on turn 58 the enemy sent 109 at castle 6, left with 5. Castle 6 fell on turn 63 despite 68 sent after it.

**The right move:** No strike: keep castle 6's 19 home and gather on it (4->6 9).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats the kind of move: take>8, sends 6->8 19 and 10->8 13 at neutral castle 8 (30), emptying castle 6 for a capture of about 2 that enemy castle 11 (33, 3 turns away) takes back.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: the same take>8 (6->8 19, 10->8 13). `neutralOption` holds only against each enemy castle's spare (`enemySpare`), and castle 11's spare is counted as nothing because it keeps a reserve against our castles.

## 3. Turn 44: a second wave at castle 3 behind a first one

Castle 0's 12 was already walking to castle 3 (12, production 4); we sent 10->3 38 behind it. The enemy fed castle 3 (11->3 18, 1->3 8 on turn 45) and castle 3 held: 50 soldiers lost, castle 10 left with 13.

**The right move:** Gather instead: feed the stack (0->6 9, 2->6 13, 10->6 47).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: stack@6, feeds 0->6 9, 2->6 13 and 10->6 47.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Makes an equivalent mistake: its stack moves to castle 10 and smashes neutral castle 8 (30) with 32 (10->8 32), beside enemy castle 11 (33, 3 turns away). In `smash`, the new hold pricing applies only to enemy-owned targets (`if owner != neutral`); a neutral target needs only its soldiers plus `strikeExtra`.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 44: `["up 10 speed", "send 0->10 x9", "send 2->10 x13"]`
- turn 57: `["send 4->10 x9", "send 6->10 x19"]`
- turn 64: `["send 0->2 x9", "send 10->2 x21"]`
