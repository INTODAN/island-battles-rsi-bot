# Game 601: the three main mistakes

Map claw; our bot was batchfront_keep, playing as team 1, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 93.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 72: castle 3 left thin beside the enemy's stack

Enemy castle 4 held 80 with 49 landing on it; castle 3 (17) is 3.6 from it. Every castle of ours was feeding the middle castle 2 (37, with 45 landing). Castle 4 sent 43 at castle 3 that turn; we added only 8 on turn 73, and castle 3 fell on turn 75.

**The right move:** Keep castle 3's 17 and send it help from castle 2 (its 37 lands in 4), so castle 4's 43 cannot take it.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats, worse: 3->2 17, castle 3 left with 0 beside castle 4.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats with the same command. Its rear-castle reserve in `feedStack` applies only when an enemy castle lands strictly before the stack's help could; castle 4 and castle 2 are both 4 turns from castle 3, so castle 3 keeps nothing.

## 2. Turn 79: castle 2 cut in half to retake castle 3

Castle 2 held 115; enemy castles 6 and 7 held 48 each, 3.6 away (4 turns). Castle 3 was the enemy's (14) with 35 of ours landing. Castle 2 sent 59 at castle 3 and kept 60; on turn 80 castles 6 and 7 sent 86 at it, and castle 2 fell on turn 83 (72 to 10).

**The right move:** Keep castle 2's 115 home: castles 6 and 7 together can send 96.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats, worse: 2->0 115, making castle 0 the stack (2.8 from enemy castle 3, nearer than castle 2's 3.0 from castle 4) and leaving castle 2 with 0.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: 2->0 59, keeping 56. Its reserve keeps against the single strongest enemy castle that lands before the stack's help (`max` over the enemies), not castles 6 and 7 together.

## 3. Turn 85: a strike that emptied our last two castles (decided the game)

We held castles 0 (41) and 5 (17); the enemy held the rest and had 121 walking to castle 3, beside castle 0. Castles 0 and 5 sent 40 and 16 at castle 4 (4), which the enemy fed with 30 the next turn; the strike failed, castle 0 (5 left) fell on turn 88 and castle 5 on turn 92.

**The right move:** No strike: gather castle 5 into castle 0 and hold.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: buys speed 3 at castle 0 and sends 5->0 17. juggernaut_fix sends 5->0 17.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 72: `["send 3->2 x17"]`
- turn 79: `["send 2->0 x59"]`
- turn 85: `["send 5->0 x17"]`
