# Game 505: the three main mistakes

Map star-of-david-duo; our bot speedhelp_fullfeed played as team 2 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 214.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 92: castle 10 left to fall

The enemy stack in castle 11 (245, fed by five castles) sent 97 at castle 10 (74), landing in 3. Castle 9 (72, speed 3) was next to it. We sent nothing; castle 10 fell on turn 94 with 82 in it, and the enemy had a second castle on our side.

**The right move:** Buy speed 4 at castle 9 and send 12 to castle 10 (74 + 12 growth + 12 holds against 97).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: help>10, buys speed at castle 9 and sends 9->10 12; then gathers castles 2, 5 and 9 into castle 4.

## 2. Turn 98: castle 9 left to fall

The same stack (267, speed 4) sent 126 at castle 9 (96), landing in 4. Castle 2 (88) could reach it in time with speed bought. We sent nothing; castle 9 fell on turn 101 with 108 in it. After it castle 2 (160) fell on turn 116 and castle 4 (174) on turn 117, each alone.

**The right move:** Buy speed 3 at castle 2 and send 15 (or more) to castle 9.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: help>9, buys speed at castle 2, sends 2->9 15, and gathers castles 4 and 5 into castle 2.

## 3. Turn 78: the army stacked in corner castle 5, where it never helped

From turn 68 every castle fed castle 5, a corner castle 10 units (10 turns at speed 1) from castles 2 and 4. It held 86 on turn 78, 210 on turn 92 and 339 on turn 112, and sent nothing while castles 10, 9, 2 and 4 fell; on turn 112 its 60 for castle 2 landed after it fell.

**The right move:** Gather in a central castle (9 or 10), 3 to 5 turns from every own castle, so its stock can land on any castle the enemy stack hits.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats in kind: stack@4, it gathers everything (2:8, 5:86, 9:16, 10:18) into castle 4, the other corner. `stackOf` takes the own castle nearest an enemy castle and breaks the tie (castles 4 and 9 are both 5.8 from an enemy) by the lowest id.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: stack@9, feeds 2->9 8 and 10->9 18 and keeps castle 5 home; it breaks the tie by the shortest total distance to the other own castles.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 78: `["send 2->9 x8", "send 10->9 x18"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 92: `["up 9 speed", "send 9->10 x42", "send 2->9 x64", "send 4->9 x74", "send 5->9 x210"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 98: `["up 2 speed", "send 2->9 x15", "send 2->9 x53", "send 4->9 x98", "send 5->9 x273"]` (avoids the mistake)
