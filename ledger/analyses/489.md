# Game 489: the three main mistakes

Map claw; our bot speedhelp_fullfeed played as team 1 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost on turn 223.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 102: saving apart beside the enemy stack (decided the game)

Castles 0, 3 and 5 held 90, 130 and 90, each 2 to 4 turns apart, while the enemy stacked 329 in castle 4 at speed 4, 2 turns from each. On turn 104 it sent 115 at castle 0 (102), which fell on turn 106; on turn 110 it sent 141 at castle 5 (126), which fell on turn 112. Castle 3 then sat alone until turn 222.

**The right move:** Gather in castle 3: 0->3 90 and 5->3 90 (310, more than the stack).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: exactly 0->3 90 and 5->3 90.

## 2. Turn 105: castle 0 left to fall

115 landed on castle 0 (102, production 4) in 2 turns; castle 3 (142) was 3 turns away at speed 2. We sent nothing and castle 0 fell on turn 106 with 106 in it.

**The right move:** Buy speed 3 at castle 3 so its help lands in 2, and send 3->0 6 (102 + 8 + 6 against 115).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: up 3 speed, 3->0 6, and 5->3 102 to gather.

## 3. Turn 76: a strike at castle 4 while castle 2 fell

Castle 2 (20, production 2) had 29 landing in 2 and fell on turn 77; castle 4 (enemy, 5) had 52 landing within 3. We sent 3->4 17, which died there, and on turn 77 fed 46 into castle 2 as it fell.

**The right move:** No strike and no feed to castle 2: gather in castle 3 (0->3 8, 5->3 8).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats an equivalent: its stack is castle 2, so it feeds it 0->2 8, 3->2 20, 5->2 8, which land after it falls (stackOf picks the castle nearest the enemy, even one its landings take).

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: the stack moves to castle 3 (0->3 8, 5->3 8).

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 76: `["send 2->3 x20", "send 0->3 x8", "send 5->3 x8"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 102: `["send 0->3 x90", "send 5->3 x90"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 105: `["up 3 speed", "send 3->0 x6", "send 5->3 x102"]` (avoids the mistake)
