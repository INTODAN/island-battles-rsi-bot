# Game 566: the three main mistakes

Map archipelago; the hosted bot was burstguard, playing as team 1, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 85.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 52: 84 thrown at castle 7

Castle 0 held 87 and castle 4 22; enemy castle 7 held 37 (production 4, speed 3), 6 turns from castle 0, with enemy castles 1, 3, 5 and 11 behind it. Our castles 8 (3) and 10 (9) had 19 and 21 landing in 2. We sent 0->7 62 and 4->7 22, keeping 4 in each; the enemy fed castle 7 26 more, it never fell, and castle 8 fell on turn 53.

**The right move:** Keep the 84 at home or move them into castle 10, the centre, and help castles 8 and 10.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: buys speed 3 at castle 0, helps 0->10 5 and feeds 116 to its stack at castle 8; no strike.

## 2. Turn 68: far strikes while castle 6 was attacked

Castle 6 held 63 with 66 landing in 1; enemy castle 8 (57) stood beside it. We sent 0->3 27 (5 turns away), 4->7 12, 10->7 19 and 0->7 1; none took anything, and castle 6 fell on turn 72 to the next groups from castle 8.

**The right move:** Send castles 0, 4 and 10's soldiers into castle 6 instead.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: feeds castle 6 with 0->6 28, 2->6 12, 4->6 30 and 10->6 19.

## 3. Turn 75: castle 2 emptied beside enemy castle 6 (decided the game)

Castle 0 (28) had 62 landing in 2; castle 2 (40) was 2 turns from it and from enemy castle 6; castle 4 held 46. We sent 2->0 27 and also 2->10 13 and 4->10 25 at enemy castle 10, keeping 4 in castle 2; castle 6 took castle 2 on turn 78, castle 0 fell on turn 82 and castle 4 on turn 84.

**The right move:** Help castle 0 with 27 and keep the rest of castle 2; move castle 4's 46 into castle 2.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: sends help 2->0 27 and feeds 4->2 46.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 52: `["up 0 speed", "send 0->10 x5", "send 0->10 x62", "send 2->10 x32", "send 4->10 x22", "send 6->10 x21"]`
- turn 68: `["send 0->10 x28", "send 2->10 x12", "send 4->10 x30"]`
- turn 75: `["send 2->0 x40", "send 4->2 x44"]`
