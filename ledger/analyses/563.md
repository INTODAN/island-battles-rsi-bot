# Game 563: the three main mistakes

Map star-of-david-duo; the league bot burstguard played as team 2 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 98.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake. All three are one fault of the expansion rule.

## 1. Turn 28: neutral castle 0 paid exactly, 23 turns away

Castle 11 sent 6 and castle 10 sent 16 at neutral castle 0 (20, at the far end of the map), at speed 1. The 6 landed on turn 40 (20 to 14); the 16 landed on turn 51 and took it with 2. Enemy castle 7 had sent 12 on turn 29, the turn after it saw ours, and took castle 0 on turn 52: 22 soldiers and 23 turns of two castles' production for the enemy.

**The right move:** Leave castle 0 (nothing can hold it at that distance), keep the 22 home and buy production.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: take>0 with 11->0 6 and 10->0 16. Its `neutralOption` (main.go) prices the capture at the neutral's soldiers + `neutralExtra` + what the enemy castles can spare now, less the neutral's growth; it does not count what they produce during a 23-turn walk, nor that the two groups land 11 turns apart.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: the same two sends; the fix does not change `neutralOption`.

## 2. Turn 35: neutral castle 4 paid by two groups 8 turns apart

Castle 9 sent 16 (12 turns' walk) and castle 2 sent 6 (20 turns) at neutral castle 4 (20). The 16 left it with 4; enemy castle 8 (5.8 away, closer than castle 2) sent 12 and took it on turn 47, before our 6 arrived.

**The right move:** No take, or one that lands at once with a margin over what castle 8 can send: here none was affordable, so keep the 22.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: take>4 with 9->4 16 and 2->4 6, by the same rule (two payers, exact price, landings not lined up).

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: the same sends.

## 3. Turn 49: neutral castle 12 (40) paid with 42 from two castles

Castle 10 sent 36 (6 turns) and castle 9 sent 6 (12 turns) at the central neutral castle 12 (40), 3 to 6 from three enemy castles. The 36 left it with 4 and the enemy took it on turn 55; we sent 18 more, took it back twice and lost it twice by turn 62.

**The right move:** No take of the center with an exact price: keep castle 10's 36 as the stack and feed it.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: take>12 with 10->12 36 and 9->12 6.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: the same sends. A fix would make `neutralOption` price the capture at its last payer's landing (the enemy's production and sends until then, as the fix already does for strikes) and count a group only if it lands with the others.
