# Game 631: the three main mistakes

Map delta; our bot was fullfeed_unfreeze, playing as team 1, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 96.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 67: a trickle of 9s at castle 4

Enemy castle 4 held 59 and was fed from castles 1, 3 and 7 (100 more walking to it). Castle 6 (85) sent 9 at it, and 17 to castle 2 (6 turns away, too late to save it from a 52 landing). It sent 9 more on turns 69, 71, 73, 75 and 77: 54 soldiers into a castle that grew to 142, for nothing.

**The right move:** Keep castle 6's soldiers and buy it speed; send castle 2 its help from castle 0 if at all.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: 0->2 17 as help and speed 3 at castle 6, no group at castle 4. juggernaut_fix sends 0->2 17.

## 2. Turn 75: castle 0 kept apart from the stack

Castle 6 held 118 beside enemy castle 4 (115, speed 3, 2.1 away); castle 0 held 52, 5.0 from castle 6. Castle 0 sent nothing then, and nothing for the next 12 turns.

**The right move:** Feed castle 0's 52 into castle 6.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: 0->6 52. juggernaut_fix does the same.

## 3. Turn 87: still apart, the turn before the strike (decided the game)

Castle 6 held 148 (speed 3) and castle 4 held 276 with 117 more landing; castle 0 held 100. On turn 88 castle 4 sent 167 at castle 6 (156 by then): castle 6 fell on turn 89 with 7 enemy soldiers left, 11 short. Castle 0, alone, fell on turn 95.

**The right move:** Send castle 0's 100 to castle 6: it lands after the 167 but retakes castle 6 from the 7 left, and the two castles stay one.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: 0->6 100. juggernaut_fix does the same.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 67: `["send 0->2 x17"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 75: `["send 0->6 x52"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 87: `["send 0->6 x100"]` (avoids the mistake)
