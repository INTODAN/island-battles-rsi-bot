# Game 477: the three main mistakes

Map claw; the hosted bot was speedhelp_landlate, playing as team 2; lost, eliminated on turn 80.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 67: an all-in strike that emptied two castles (decided the game)

Home castle 1 had 8 with enemy groups of 3, 10 and 14 landing in 3 to 6 turns; castle 2 had 22 with enemy castles 3 and 5 (12 each) 3.6 away. We sent 2->4 22, 1->4 8 and 6->4 7 at castle 4 (11), emptying castles 2 and 1; the enemy took castle 2 with 13 + 6 on turn 71 and home on turn 72.

**The right move:** Send nothing: castle 1 holds with its own growth, and castle 2's 22 holds against 19.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: sends nothing.

## 2. Turn 69: home sent its garrison away

Castle 1 had 8 with 3, 10 and 14 landing in 2 to 4 turns; we sent 1->7 8 at castle 7 (3). We took castle 7, lost it on turn 75, and home fell. On turn 70 we sent 6->4 7 where 6->1 8 was needed.

**The right move:** Keep the 8 home; with castle 6's 4 landing in 2 it holds. On turn 70 send 6->1 8.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: sends nothing on 69, and 6->1 8 on 70.

## 3. Turn 45: castle 4 spent everything

We bought speed and production at castle 4 and sent 4->2 10, leaving it nearly empty; castle 2 was retaken on turn 49 and castle 4, with 17 when 5 + 15 + 12 was sent at it, fell on turn 54 (20 against 27).

**The right move:** Help castles 6 and 7 (both attacked), buy production only at castle 4 and keep its 20.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: helps castles 6 and 7 and buys production only.
