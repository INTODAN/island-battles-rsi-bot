# Game 471: the three main mistakes

Map claw; the hosted bot was ori_hold, playing as team 1; lost, eliminated on turn 137.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 108: castle 3 left to fall (decided the game)

Castle 3 held 146 (production 4); enemy groups of 177 (from castle 4) and 45 (from castle 2) landed in 3 turns. Castle 0 had 92 at 2.8 (lands in 3), castle 5 had 100 at 4.0 (lands in 4). We sent nothing on turns 107 and 108; castle 3 fell on turn 110 with about 154 in it, and the enemy stacked 335 there.

**The right move:** Send castle 0's 92 to castle 3 (and castle 5's 100 behind it); better, move both into castle 3 on turns 106 to 107.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: feeds castle 3 on turns 106 to 107 (0->3 84, 5->3 92) and on turn 108 sends help 0->3 65 plus 0->3 27 and 5->3 100.

## 2. Turn 120: castle 0 left to fall

An enemy group of 159 landed on castle 0 (140) in 2 to 3 turns; castle 5 had 148 at 2.8. We sent nothing and castle 0 fell on turn 122 with 148 in it.

**The right move:** Send castle 5's 148 to castle 0.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: sends 5->0 148; on turn 121 it also buys speed at castle 5 so the help lands in 2.

## 3. Turn 72: a retake that could take castle 4 but not hold it

Castle 4 was the enemy's with 4 (4 more landing in 1); enemy castle 6 (15) was 3.2 away and castle 2 (19) beside it. We sent 0->4 24 and 3->4 3, took castle 4 on turn 77 with 3 and lost it on turn 78. The same happened about six times between turns 25 and 78.

**The right move:** No strike: keep the soldiers home (or in castle 3) and buy production.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeated: smash>4 with 14 from its stack (castle 3, emptied to 0), capturing with about 2 just before castle 6's 14 lands.

**Proposed fix (not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed):** A capture's price now also counts what every enemy castle within 4 turns of the target will hold by the landing (its stock, its incoming groups and its growth) less the capture's growth; on this board juggernaut keeps its 14.
