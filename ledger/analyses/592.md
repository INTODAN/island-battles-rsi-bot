# Game 592: the three main mistakes

Map gyre; our bot was juggernaut, playing as team 2, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; won on turn 90. It is game 589 move for move with the sides swapped (castles 0/1, 2/3, 4/5, 6/7 and 8/9 exchanged), and the mistakes are the same.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake. The champion played this game, and on each board it gives the commands it really sent.

## 1. Turn 30: the neutral centre castle taken with 4

Castle 3 sent 16 and castle 5 sent 15 at neutral castle 8 (25), 7 turns away. They took it on turn 37 with 4; the enemy's 30, sent on turn 32, took it on turn 38 with 23, and the enemy held it until turn 74. Cost: 31 soldiers and the centre for 36 turns.

**The right move:** Keep the 31 and buy production 3 at castle 3 (20).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: `send 3->8 x16`, `send 5->8 x15` (`take>8`). `neutralOption()` holds the capture only against what the enemy castles could spare now, not what they produce during the walk.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats, by the same rule in `neutralOption()`.

## 2. Turn 40: a strike on castle 8 with a margin of 2

The stack, castle 3 (28), struck enemy castle 8 (15, 4 turns away) with 25. Enemy castles 0 and 4 sent 12 and 4 on turn 41 (castle 4 after buying speed); they landed first and the 25 were lost.

**The right move:** No strike: keep the 28 in the stack.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: `send 3->8 x25` (`smash>8 in 4 need 25`). `smash()` counts a neighbour's stock only if it lands by then at its present speed.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: its hold term counts only enemy castles within `guardWalk` (4 turns) at their present speed.

## 3. Turn 55: a strike on castle 9 that couldn't take it

The stack, castle 3 (103, speed 4), struck enemy castle 9 (19, production 1, 3 turns away) with 24; the enemy's 4 from castle 4 kept it, and the 24 were lost (it fell only on turn 66).

**The right move:** No strike, or one with castles 2's and 4's 47 each counted.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: `send 3->9 x24`, `send 1->3 x8` (`smash>9 in 3 need 24`).

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: only `send 1->3 x8`; its hold term prices the capture against the enemy castles nearby.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 30: `[]`
- turn 40: `["up 5 generation_amount", "send 7->3 x8"]`
- turn 55: `["send 1->3 x8"]`
