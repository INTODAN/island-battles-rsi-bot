# Game 635: the three main mistakes

Map estuary; the league bot fullfeed_unfreeze played as team 1 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 141.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 27: the center taken with an exact price, beside the enemy

Castle 0 (31) sent 30 at neutral castle 4 (25, production 1), 4.5 from enemy castle 1 (22), which sent 17 the next turn. We took it on turn 29 with 5, and then fed it 10 to 12 every two or three turns against 11 to 33 from castles 1 and 3: over 300 soldiers by turn 72, five changes of hands, and our two castles stayed near 4 soldiers each the whole time.

**The right move:** Leave castle 4 to the enemy, keep castle 0's stock and buy production and speed in castles 0 and 2.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: smash>4 with 0->4 27. The smash prices the target at the landing and keeps the stack's guard, but castle 4 is never held against castle 1 next door.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: 0->4 27. Its capture price counts enemy castles within `guardWalk` (4) turns of the target; castle 1 is 5 turns away at speed 2, so it is not counted.

## 2. Turn 74: thin take of neutral castle 6

Castles 2, 4 and 0 sent 12, 12 and 6 (then 10 and 10) at neutral castle 6 (25), 4 from enemy castle 1. We took it on turn 80 with 3; castle 1's 27 and castle 3's 21 took it back on turn 85.

**The right move:** Feed castle 4, the castle the enemy was attacking.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: stacks on castle 4, 0->4 8 and 2->4 12.

## 3. Turn 90: castle 4 emptied for a production-1 castle

Castle 5 (9, production 1) had 37 enemy soldiers landing in 4. Castle 4 (36, production 4) sent 31 to it and castles 0 and 2 sent 13 and 12, leaving castle 4 with 9 at 4.5 from enemy castle 1 (30). Castle 1 sent 39 and took castle 4 on turn 96; castle 5 fell on turn 98. The enemy stacked castle 4 up to 593 and took castles 0 and 2 (168 and 144) on turns 139 and 140.

**The right move:** Keep castle 4 as the stack (feed it from castles 0 and 2) and let castle 5 go, or help it with no more than what holds it.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: help 4->5 21, then makes castle 5 the stack, 4->5 15, 0->5 13, 2->5 12; castle 4 is left with 0. `defend()` sends help to any castle that would fall, whatever its production, and `stackOf` then picks castle 5.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: 4->5 21 and 4->5 15, keeping castles 0 and 2's 25 home; its reserve rule doesn't hold castle 4's soldiers back because it judges castle 5's help would land first.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 27: `[]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 74: `["send 0->4 x8", "send 2->4 x12"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 90: `["send 5->4 x9", "send 0->4 x13", "send 2->4 x12"]` (not judged)
