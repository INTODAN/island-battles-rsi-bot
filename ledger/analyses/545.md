# Game 545: the three main mistakes

Map estuary; our bot pounce played team 1 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 115.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 103: a strike at castle 4 while castle 6 was under attack

105 from the enemy's castle 5 were landing on castle 6 (90) in 3. We sent 0->4 61 and 2->4 92 at enemy castle 4 (68), which four enemy castles stood beside; they sent 105 into it on turn 103 and castle 4 held. Castle 6 was left with 4 after the landing and fell on turn 109; castles 0 and 2, emptied, fell on turns 113 and 114.

**The right move:** No strike: help castle 6 (0->6 7) and gather castle 2's 163 into castle 0.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: up speed at castle 0, help 0->6 7, and 2->0 163.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: the same commands.

## 2. Turn 84: castle 4 left with 5 beside the enemy's 98

Our castle 4 (5, production 2, speed 1) was 2 from enemy castle 5 (98, with 22 more landing); castles 2 and 0 had 87 and 22. We sent nothing; castle 5 sent 17 on turn 85 and castle 4 fell on turn 86. The enemy kept it, and from turn 103 it held off our strike.

**The right move:** Feed castle 4: 0->4 22 (lands in 2) and 2->4 87.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: its stack is castle 4: 0->4 22, 2->4 87, and production at castle 6.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: its stack is castle 4 but it sends nothing (147 kept home), because the new rear reserve keeps what castle 5's 98 could land on castles 0 and 2 before the stack's help.

## 3. Turn 104: a second wave into castle 4

With the first 153 still walking and 105 enemy reinforcements already sent into castle 4, we sent 2->4 51 more, leaving castle 2 with 28; it was lost with the first wave.

**The right move:** No second wave: 2->0 75 (or keep it home).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: 2->0 75.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: 2->0 75.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 84: `["send 0->4 x22", "send 2->4 x87", "send 6->4 x33"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 103: `["up 0 speed", "send 0->6 x7", "send 2->0 x163"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 104: `["send 2->0 x75"]` (avoids the mistake)
