# Game 579: the three main mistakes

Map delta; our bot was juggernaut, playing as team 2, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 91. It is game 578 move for move with the sides swapped (castles 0/1, 2/3, 4/5 and 6/7 exchanged), and the mistakes are the same.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake. The champion played this game, and on each board it gives the commands it really sent.

## 1. Turn 62: a strike on castle 6 with a margin of 2

The stack, castle 3 (22, speed 4), struck enemy castle 6 (4, production 4) four turns away: 20 there at the landing plus 2. Enemy castle 4 bought speed on turn 63 and sent 4, which landed in time; castle 6 held and our 22 were lost. Castle 5 fell on turn 64 and castle 3 on turn 75.

**The right move:** No strike: keep the 22 in castle 3.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: `send 3->6 x22` (`smash>6 in 4 need 22`). `smash()` prices a neighbour's reinforcement only at its present speed (castle 4 at speed 1: 5 turns).

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: its hold term counts enemy castles within `guardWalk` (4 turns) at their present speed, and castle 4 is 5.

## 2. Turn 80: castle 1 fed the stack beside a stocked enemy castle

Castle 1 (8) sent its 8 to the stack in castle 7, keeping 4, while enemy castle 5 held 64 with 89 more landing, 7 turns away, and enemy castle 3 (speed 4) 3 turns away. On turn 84 castle 5 sent 79 at castle 1.

**The right move:** Keep the 8 home while castle 5 is stocked.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: `send 1->7 x8` (`feed>7`), from `feedStack()`.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: no commands; its rear castle keeps a reserve against enemy castle 3, which lands before the stack's help.

## 3. Turn 85: a defence sized for the first landing only

Castle 1 (4) had 79 landing in 6 turns, and castle 5 still held 70. The stack, castle 7 (75), sent exactly the 52 castle 1 lacked and kept 27; the enemy's follow-ups (19 and 4 at castle 1, 47 at castle 7) took castle 7 on turn 89 and castle 1 on turn 90.

**The right move:** Send all 75 to castle 1, or keep them all in castle 7; either way one castle holds.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: `send 7->1 x52` (`help>1 in 6`): `defend()` sends what the first losing landing lacks plus 1, with no margin for the attacker's next groups.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats, by the same rule in `defend()`.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save).
