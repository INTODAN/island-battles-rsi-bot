# Game 612: the three main mistakes

Map star-of-david-duo; the league bot fullfeed_unfreeze played team 2 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost on turn 236 with 1 castle against 12.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 100: the centre stack moved away from the enemy (decided the game)

We led, 7 castles to 6. Castle 12, the centre (79, with 103 of our soldiers landing over the next eight turns), sent 35 to castle 5 at the back, and 42 more on turn 101. Enemy castles 7 (68) and 8 (57) are 5.8 away at speed 3. On turn 102 they sent 80 and 62, which took castle 12 on turn 105 (123 against 142). Our groups still walking to castle 12 landed on an enemy castle. From then on we lost castles one by one.

**The right move:** Keep castle 12's 79 home (it would have held about 200 against the 142).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats in effect: `send 12->4 x79` with castles 2, 5, 9 and 10 (stack at castle 4). `stackOf` ties castles 4 and 12 at the same walk from an enemy and takes the lower id, and `feedStack` empties castle 12 into it, as no enemy group is bound for castle 12 yet.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: makes castle 9 the stack and feeds it only from castles 2 and 10; castle 12 keeps its 79 as the reserve against castles 7 and 8, which land before the stack's help.

## 2. Turn 103: two captures with 2 that could not hold

Castle 4 (48) sent 25 at enemy castle 8 (7) and castle 10 (35) sent 35 at enemy castle 11 (17). Both were taken on turn 106 with 2, and both were retaken on turn 110 (by 22 from castle 7 and 22 from castle 6). Castle 10 kept 4 and castle 4 kept 23.

**The right move:** No strikes: keep both castles' soldiers home while the enemy's 142 lands on castle 12.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: no strike; it sends castle 10's 5 to castle 12 and feeds its stack at castle 4.

## 3. Turn 109: help sent to a castle that falls next turn

Castle 8 (10) had 22 landing in 2. Castles 5, 2 and 9 sent 20, 24 and 24 to it (landing in 6 to 14 turns), and castle 10 sent 23 at castle 12. Castle 8 fell on turn 110. Castle 4 (27) had 88 from castle 7 landing in 7; it got nothing and fell on turn 115 (51 against 88).

**The right move:** Send castle 9's 24 and castle 2's soldiers to castle 4, and keep the rest home.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: `send 9->4 x24` and castle 2's 4 (after buying speed), and feeds 44 to castle 8 as its stack.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 100: `["send 2->9 x18", "send 4->9 x36", "send 5->9 x12", "send 10->9 x23"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 103: `["send 12->7 x40", "send 2->9 x30", "send 4->9 x48", "send 5->9 x24", "send 10->9 x35"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 109: `["send 8->4 x10", "send 11->5 x10", "send 2->10 x24", "send 5->10 x20", "send 9->10 x24"]` (still makes it)
