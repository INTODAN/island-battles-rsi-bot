# Game 594: the three main mistakes

Map estuary; the hosted bot was burstguard, playing as team 2, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 108.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 89: the last strike at castle 4 emptied castles 1 and 3 (decided the game)

We held castles 1 (28), 3 (28) and 7 (43); the enemy held castle 4 with 4 but castles 0, 2, 5 and 6 around it were feeding castle 5 and could feed castle 4. We sent 1->4 28, 3->4 28 and 7->4 14, keeping 4 in castles 1 and 3. Castle 4 held; on turn 90 enemy castle 5 sent 24 at castle 1 and took it on turn 92, then castle 3 fell on turn 101 and castle 7 on turn 107.

**The right move:** Keep castles 1 and 3 stocked; gather in castle 1 and buy speed there.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: buys speed 3 at castle 1 and feeds it 3->1 28 and 7->1 43.

## 2. Turn 73: 146 thrown at castle 4

Castle 4 held 72 (production 4), 6 turns from castle 3, with enemy castles 0, 2 and 6 (74 between them) 2 to 3 turns from it. We sent 3->4 93, 7->4 31 and 1->4 22, keeping 4 in each. The enemy fed castle 4 53 on turn 74; the strike only knocked it to 3, and castle 5 (1) fell on turn 74.

**The right move:** Keep the soldiers home, or gather them in one castle; no strike that must beat a castle fed by three neighbours.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids the strike: it feeds 146 into castle 5, which is about to fall (the stack choice that the proposed fix corrects: juggernaut_fix gathers in castle 1 instead).

## 3. Turn 79: 46 more at castle 4

Castle 4 held 3, 5 turns from castles 1 and 3; four enemy groups (76) were bound for castle 5 beside it and castle 0 had 4 more to send. We sent 1->4 24 and 3->4 22, keeping 4 and 6; castle 4 was refilled to 27 by turn 82 and the 46 were lost.

**The right move:** Keep the 48 home (or gather them in castle 1).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: buys speed 3 at castle 1 and feeds it 3->1 24 and 7->1 24.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 73: `["send 3->1 x93", "send 7->1 x31"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 79: `["send 3->1 x24", "send 7->1 x24"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 89: `["send 3->1 x28", "send 7->1 x43"]` (avoids the mistake)
