# Game 516: the three main mistakes

Map archipelago; speedhelp_fullfeed played as team 2 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost on turn 80.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 65: saving apart beside the enemy's stack (decided the game)

The enemy fed castle 11 (167, speed 3) every turn; it was 2 turns from castle 3 (48) and 3 from castle 7 (40). Castle 1 held 76. We sent nothing: on turn 65 castle 11 sent 61 to castle 3, which fell on turn 66 with 52 in it.

**The right move:** Gather into one castle: castle 1's 76 and castle 7's 40 into castle 3 (164, which holds the 61), or castles 3 and 7 into castle 1.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: stack@1, feeds it castle 3's 48 and castle 7's 40 (164 together); castle 3 is given up but its soldiers are not.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: stack@3, feeds it castle 1's 76 and castle 7's 40.

## 2. Turn 68: castle 7 kept a reserve it could not hold

Castle 7 held 52 (growing 4 a turn), 2 to 3 turns from castle 11 (158, speed 3); castle 1's help (88) needed 4 turns. That turn castle 11 sent 69 to castle 7, which fell on turn 70 with 60 in it.

**The right move:** Move castle 7's 52 into castle 1 (140 there) instead of losing it.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: `send 7->1 x52` (feed>1).

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: sends nothing. Its `feedStack` keeps in a rear castle what an enemy castle landing before the stack's help could take (here more than 150), so castle 7 keeps all 52, which hold against nothing that castle 11 can send. A reserve smaller than the threat should go to the stack instead.

## 3. Turn 47: a strike that could not take castle 10

Castle 7 (40) sent 28 to enemy castle 10 (10, production 4, about 4 turns away), with enemy castles 0 and 4 beside it; castle 0 fed it 5 on turn 49. The strike landed short, and castle 7 was left with 16 while castle 11 was being lost.

**The right move:** No strike at castle 10: keep castle 7's 40 (or take the neutral castle 9 with exactly what it needs).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: no strike at castle 10; it takes the neutral castle 9 (smash>9 in 3 need 32) and feeds castle 7 from castles 1, 3 and 5.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 47: `["send 11->1 x8", "send 1->7 x8", "send 3->7 x12", "send 5->7 x14"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 65: `["send 1->3 x76", "send 7->3 x40"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 68: `["send 7->1 x52"]` (avoids the mistake)
