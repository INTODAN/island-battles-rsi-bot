# Game 480: the three main mistakes

Map claw; the hosted bot was speedhelp_landlate, playing as team 1; lost, eliminated on turn 90.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 51: home attacked while castle 3 was under attack

72 enemy soldiers were landing on castle 3 (21) in 5 to 7 turns; castle 0 had 13 at 2.8 (3 turns). We sent 0->4 13 and on turn 54 another 16 at castle 4, all wasted; castle 3 fell on turn 57. (The production bought at home on turn 48 was not the cause: the attack was sent on turn 49 and help from turn 50 holds.)

**The right move:** Send 0->3 13 and castle 0's production into castle 3 every turn after.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: help 0->3 5 and feed 0->3 8, about 45 over turns 51 to 55, which holds castle 3.

## 2. Turn 78: castle 5 left alone

14 (in 3) and 35 (in 5) were coming at castle 5 (15). We sent nothing on turn 78 and 0->3 10 at enemy castle 3 on turn 79; castle 5 fell on turn 82, about 20 short, and castle 0 on turn 89.

**The right move:** Send 0->5 11 on turn 78 and 0->5 15 on turn 79.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: exactly those sends (help>5).

## 3. Turn 24: a slow strike into a reinforced castle

We sent 0->2 15, 5->2 5 and 3->2 6 at speed 1 (5 to 10 turns) at castle 2 (5). The enemy sent 15 there on turn 29, our groups lost clashes on turns 33 and 34, and castle 2 was ours only on turn 42; the 26 were missing while castle 4 was fought over.

**The right move:** Send nothing that turn.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: sends nothing.
