# Game 588: the three main mistakes

Map delta; the hosted bot was pounce_reach, playing as team 1, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 135.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 87: a strike that landed one group at a time (decided the game)

The enemy's castle 7 held 109 (speed 3). We sent 0->7 111 (13 turns), 2->7 91 (8 turns), 6->7 29 and 4->7 13 (speed 1, about 20 turns): 244 soldiers. The enemy fed castle 7 with 54 more and met the groups one by one; castle 7 was not taken by them. Castles 6 (left with 16 after 20 more went to castle 4) and 4 (1) fell on turns 95 and 96.

**The right move:** No strike from the far side of the map: gather castles 0 and 6 into castle 2 and buy its speed (and production where it is missing).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: 0->2 111, 6->2 23, speed 3 and 4 at castle 2, production at castles 4 and 6.

## 2. Turn 116: a strike on the enemy stack four soldiers short

Castle 6 was the enemy's with 114 (speed 3, 11 landing in 3). We sent 0->6 116 and 2->6 31 (5 turns); the enemy added 10 and castle 6 kept 4. Castle 0, left with 4, fell on turn 134 to 53.

**The right move:** No strike: keep castle 0's 116 and feed it with castle 2.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: 2->0 32, a stack of 148 in castle 0 (the fix sends nothing and keeps 184 home).

## 3. Turn 60: 128 soldiers for a production-1 castle

Castle 5 was the enemy's with 14 (production 1), ten turns from castle 0. We sent 0->5 103, 2->5 18 and 4->5 7. The enemy fed it with 99 (28, 33 and 38 on turn 62); we took it on turn 70 with 14 and lost it on turn 82 to 38.

**The right move:** No strike: move castle 0's 106 forward into castle 2 and buy production at castle 4.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: 0->2 106 and production at castle 4 (the fix feeds castle 4 with 77).

## The final proposed fixes on these boards
