# Game 573: the three main mistakes

Map bight; our bot was juggernaut, playing as team 2, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 76.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake. The champion played this game, and on each board it gives the commands it really sent.

## 1. Turn 54: castle 5 emptied beside a stocked enemy castle (decided the game)

Castle 5 (production 4, speed 4) had 19, with 8 + 8 from castle 1 landing; enemy castle 2 held 64, about 5 units (6 turns) away. The stack was castle 3 (2 soldiers, production 1), the castle nearest the enemy, and we fed it 19 from castle 5 and 8 from castle 1. The enemy sent 51 from castle 2 next turn; castle 5 fell on turn 60 with 40 in it. Kept home, it would have held about 59. Castles 1 and 3 fell on turn 65.

**The right move:** Send nothing: keep castle 5's 19 (and castle 1's 8) home against castle 2's 64.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: `send 1->3 x8`, `send 5->3 x19` (`feed>3`). `feedStack()` gives the stack whatever a rear castle's own landings don't need, ignoring enemy castles that could strike it.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: no commands, 29 kept home; its rear castles keep a reserve against an enemy castle that lands before the stack's help.

## 2. Turn 25: a 16-turn walk to a neutral castle that could not be held

Castle 2 (2 soldiers) could not be saved from the enemy's 27 (our help would land 4 turns late). We sent 19 from castle 1 and 10 from castle 5 at neutral castle 3 (15), 16 turns away; they took it on turn 40 with 14, and the enemy's 19 from castle 0 (production 3, sent on turn 34) took it on turn 41 with 4 left. Cost: 29 soldiers, and castle 3 handed to the enemy.

**The right move:** Keep the 29 and buy production 3 at castle 5 (20), growing at home.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: `send 5->3 x10`, `send 1->3 x19` (`take>3`).

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats. `neutralOption()` makes the capture hold only against what the enemy castles could spare now, less the capture's growth; it does not count what castle 0 (27, 3 a turn) produces during a 16-turn walk.

## 3. Turn 10: a neutral capture with 2 left in the middle

We sent 12 from castle 1 and 5 from castle 5 at neutral castle 2 (15). They took it on turn 24 with 2; the enemy's 27, sent on turn 16, took it on turn 31 with 18. Cost: 17 soldiers, and the neutral's 15 cleared for the enemy.

**The right move:** Keep the 17 and buy production 3 at castle 1 (20) on the next turn.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: `send 5->2 x5`, `send 1->2 x12` (`take>2`).

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats, by the same rule in `neutralOption()`: enemy castle 0 had 5 when we sent, and its later production is not counted.
