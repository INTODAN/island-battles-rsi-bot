# Game 543: the three main mistakes

Map estuary; the league bot was speedhelp_fullfeed, playing as team 1, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; won, the last team standing on turn 286.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 121: 160 turns without an attack

From turn 107 we sent nothing until turn 275. On turn 121 we held 331 in 5 castles (castle 5: 94, 2 from enemy castle 1's 160; castle 3 held 8, castle 7 48); by turn 269 the enemy's castle 1 held 1478.

**The right move:** Gather at castle 5 (0->5 64, 2->5 60, 4->5 76) and strike castle 1 or castle 3 the next turns.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: feeds castle 5 with exactly those 200 and buys production at castle 6.

**juggernaut_fix:** Avoids: feeds 4->5 76 (castles 0 and 2 keep a reserve against castle 1).

## 2. Turn 269: castle 5 left beside the enemy's 1478

Castle 5 (571) was 2.2 from castle 1 (1478, with 96 more landing in 2). We sent nothing; castle 1 sent 584 the next turn and took castle 5 on turn 271.

**The right move:** Feed castle 5 from castles 0, 2 and 4 (about 1660), making it hold and outnumber castle 1.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: feeds castle 5 with 553, 549, 561 and 283 (2237 in all).

## 3. Turn 275: the rear emptied beside the enemy's 660

Castle 1's 642 was landing on castle 5 in 1 (it then held 660); castles 1, 3 and 7 held 4 each. Castles 0 and 2 sent 514 and 573 into castle 4, keeping 4 each; castle 5 sent 531 at castle 0 on turn 278 (held only by 508 back from castle 4) and 52 at castle 2, which fell on turn 282.

**The right move:** Strike the empty castles 1, 3 and 7 from castle 4 (about 60 each) and keep castles 0 and 2 stocked against castle 5.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: sends 0->4 540 and 2->4 573 (and smashes castle 1 with 52 in 5). `feedStack` gives the stack each rear castle's `safeOut`, which counts only groups bound for that castle, not the enemy castle 4.5 away about to hold 660.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: the same feeds. Its feed reserve counts only enemy castles that land before the stack's help could (castle 5 needs 3 turns to castle 0, castle 4 2), so it keeps nothing.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 121: `["send 0->5 x64", "send 2->5 x60", "send 4->5 x76", "send 6->5 x37"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 269: `["send 0->5 x553", "send 2->5 x549", "send 4->5 x561", "send 6->5 x333"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 275: `["up 0 speed", "send 0->6 x7", "send 0->4 x540", "send 2->4 x573"]` (still makes it)
