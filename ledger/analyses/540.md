# Game 540: the three main mistakes

Map estuary; the league bot landgrab played team 1 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 92.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 23: castle 4 paid for in two landings (decided the game)

Neutral castle 4 (25) is 2.8 from castle 0 and 4.5 from enemy castle 1. Castle 0 sent 18 (six turns away at speed 1) and castle 2 sent 10 (nine turns away). The 18 landed alone and left castle 4 neutral at 7; the enemy sent 6 and 7 and took it on turn 31. We retook it with 2 on turn 32 and lost it on turn 36. Castle 4 became the enemy's production-4 stack (169 on turn 81), which struck castle 2 with 103 on turn 82.

**The right move:** Wait (castle 0 had 18 and production 3) and take castle 4 later in one landing, with enough to hold against castle 1.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: sends nothing (stack at castle 0).

## 2. Turn 56: help poured into a production-1 castle

Castle 6 (19, production 1, never upgraded since its capture on turn 29) had 28 enemy soldiers landing in 2 and 29 more in 7. Castle 0 sent 15 and castle 2 sent 9. The enemy had struck castle 6 every five turns since turn 45 and we had answered each time with 20 to 24, spending all of castle 0's production. Castle 6 fell on turn 59, was retaken on turn 60, and fell for good on turn 62. Castle 0 stayed at production 3 the whole game.

**The right move:** Keep the 24 home and let castle 6 go (or buy production 2 at castle 6 with its own 19); put castle 0's soldiers into production 4.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: `send 0->6 x15`, `send 2->6 x9`. `defend` sends help to every castle that a landing would take, by its walk, without asking what the castle is worth or whether the next strike takes it anyway.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats with the same commands; the fixes don't touch `defend`.

## 3. Turn 83: castle 0 emptied to save castle 2

A 103 strike from castle 4 landed on castle 2 (36) in 4, with 32 more sent on turn 84. Castle 0 (81) sent 52 on turn 83 and 24 on turn 85, keeping 14. Castle 4 (70, with 39 of its own landing) is 2.8 from castle 0; on turn 87 it sent 45 at castle 0 (20) and 30 at castle 2. Castle 0 fell on turn 89, castle 2 on turn 91, and we were out.

**The right move:** Keep castle 0's 81 home: castle 2 falls, but castle 0 (production 3) holds against castle 4.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: `send 0->2 x52`. `defend` sends the helpers' `safeOut`, which counts only the groups already bound for the helper, not an enemy castle that can strike it once it has sent.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats with the same command (its reserve rule is in `feedStack`, not in `defend`).

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 23: `[]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 56: `["up 2 generation_amount"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 83: `["send 2->0 x36"]` (avoids the mistake)
