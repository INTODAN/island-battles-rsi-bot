# Game 572: the three main mistakes

Map archipelago; our bot pounce_reach played as team 2 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 94.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 82: 138 into castle 8, the enemy's central stack

Castle 8 (123, production 4, speed 4) was the enemy's stack in the middle of the map, fed by its five other castles. We sent 109 from castle 5, 25 from castle 7 and 4 from castle 1 at it, landing in 3 to 4; castle 8 held 135 by then and the strike took nothing (43 more on turn 85 also failed). Castles 5 and 1 were left with 4. The enemy then took castle 1 on turn 88, castles 3 and 5 on turn 90 and castle 7 on turn 93.

**The right move:** No strike on a castle that grows and is fed faster than the strike lands; gather castles 5 and 7 into one castle and hold.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: no strike; it gathers 5:109 and 7:59 into castle 1.

## 2. Turn 52: neutral castle 8 worn down for the enemy to take

Neutral castle 8 (30, production 1) sat in the centre, 3 turns from enemy castle 6 (42, 68 on turn 53). We sent 25 from castle 3 and 7 from castle 11 at it, exactly enough, in two groups. The garrison was worn away and the enemy, with 5 from castle 6, took castle 8 on turn 55 with 3; we took it back on turn 56 and lost it on turn 58. It became the enemy's central stack (165 on turn 76) that took all our castles.

**The right move:** Leave the neutral to the side that spends less on it, or take it only with enough to hold against castle 6; keep castle 3's 25 home.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: take>8 with 11:7 and 3:25 (32 against 30). `neutralOption` prices the capture with `enemySpare`, which assumes castle 6 keeps its whole stock as a reserve against castle 1's 87 (7 turns away), so it counts no enemy soldier against the capture.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: the same take>8 11:7 3:25. Its capture pricing against nearby enemy castles covers strikes on enemy castles (`smash`), not `neutralOption`.

## 3. Turn 47: neutral castle 9 taken with nothing to hold it

Neutral castle 9 (30, production 1) was 3 turns from enemy castles 6 (46) and 4 (23). We sent 17 from castle 3 and 22 from castle 11, took it on turn 50 with 9, and lost it on turn 55 to 21 from castle 6 and 3 from castle 4. About 39 soldiers spent for 5 turns of a production-1 castle.

**The right move:** Do not take it; buy production at castle 7 and gather in castle 11.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: take>9 with 11:22 and 3:17, by the same `enemySpare` reserve that counts castle 6's 46 as kept against castle 1.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: buys production at castle 7, speed at castle 11, and gathers 1:67, 3:22, 5:12 into castle 11, with no capture.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 47: `["up 7 generation_amount", "send 1->11 x67", "send 3->11 x22", "send 5->11 x12"]`
- turn 52: `["send 1->9 x50", "send 7->9 x15"]`
- turn 82: `["send 7->3 x59"]`
