# Game 590: the three main mistakes

Map delta; our bot burstguard played team 2 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 92.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 78: castle 1 emptied to retake castle 5 while the enemy's 105 waited

Castle 1 held 156; enemy castle 2 held 105 at speed 4; castles 3 and 7 had 16 and 8. We sent 1->5 119 to retake castle 5 (4) and 1->7 6, leaving castle 1 with 5 after a speed upgrade. On turn 80 castle 2 sent 51 at castle 1, 59 at castle 3 and 23 at castle 7; all three fell on turns 83 to 85.

**The right move:** Keep castle 1 stocked against castle 2 (or gather castles 3 and 7 into it); retake castle 5 later.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats in an equivalent form: speed at castle 1, 1->7 6 and 1->7 120, 3->7 16, leaving castles 1 and 3 empty with castle 2's 105 six turns away (its stack is castle 7 instead of castle 5).

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: the same commands. Its rear reserve keeps only against an enemy castle that lands before the stack's help, and castle 7's help is faster than castle 2's walk.

## 2. Turn 82: upgrades that emptied castle 5 with 74 walking to it

Castle 5 had just been retaken with 107 (production 2, speed 1); enemy groups of 37 and 37 were walking to it (8 and 10 turns). We bought burst and production 2 to 4 (60 soldiers); castle 5 had 75 when the groups landed, 5 after them, and fell on turn 91 to 22 more.

**The right move:** No upgrade at castle 5 until the 74 have landed (or one production level).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: buys production 3 and 4 at castle 5 (50 soldiers), leaving about 10 over the 74 when the enemy's next 22 came.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: production 3 and 4 and speed 2 at castle 5 (60). The upgrade reserve counts what enemy castles hold (here 1 to 8 each), not the enemy groups already walking to the castle.

## 3. Turn 76: a strike at castle 4 that couldn't take it

We sent 3->4 36 at enemy castle 4 (6, production 1), leaving castle 3 with 12; the enemy fed castle 4 with 25 from castle 6 and the 36 were lost. Castle 3, so thinned, fell to 59 on turn 84.

**The right move:** No strike: keep castle 3's 44 and gather castle 1's 148 with it.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: speed at castle 3 and a feed 1->3 148.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: feeds castle 5 (1->5 29) and makes no strike.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 76: `["up 3 speed", "send 1->3 x148"]`
- turn 78: `["up 1 speed", "send 1->7 x6", "send 1->7 x120", "send 3->7 x16"]`
- turn 82: `["up 5 generation_amount", "up 5 generation_amount", "up 5 speed"]`
