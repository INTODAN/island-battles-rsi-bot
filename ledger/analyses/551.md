# Game 551: the three main mistakes

Map gyre; the league bot was speedhelp_fullfeed, playing as team 1, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 67.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 47: castle 2 emptied while enemy castle 3 stood beside it (decided the game)

Castle 2 held 18 (production 4); enemy castle 3 held 26 with 24 more landing. We sent 2->5 17 at castle 5 (8, 12 landing), keeping 5; castle 5 was not taken, castle 3 sent 21 at castle 2 on turn 48 and castle 2 fell on turn 50. Castle 4 fell the next turn, castles 6, 9 and 0 by turn 66.

**The right move:** Keep castle 2's 18 home.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats, equivalent: stack@9, feed>9 2:18, emptying castle 2 into the newly taken castle 9.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: the same 2->9 18. Its rear-castle reserve keeps only against enemy castles that land before the stack's help could (castle 3 needs 5 turns, the stack 2), but the stack at castle 9 holds 6 and has no help to give.

## 2. Turn 44: the stack spent on neutral castle 9 as the enemy gathered

Castle 2 (the stack) held 43 with 22 landing; enemy castles 3 (23, 34 landing) and 5 (30) stood beside castles 2 and 4 (16). We sent 2->9 30 at neutral castle 9 (25), taking it with 5. The next turn the enemy sent 65 at castle 4, which fell on turn 51, and our soldiers were split between castle 9 and the front.

**The right move:** No capture: gather on castle 4 (0->4 8, 2->4 43, 6->4 8).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: smash>9 in 2 with 27 from castle 2.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: stack@4, feed>4 0:8 2:43 6:8 (the stack moves off castle 2, which its landings would take).

## 3. Turn 58: castle 9 sent at castle 5 before the enemy's strike on it

Castle 9 held 36; enemy castle 2 held 25 with 68 landing on it. We sent 9->5 22 at castle 5 (4, 20 landing), keeping 17; castle 5 was not taken, and castle 2 sent 39 at castle 9 on turn 62: it fell on turn 63 with 29.

**The right move:** Keep castle 9's 36 home: with it castle 9 holds against the 39.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: keeps castle 9 and feeds it (0->9 13, 6->9 13).

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 47: `["send 2->9 x18"]`
