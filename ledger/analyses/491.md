# Game 491: the three main mistakes

Map claw; the league bot was landgrab, playing as team 1, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 102.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 88: castle 5 emptied into a strike on castle 2 while castle 3 was under attack

Castle 3 held 4 with enemy groups of 15 (in 1) and 13 (in 3) landing on it, and enemy castle 4 (14, production 4) stood beside both our castles. We sent 5->2 39 at castle 2 (11, 5 turns away), keeping 4; the 39 did not take castle 2. Castle 3 fell on turn 92, and the same turn the enemy sent 73 at castle 5 (20), which fell on turn 99; castle 0 fell on turn 101.

**The right move:** Keep castle 5's 39 home, or send it to castle 3 (lands in 2) with castle 0's 7.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: help>3, sends 0->3 7 and 5->3 39.

## 2. Turn 57: a strike on castle 2 in two waves that took nothing

Castle 2 was the enemy's with 10 (production 1), enemy castle 4 (20) beside it. We sent 0->2 20 on turn 57, then 3->2 20 and 5->2 9 on turn 59; the waves landed apart, enemy castle 4 fed castle 2 (8, then 12 and 13 more), and castle 2 stayed the enemy's: 49 soldiers lost for nothing while the enemy bought production.

**The right move:** No strike: buy production at castle 0 and keep the soldiers home.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: buys production at castle 0 and sends nothing.

## 3. Turn 29: a 12-turn walk to take castle 4, held one turn

Castle 4 was neutral with 10, enemy castle 6 (6) 7 turns from it. We sent 0->4 10 and 3->4 2 (speed 1, 12 turns from castle 0); castle 4 fell to us on turn 40 with 2 and the enemy took it back on turn 41 with 6, keeping it the rest of the game.

**The right move:** No take of castle 4: keep castle 0's 10 home (or buy production there) until a capture can hold against castle 6.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: take>4 with 3->4 2 and 0->4 10, exactly our move.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats the same take. Its fix prices strikes from the stack, not neutral takes: `neutralOption` holds against each enemy castle's spare now less the target's growth on the enemy's way (`enemySpare - rate(target)*travel(enemy, target)`), and ignores what castle 6 grows during our own 12-turn walk, so the 6 of castle 6 counts as nothing.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 29: `[]`
- turn 57: `["up 0 generation_amount"]`
- turn 88: `["send 0->3 x7", "send 5->3 x39"]`
