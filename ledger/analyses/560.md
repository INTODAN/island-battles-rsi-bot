# Game 560: the three main mistakes

Map star-of-david-duo; the hosted bot was burstguard, playing as team 1, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 117.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 84: burst potential bought in a front castle

The armies were even (our 435 in five castles against the enemy's 430 and 123 walking). Castle 6 (98) faced enemy castles 0 (107, speed 3) and 11 (138). We bought burst potential 1 to 4 there, 60 soldiers. On turn 85 castle 0 sent 80 at it, castle 11 70 and castle 0 26 more; castle 7 sent 103 to hold it, and castle 6 still fell on turn 97.

**The right move:** No burst: gather into castle 6 (or castle 7) so the front holds the strikes without draining the rear.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: feeds its stack at castle 6 with everything (1->6 98, 3->6 78, 7->6 128, 8->6 33); the fix sends 226 there.

## 2. Turn 94: a strike on a production-1 castle that emptied castle 7

Castle 4 was the enemy's with 33 (production 1), beside its castle 9 (119, speed 4). We sent 7->4 35 and 8->4 54, leaving castle 7 with 0 and castle 8 with 19. The same turn the enemy sent 56 at castle 7 and 45 at castle 6 (5); it fed castle 4 with 60, our 99 took nothing, and castle 6 fell on turn 97.

**The right move:** No strike: feed castle 6 from castles 1 and 3 (and hold castle 7).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: feeds castle 6 with 364; the fix sends 160 there.

## 3. Turn 98: a strike on castle 6 that the enemy could reach first

Castle 6 had just fallen (8 left). Castle 3 (134, speed 2) was six turns away; the enemy's castle 0 (84, speed 3) four. We sent 3->6 117. The enemy sent 87 from castle 0 and 62 from four other castles; castle 6 held with 70 on turn 104, and castle 3 fell on turn 116 with 11.

**The right move:** Keep castle 3's 134 at home or gather it with castle 1 (97); the enemy was spending 192 on castle 8 at the same time.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: smash>6 in 6 need 134, all of castle 3. Its smash price counts castle 0's 84 (it lands before the strike) but not the 62 that castles 2, 5, 10 and 11 can add, which arrive after the strike lands.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: its stack is castle 7, which it feeds (3->7 134, 1->7 32), and it sends 1->12 42 to take the middle castle.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 84: `["send 1->6 x20", "send 3->6 x78", "send 7->6 x128", "send 8->6 x33"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 94: `["send 1->6 x42", "send 3->6 x118", "send 7->6 x35", "send 8->6 x73"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 98: `["send 1->8 x97", "up 3 speed", "send 3->8 x34", "send 3->7 x80"]` (avoids the mistake)
