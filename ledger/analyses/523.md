# Game 523: the three main mistakes

Map delta; our bot was batchfront, playing as team 2, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 94.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 55: more waves at castle 4 that never took it

Enemy castle 4 held 10 and was fed from castle 6 (30). Castle 5 sent 22 and castle 1 sent 12 at it, an 8-turn walk, on top of 13 already walking. From turn 52 to 58 we sent 83 soldiers at castle 4 in six groups and it held; meanwhile castle 3 (21, production 1) fell on turn 57 to the enemy's 46, and we spent 60 more retaking it on turn 64.

**The right move:** No strike at castle 4: keep castle 5's 47 and castle 1's 13 home and buy production (castle 3 had production 1).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids the strike, but feeds everything to castle 3 (5->3 47, 1->3 13, 7->3 13), a 7-turn walk into a castle that falls in 3, leaving castle 5 empty. juggernaut_fix gathers at castle 5 instead (1->5 13, 7->5 13).

## 2. Turn 75: castle 5 emptied beside the enemy's stack (decided the game)

The enemy had just taken castle 3 (7). Enemy castle 2 held 98 at speed 3, 5.0 from castle 5 (37), so 2 turns away. Castle 5 sent 36 at castle 3 and kept 5; the same turn castle 2 sent 69 at castle 5, which fell on turn 78 (13 to 48). Castle 7 fell on turn 80 and castle 1 on turn 85.

**The right move:** Keep castle 5's 37 home and feed it from castles 1 and 7 (13 each), so castle 2's 69 cannot take it.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats an equivalent mistake: it sends 5->1 37 and 7->1 13, gathering at castle 1 and leaving castle 5 with 0 beside castle 2's 98.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: 1->5 13 and 7->5 13, a stack at castle 5. In juggernaut, `stackOf` takes the own castle nearest an enemy by walk at the slowest speed, ties by id: castles 1 and 5 are both 11 turns from an enemy, so castle 1 wins; the fix breaks the tie by the shortest total distance to the other own castles.

## 3. Turn 82: production and a 6-turn feed for a castle about to fall

Castle 4 (ours, 21) had enemy groups of 48 and 6 landing in 4 and 2; castle 1 (17) had 55 landing in 4. Castle 3 (74) bought production 4 for 30 and sent 43 to castle 4, a 6-turn walk: castle 4 fell on turn 84, the 43 landed on the enemy, and castle 3, left with 5, fell on turn 93 to 113.

**The right move:** Keep castle 3's soldiers home (production and speed bought there are fine): it was the castle the enemy would come for next.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: it buys production at castle 3 and sends 3->4 44 to make castle 4 its stack.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: castle 4 falls before any feed could land, so `stackOf` skips it; it keeps castle 3 as the stack and buys production 4 and speed 3 there.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 55: `["send 1->5 x13", "send 7->5 x13"]`
- turn 75: `["send 1->5 x13", "send 7->5 x13"]`
- turn 82: `["up 3 generation_amount", "up 3 speed", "up 3 speed"]`
