# Game 605: the three main mistakes

Map claw; the league bot fullfeed_unfreeze played as team 2 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 95.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 48: soldiers into the fight for castle 2 instead of production

Castle 2 (center, production 2) changed hands 16 times between turns 30 and 76, each capture with 1 to 7 soldiers. On turn 48 castle 1 sent 12 and castle 6 13 at it (13 in it), and castle 6 14 at castle 4 (8); castle 2 fell to us on turn 52 with 2 and back on turn 58. Castles 6 and 7 were still at production 3 and 2.

**The right move:** Leave castle 2 and buy production at castles 6 and 7, feeding castle 6 as the stack.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: buys production and speed at castle 6 and production at castle 7, and feeds 1->6 12.

## 2. Turn 69: castle 6 emptied at castle 3

Castle 6 (26) sent 23 at enemy castle 3 (5, 3.6 away), leaving 6, as it had sent 22 and 6 on turns 61 and 63. Castle 3 was fed and never fell, and the enemy stacked castle 4 (beside castle 6) with 12 + 12 + 27 + 20.

**The right move:** No strike: feed castle 6 from castles 1 and 7.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: 1->6 16 and 7->6 13, nothing at castle 3.

## 3. Turn 84: castle 6 left to fall

72 + 6 from enemy castle 4 landed on castle 6 (65) in 2. Castle 7 (60) sent 21 at castle 2 and castle 1 (60) sent 25 to castle 6 at speed 2, landing in 3. Castle 6 fell on turn 85 with 68 in it; we retook it on turn 86 and lost it on turn 88 to 54 more, and castles 1 and 7 fell on turns 93 and 94.

**The right move:** Speed at castle 1 so its help lands in 2, and castle 7's 60 to castle 6 as well.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: buys speed at castle 1, help 1->6 8 landing in 2, then 1->6 32 and 7->6 60.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 48: `["up 6 generation_amount", "up 6 speed", "up 7 generation_amount", "send 1->6 x12"]`
- turn 69: `["send 1->6 x16", "send 7->6 x13"]`
- turn 84: `["up 1 speed", "send 1->6 x8", "send 1->6 x32", "send 7->6 x60"]`
