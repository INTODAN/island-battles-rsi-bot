# Game 622: the three main mistakes

Map bight; punisher played as team 2 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost on turn 143.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 112: castle 2 left to fall by 9 (decided the game)

The enemy's stack in castle 3 sent 185 to castle 2 (172, production 3, speed 1), landing in 4. Castles 1 and 5 held 120 each. We sent nothing; castle 2 fell on turn 115 (181 to 1), and the enemy held the centre from then on.

**The right move:** Buy speed at castle 5 and send a few soldiers to castle 2 (it holds), then feed it.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: `up 5 speed`, `send 5->2 x2` (help>2 in 4), then feeds castle 2 (`1->2 x120`, `5->2 x98`).

## 2. Turn 123: an all-in strike that left castles 1 and 5 empty

With castle 3 (152) having sent 120 to retake castle 2, castle 5 sent 145 and castle 1 65 into castle 3, castle 1 sent 61 to castle 0 and 38 to castle 5; castles 1 and 5 kept 4 each. Castle 3 was taken on turn 130 with 2 and lost on turn 134; castle 2's 69 took castle 1 on turn 133.

**The right move:** No strike: gather castle 1 into castle 5 and buy its speed.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: `up 5 speed` twice, `send 1->5 x131` (stack@5); no strike.

## 3. Turn 99: saving apart while the enemy stacks

Castle 3 (225) was fed from castles 0 and 4 every turn, 5 away from castle 2 (133). Castles 1 and 5 held 68 each and fed nothing; castle 2 stayed at speed 1 and production 3.

**The right move:** Gather: feed castles 1 and 5 into castle 2 and buy its production and speed.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: production 4 and speed 4 at castle 2, `send 1->2 x68`, `send 5->2 x68`.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 99: `["send 1->2 x68", "send 5->2 x68"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 112: `["up 5 speed", "send 5->2 x2", "send 1->2 x120", "send 5->2 x98"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 123: `["send 1->5 x131"]` (avoids the mistake)
