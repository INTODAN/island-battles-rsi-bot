# Game 521: the three main mistakes

Map bight; the league bot landgrab played team 2 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 126.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 54: a retake of castle 3 that castle 2 could take back (decided the game)

Castle 3 (enemy, 13, production 1) is 7.8 from our castle 1 and 5 from enemy castle 2 (54 and fed 12 to 22 a turn). Castle 1 sent 48 and castle 5 sent 4; they took castle 3 on turn 61 with 31. On turn 62 castle 2 (85) sent 43, which took it back on turn 66; our help was eight turns away. That was about the eighth change of hands at castle 3. From then on we held two castles producing 8 a turn against four producing 16, and the enemy's 424 from castle 2 on turn 113 ended it.

**The right move:** No strike: keep the 48 and buy speed and production while castle 2 grows; a capture of castle 3 holds only with castle 2 kept busy.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: `send 1->5 x48`, feeding its stack at castle 5.

## 2. Turn 42: the same retake, smaller

Castle 1 sent 24 at castle 3 (enemy, 7, with our 12 landing in 2 and the enemy's 15 in 7). It retook castle 3 on turn 49 and lost it on turn 53. With the sends of turns 33 and 36 (12 each) about 100 soldiers went into castle 3 for no lasting gain.

**The right move:** Keep the 24 home (castle 1 was at speed 2 and production 4, castle 5 at production 3).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: `send 1->5 x24`.

## 3. Turn 14: the centre castle taken with a margin of 3

Castle 1 (19) sent 16 and castle 5 sent 2 at neutral castle 2 (15), fifteen turns away at speed 1. They took it on turn 28 with 3. Enemy castle 0 had 17, eight units away; it sent 15 on turn 15, which retook castle 2 on turn 30. Castle 2 became the enemy's stack (743 by turn 113).

**The right move:** Hold castle 1's soldiers (buy speed first) and take castle 2 later with a margin above what castle 0 could send by the capture plus two turns.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: `send 5->2 x2`, `send 1->2 x16`. `neutralOption` prices the capture at the neutral's 15 plus `neutralExtra` 2 plus what enemy castles could spare, and `enemySpare` keeps castle 0's 17 as its reserve against our castle 1, so the hold is 0.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats with the same commands; its price of holding a capture is in `smash`, not in `neutralOption`.
