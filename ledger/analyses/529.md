# Game 529: the three main mistakes

Map delta; our bot landgrab played team 1 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 148.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 69: castle 4 given help sized to the bare margin, 23 sent elsewhere

Castle 4 (49) faced about 100 landing from five enemy groups. We sent 6->4 12 and 0->4 2, and 0->5 23 at enemy castle 5 (3), which reinforcements kept. Castle 4 fell on turn 74 with nothing left on either side (13 to 0); the enemy then fed it for 55 turns into a stack of 612.

**The right move:** All spare soldiers into castle 4: 6->4 12, 0->4 25.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: help 6->4 12 and 0->4 2 (the same thin help), and castle 0's 23 feeds castle 2 instead of castle 4.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: the same help plus 0->4 23, as its stack is castle 4.

## 2. Turn 122: saving apart while the enemy stacked 504 at castle 4

Castle 4 (enemy) held 504 and grew by 12 to 24 a turn; we had 160, 148, 116 and 180 in four castles and sent nothing between turns 86 and 131. On turn 131 the stack sent 230 at castle 6 (220) and 147 at castle 5 (136); both fell, on turns 132 and 136.

**The right move:** Gather in one castle: 0->5 160, 2->5 148, 6->5 180 (or into castle 6).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: exactly 0->5 160, 2->5 148, 6->5 180.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats in part: only 0->6 160; castles 2 and 5 keep everything, because the new rear reserve keeps what an enemy castle could land before the stack's help, and the 504 stack reaches every castle first.

## 3. Turn 132: castle 5 left to fall and 162 sent at castle 3

230 were landing on castle 6 (220) in 1 and 147 on castle 5 (136) in about 4; castles 0 and 2 had 200 and 188. We sent 2->5 2 and 0->3 162 at castle 3 (4), 8 turns away; the enemy sent 127 back into castle 3 and 66 at castle 0, castle 5 fell on turn 136 and castle 0 on turn 147.

**The right move:** Hold castle 5: 0->5 200 and 2->5 166.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: help 2->5 2 and its stack is castle 5: 0->5 200, 2->5 166.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: help 2->5 2 only and no feed (the same rear reserve keeps castles 0 and 2 whole), so castle 5 falls as in the game; it does not make the strike at castle 3.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 69: `["send 6->4 x12", "send 0->4 x19"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 122: `["send 0->6 x160", "send 2->6 x148", "send 5->6 x116"]` (still makes it)
- turn 132: `["up 2 speed", "send 2->5 x168", "send 6->4 x115", "send 0->5 x200"]` (avoids the mistake)
