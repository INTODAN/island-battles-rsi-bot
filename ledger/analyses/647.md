# Game 647: the three main mistakes

Map gyre; our bot punisher played team 1 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 200.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 121: castle 4 helped short while castle 6 struck three castles

457 were landing on castle 4 (221) from castles 8, 3 and 5. We sent 0->4 129 and 2->4 96, and from castle 6 (202) sent 6->3 66, 6->9 30 and 6->7 15 at three other castles. Castle 4 fell on turn 126 by a few soldiers (9 to 4) and the 111 took nothing that lasted.

**The right move:** Everything into castle 4: 0->4 217, 2->4 123, 6->4 202.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: help 0->4 217 and 2->4 8, then its stack is castle 4: 2->4 115, 6->4 202 (and take>9 2->9 27, production at castle 2).

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: the same commands.

## 2. Turn 132: soldiers scattered at six castles

With castle 4 lost, we sent 0->8 17, 0->5 21, 0->4 9, 2->7 20, 2->9 3 and 2->3 4 (and more small groups on turns 130 to 134), about 150 in all, at enemy castles that each held more or were fed in time; none was taken.

**The right move:** Gather: 0->2 84 and 6->2 86.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: exactly 0->2 84 and 6->2 86.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: 6->2 86 (castle 0 keeps its 84 against castle 4 next to it).

## 3. Turn 175: saving apart while the enemy stacked 442 at castle 9

Castles 0, 2 and 6 held 185, 208 and 202 apart; enemy castle 9 (442) was fed every turn, 3 from castle 2. We sent nothing; on turn 178 castle 9 sent 229 at castle 2, which fell on turn 179 (224 to 1), then 380 took castle 0 on turn 185 and castle 6 fell on turn 199.

**The right move:** Gather in castle 2: 0->2 185 and 6->2 202.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: 0->2 185 and 6->2 202.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids for castle 2: 6->2 202 (410 against the 229); castle 0 keeps its 185 against castle 4 beside it.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 121: `["send 0->4 x217", "send 2->4 x180", "send 6->4 x202"]`
- turn 132: `["send 6->2 x86"]`
- turn 175: `["send 6->2 x202"]`
