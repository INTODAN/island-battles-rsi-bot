# Game 557: the three main mistakes

Map claw; our bot was burstguard, playing as team 2, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 89.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 55: a capture of castle 2 that could not hold

Enemy castle 2 held 28 (production 2) in the middle of the map, 3 from enemy castle 4 (40, with 32 landing). Castle 7 sent 38 at it and kept 4. It took castle 2 on turn 58 with 2; castle 4's 23 took it back on turn 65, and castle 7 sent 31 and 15 more on turns 66 and 68 without retaking it: about 84 soldiers for nothing.

**The right move:** No strike: gather castles 1 and 7 into one castle facing castle 4 and keep producing.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: 1->6 34 and 7->6 38, a stack at castle 6. juggernaut_fix does the same.

## 2. Turn 83: 50 soldiers spent on speed and burst beside the enemy's stack

Castle 1 held 116. Enemy castle 4 (116, speed 3) had 168 more landing on it and was sending 77 at castle 6 (64). Castle 1 bought speed 3 (20) and burst level 4 (30), sent 8 to castle 6 and kept 62. On turn 84 castle 4 sent 84 at castle 1; castle 1 fell on turn 88 with 1 left before the last 5 landed.

**The right move:** Keep castle 1's soldiers: buy at most the speed that gets help to castle 6 in time (20), and no burst level.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids the burst: it buys speed 3 at castle 1 and sends 1->6 8 and 1->6 88, 7->6 66 to stack at castle 6 (castles 1 and 7 left empty). juggernaut_fix buys speed 3 and sends 1->6 8 only, keeping 88 in castle 1.

## 3. Turn 85: castle 7 emptied as castle 4 turned on our castles (decided the game)

Castle 4 (66, speed 4) had sent 84 at castle 1 (66) and had 112 landing on it in 1. Castle 7 (74) sent 53 at castle 2 (15) and kept 15 (it also bought burst level 2). On turn 86 castle 4 sent 69 at castle 7: castle 6 fell on turn 87, castles 1 and 7 on turn 88.

**The right move:** No strike: keep castle 7's 74 home (or send castle 1 the 11 it needs), so castle 4 has no empty castle to take.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats an equivalent mistake: it buys speed 3 at castle 7, sends 7->1 11 as help and feeds 7->6 43 to its stack, leaving castle 7 with 0 beside castle 4.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: buys speed and sends 7->1 11, keeping 43 in castle 7. In juggernaut, `feedStack` sends every rear castle's whole safe surplus to the stack; the fix keeps a reserve against an enemy castle that lands before the stack's help.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 55: `["send 1->6 x34", "send 7->6 x38"]`
- turn 83: `["up 1 speed", "send 1->6 x8"]`
- turn 85: `["up 7 speed", "send 7->1 x11"]`
