# Game 511: the three main mistakes

Map archipelago; our bot landgrab played team 2 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 117.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 100: a strike at castle 4 that emptied castles 5 and 1

Enemy castle 4 had 4, but 133 from it were walking to castle 10 next door, and 113 were landing on our castle 8 (104) in 1 turn. We sent 5->4 122 and 1->4 29, leaving castles 5 and 1 with 4 each. The enemy sent castle 10's 125 back into castle 4 on turn 101, so the strike took nothing; castle 8 fell on turn 100, and the empty castles 5 and 1 fell on turns 113 and 116.

**The right move:** No strike: keep castle 5's 122 home (or feed castle 8 so it is retaken at once: 5->8 122, 1->8 29).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: help 3->7 24 and 11->9 7, and its stack is castle 8: 5->8 122, 1->8 29, 11->8 22, 3->8 9.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: the same help, no strike, and castle 5's 122 stays home.

## 2. Turn 77: 118 thrown into the enemy stack at castle 6

Enemy castle 6 held 195 (speed 3) after sending 109 at our castle 11. We sent 3->6 118, leaving castle 3 with 20; it died against castle 6's stock, which still sent 81 and 64 at castle 11 on turns 80 and 85.

**The right move:** Hold castle 11 (help 1->11 7 lands in 2) and gather the rest in one castle: no attack on a stack bigger than the attackers.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: help 1->11 7 and a feed of castle 7 (1->7 105, 3->7 134, 5->7 154).

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: the same help, and it gathers everything in castle 11.

## 3. Turn 84: 115 at castle 0 that the enemy could reinforce in time

We sent 7->0 115 at enemy castle 0 (20), 5 castles over, leaving castle 7 with 27. The enemy sent 2->0 24 and 10->0 45 the next turn, and castle 0 held; the 115 were lost.

**The right move:** No long strike: feed one stack (castle 5's 182 and castle 7's 138 together) until it beats the enemy castles that can help the target.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: feeds castle 7 (1->7 68, 3->7 44, 5->7 182).

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: feeds castle 11 with the same castles.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 77: `["up 1 speed", "send 1->11 x7", "send 1->11 x105", "send 3->11 x134", "send 5->11 x154", "send 7->11 x110"]`
- turn 84: `["send 1->11 x68", "send 3->11 x44", "send 5->11 x182", "send 7->11 x17"]`
- turn 100: `["send 3->7 x33", "up 11 speed", "send 11->9 x7"]`
