# Game 623: the three main mistakes

Map bight; the league bot was batchfront_keep, playing as team 2, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost on turn 220, down to castle 1 alone from turn 81 (about 600 against 2600 at the end).

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 66: the stack at castle 2 sent on an 8-turn strike at the enemy's home

Castle 2 held 59 (production 4); enemy castle 3 held 72 (production 4), 5 turns from castle 2 and from castle 5 (9). We sent 2->0 58 at castle 0 (12), keeping 17, and kept feeding castle 2 from castle 5 (left with 5). Castle 0 was not taken; on turn 67 castle 3 sent 49 at castle 5, which fell on turn 71.

**The right move:** Keep the stack home: castle 2's 59 (and castle 5's soldiers) hold against castle 3.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: smash>0 in 8 with 50 from castle 2, and feeds 1->2 9 and 5->2 9 (castle 5 emptied).

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats the same. `smash` spends the stack's `safeOut - guard`, and `guard` counts only enemy castles within `guardWalk` (4) turns: castle 3 (72) at 5 turns is left out although the strike walks 8.

## 2. Turn 75: castle 2 emptied while the enemy gathered beside it (decided the game)

Castle 5 had just fallen; castle 2 held 62 with 47 already walking home to castle 1, enemy castle 5 (32, 69 landing) and castle 3 (30) beside it. We sent 2->4 47 at castle 4 (4, 8 to 12 landing), keeping 19; castle 4 was not taken, the enemy sent 50 at castle 2 on turn 76 and castle 2 fell on turn 81.

**The right move:** Keep castle 2's 62 home.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats, worse: stack@1, feed>1 2:62, emptying castle 2.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: sends nothing; castle 2 keeps the reserve against castles 3 and 5, which land before castle 1 could help.

## 3. Turn 47: castle 5's 56 sent at castle 4 for nothing

Castle 5 held 61 (production 4); enemy castle 4 held 4 with feeds coming from castles 0 and 3, 10 away. We sent 5->4 56, keeping 9; castle 4 was fed and held (49 there on turn 56), and the 56 were lost.

**The right move:** Gather instead: feed castle 2 (5->2 61).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: stack@2, feed>2 5:61.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 47: `["send 5->2 x61"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 66: `["send 1->2 x9", "send 5->2 x9"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 75: `[]` (avoids the mistake)
