# Game 584: the three main mistakes

Map delta; the league bot was burstguard, playing as team 1, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; won, the last team standing on turn 266.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 162: a strike without the biggest stock

Castles 2 (136), 6 (171) and 4 (25) sent 332 at castle 3 (306, production 4, speed 4), while castle 0 kept 492 at home. Castle 3 held; we fell from 820 to 608 soldiers by turn 171, the enemy from 820 to 554, and the game stood still for 80 more turns.

**The right move:** Gather first, castle 0's 492 included, and strike with about 800, or gather at castle 5 beside castle 7 (325).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: feeds castle 5, its stack, with 492, 136, 29 and 171 (838 in all), 2 from castle 7.

**juggernaut_fix:** Avoids: feeds 0->5 186, keeping reserves in the castles facing castle 3.

## 2. Turn 240: castle 5 left beside castle 7's 539

Castle 5 (182) was 2 from castle 7 (539, with castle 1's 392 sent the same turn), while castle 0 held 804 in the far corner; castle 3 held 1. We sent 8 and 3 at castle 3; castle 7's 212 took castle 5 on turn 241 (186 lost), retaken only on turn 249.

**The right move:** Feed castle 5 from castle 0 and castle 4 (and strike castle 3's 1 with a few dozen).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: feeds castle 5 with 804, 316 and 180.

**juggernaut_fix:** Avoids: feeds 0->5 292, enough against the 212.

## 3. Turn 77: a strike at a production-1 castle beside the enemy's 47

Castle 2 (49) sent 47 at castle 5 (7, production 1), keeping 6, with enemy castle 7 (47) 2 away; castle 7 sent 35 the next turn. Castle 5 was not taken until turn 101 and then changed hands eight times by turn 142.

**The right move:** No strike: gather at castle 2 (0->2 152) and defend castle 4 (6->4 10).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: sends 6->4 10 to help castle 4, buys speed at castle 2 and feeds 0->2 152.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 77: `["send 6->4 x12", "send 0->2 x152"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 162: `["send 0->5 x186", "send 2->5 x136", "send 4->5 x29", "send 6->5 x171"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 240: `["send 0->5 x292", "send 4->5 x316", "send 6->5 x180"]` (avoids the mistake)
