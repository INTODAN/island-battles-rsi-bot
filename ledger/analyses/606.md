# Game 606: the three main mistakes

Map claw; our bot punisher, playing as team 2, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 66.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 48: castle 7 left to fall while its neighbours strike elsewhere

Castle 7 (26, production 2) had 27 landing now and three groups of 15 behind it. Castle 1 (7 left after sending 11 at castle 2) and castle 6 (15) could reach it; castle 6 sent its 15 at castle 4 instead. Castle 7 fell on turn 52 with 7 in it, and the enemy held 21 there, 3 turns from our home castle 1.

**The right move:** Send castle 6's 15 and castle 1's 7 to castle 7.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: help 1->7 7 and 6->7 15.

## 2. Turn 52: castle 1 fed out as castle 7 fell beside it

Castle 7 was falling that turn (45 landing on 7). Castle 1 (12, production 4, speed 2), 2.8 map units from it, sent 12 to castle 4 and kept 4; castle 6 sent 12 at castle 2. Castle 7, the enemy's with 21, bought speed and sent 25 at castle 1 on turn 54, and took it on turn 56 with 5. Keeping the 12 would have held (12 + 16 of growth against 25).

**The right move:** Keep castle 1's 12; send nothing to castle 4.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats exactly: help 6->2 12 and feed 1->4 12 (castle 4, with 2, is its stack). Rear castles feed the stack all they can spare; castle 7 is still ours on this board, so nothing counts it as an enemy castle.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: the same two sends. Its rear reserve counts only enemy castles that can land before the stack's help; a castle of ours that falls this turn is not counted as the enemy's castle it is about to become.

## 3. Turn 58: castle 6 sent out beside two enemy castles

Castle 6 (18) sent 13 at castle 2 (the enemy's, 3) and kept 8, while castles 1 (9) and 7 (11), both the enemy's, were 4 map units away. It took castle 2 on turn 61, but castles 1 and 7 sent 31 at castle 6, which fell on turn 64; castle 2 fell back on turn 65.

**The right move:** Keep castle 6's 18 (it grows 3 a turn) against castles 1 and 7.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: sends nothing.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 48: `["send 1->7 x7", "send 6->7 x15"]`
- turn 52: `["send 6->2 x12", "send 1->4 x12"]`
- turn 58: `[]`
