# Game 542: the three main mistakes

Map estuary; our bot was landgrab, playing as team 2, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 102.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 23: a split grab at the central castle 5

Neutral castle 5 (25, production 1) sits in the middle, next to the enemy. With 36 soldiers already walking to the far corner castle 7, castle 1 sent 18 and castle 3 sent 10 at castle 5, landing 4 turns apart at speed 0.5. They and an enemy 6 left castle 5 neutral with 7; the enemy took it on turn 31, we retook it with 2 and lost it on turn 36, and castle 5 became the enemy's hub (375 soldiers by turn 91).

**The right move:** No grab at castle 5 now: keep castles 1 and 3's 30 home and buy production, then take castle 5 in one group that lands at once.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: sends nothing (stack at castle 1). juggernaut_fix does the same.

## 2. Turn 80: two castles saving apart beside the enemy's stack

We held castles 1 (76) and 3 (72), 1.5 apart; the enemy held castle 5 with 247 (fed 24 every three turns), 2.8 from castle 1 and 4.0 from castle 3. From turn 60 to turn 92 we sent nothing: no gathering and no strike.

**The right move:** Gather: castle 3's soldiers into castle 1 (the castle facing castle 5), so one castle holds more than the enemy can send.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: buys production 4 at castle 3 and sends 3->1 42.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: sends nothing. Its new rear-castle reserve in `feedStack` keeps what an enemy castle that lands before the stack's help could take (castle 5's 247 and more), so castle 3 keeps all of it.

## 3. Turn 91: still apart, the turn before the enemy's 207 (decided the game)

Castles 1 (120) and 3 (105) were still apart; castle 5 held 375. On turn 92 castle 5 sent 207 at castle 1 (128); castle 3 emptied 72 into it to hold with 13, and on turn 97 castle 5 sent 53 at castle 1 and 85 at castle 3 (54): castle 1 fell on turn 99 and castle 3 on turn 101.

**The right move:** Gather castle 3 into castle 1 (225 in one castle), which the 207 strike cannot take.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: buys production 4 at castle 3 and sends 3->1 75.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: sends nothing, for the same reason (the `feedStack` reserve against castle 5).

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 23: `[]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 80: `["send 3->1 x72"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 91: `["send 3->1 x105"]` (avoids the mistake)
