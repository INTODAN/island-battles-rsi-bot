# Game 637: the three main mistakes

Map estuary; the hosted bot was fullfeed_unfreeze, playing as team 2, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 157.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 128: saving apart beside the enemy's stack (decided the game)

The enemy had stacked 196 in castle 4, 2 turns from our central castle 5 (96). Castles 1 (70) and 3 (75) were 3 and 5 turns from castle 5, castle 7 had 69. We sent nothing; castle 4 sent 105, took castle 5 on turn 129, and the enemy gathered over 190 there, took castles 3 and 7 on turns 144 and 145 and castle 1 on turn 156.

**The right move:** Feed castle 5 from castles 1, 3 and 7 (it then holds or is retaken at once), as on the turns before.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: feeds 1->5 70, 3->5 75 and 7->5 39.

## 2. Turn 115: soldiers trickled at castle 0

Castle 5 held 62; enemy castle 0 held 7, 5 turns away, beside enemy castle 4 (45), which the enemy kept feeding. We sent 29 from castle 5 to castle 0; it was the ninth such group since turn 90 (26, 26, 17, 17, 13, 17, 17, 13, 29: 175 soldiers), and castle 0 never fell.

**The right move:** Keep castle 5's 62 home and feed it from castles 1 and 3.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: buys speed 3 at castle 5, production at castle 7, and feeds 1->5 28 and 3->5 23.

## 3. Turn 135: thin strikes after the loss of castle 5

Castle 5 was the enemy's with 11, 112 landing on it in 1 and 80 more in 4 to 5. Castle 1 held 98, castle 3 93, castle 7 90. We sent 1->0 26 and 1->6 26 (strikes that took nothing), 3->1 71 and 7->5 17, keeping 26 in castle 3; on turn 136 the enemy sent 83 at castle 3 and took it on turn 144.

**The right move:** Gather in castle 1 (castle 3's 93 and castle 7's soldiers), no strikes.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats an equivalent: it strikes castle 4 (53) with 94 from castle 1, landing in 5, 2 turns from castle 5, where 192 will stand; `smash()` counts castle 5's 11 soldiers, not the groups landing on it. It also feeds 3->1 93 and 7->1 60.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: its price counts castle 5's landings, so it makes no strike and feeds 3->1 93.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 115: `["up 7 generation_amount", "send 1->5 x28", "send 3->5 x23"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 128: `["send 1->5 x70", "send 3->5 x75", "send 7->5 x69"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 135: `["up 7 generation_amount", "send 3->1 x93", "send 7->1 x60"]` (avoids the mistake)
