# Game 645: the three main mistakes

Map estuary; the league bot was punisher, playing as team 1, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; won, the last team standing on turn 127.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 84: castle 4 thinned beside the enemy's 66

Castle 4 (39, production 4, speed 1) sent 10 at neutral castle 6 and 7 at castle 7 (17), with castles 0 and 2 sending 20 more at castle 6; it kept 16. Enemy castles 1 (38) and 3 (28) were 4.5 and 5 away; they sent 72 the next turn and took castle 4 on turn 90 (castle 6 was taken on turn 90 with 5).

**The right move:** Keep castle 4's 39 and feed it (0->4 12, 2->4 8); take castle 6 later from a held castle 4.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: smashes castle 6 with 27 from castle 4 (8 turns at speed 1), buys speed there and feeds 0->4 12, 2->4 8, leaving castle 4 about 30 short of the 72. `smash` keeps the stack's `guard`, which counts only enemy castles within `guardWalk` (4) turns; castles 1 and 3 are 5 away.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: the same commands. Its hold pricing applies only to enemy-owned targets, and its guard has the same 4-turn reach.

## 2. Turn 98: castle 6 left to fall

Castle 6 (29, production 1) had the enemy's 29 landing in 1 and 40 more in 6. Castle 4 (26), 3.9 away, sent 21 at castle 1 (8) instead; castle 6 fell on turn 103 with 34 for the enemy.

**The right move:** Help castle 6: 4->6 26 and 0->6 8.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: exactly 4->6 26 and 0->6 8, and feeds 2->4 8.

## 3. Turn 101: one more small wave at castle 1

Castle 4 (28) sent 11 at castle 1 (20), after 21 and 13 on turns 98 to 99; between turns 98 and 109 it sent about ten waves of 6 to 21, and castle 1 fell only on turn 111.

**The right move:** No wave: gather at castle 4 and strike once with enough.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: buys speed at castle 4, sends 8 to help castle 6 and feeds castle 4 (0->4 8, 2->4 8).

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 84: `["send 0->4 x12", "send 2->4 x8"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 98: `["send 4->6 x26", "send 0->6 x8", "send 2->4 x8"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 101: `["send 0->4 x8", "send 2->4 x8"]` (avoids the mistake)
