# Game 589: the three main mistakes

Map gyre; our bot was juggernaut, playing as team 1, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; won on turn 90. Game 592 is the same game move for move with the sides swapped.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake. The champion played this game, and on each board it gives the commands it really sent.

## 1. Turn 30: the neutral centre castle taken with 4

Castle 2 sent 16 and castle 4 sent 15 at neutral castle 9 (25), 7 turns away. They took it on turn 37 with 4; the enemy's 28 and 2, sent on turn 32 from castles 5 and 1, took it on turn 38 with 23, and the enemy built it up to 83 before we took it back on turn 74. Cost: 31 soldiers and the centre for 36 turns.

**The right move:** Keep the 31 and buy production 3 at castle 2 (20).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: `send 2->9 x16`, `send 4->9 x15` (`take>9`). `neutralOption()` holds the capture only against what the enemy castles could spare now, less the capture's growth, not what castles 5 and 1 (production 3 and 4) produce during the 7-turn walk.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats, by the same rule in `neutralOption()`.

## 2. Turn 40: a strike on castle 9 with a margin of 2

The stack, castle 2 (28), struck enemy castle 9 (15, 4 turns away) with 25. On turn 41 enemy castle 5 bought speed 3 and sent 4, and castle 1 sent 12; they landed first, and castle 9 kept 4. The 25 were lost.

**The right move:** No strike: keep the 28 in the stack.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: `send 2->9 x25` (`smash>9 in 4 need 25`), with production 4 at castle 4 and a feed from castle 6. `smash()` counts a neighbour's stock only if it lands by then at its present speed (castle 5 at speed 1: 7 turns).

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: its hold term counts enemy castles within `guardWalk` (4 turns) at their present speed, and a speed purchase brings castle 5 in.

## 3. Turn 55: a strike on castle 8 that couldn't take it

The stack, castle 2 (103, speed 4), struck enemy castle 8 (19, production 1, 3 turns away) with 24, its expected 22 plus 2. Enemy castle 5 sent 4 at speed 3, castle 8 kept about 1, and the 24 were lost; taking it took 56 more on turn 60 and two more groups (turn 66).

**The right move:** No strike, or one with castle 5's and castle 3's 47 each counted.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: `send 2->8 x24`, `send 0->2 x8` (`smash>8 in 3 need 24`).

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: only `send 0->2 x8`; its hold term prices the capture against castles 3 and 5 nearby, so it keeps the 24.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 30: `[]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 40: `["up 4 generation_amount", "send 6->2 x8"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 55: `["send 0->2 x8"]` (avoids the mistake)
