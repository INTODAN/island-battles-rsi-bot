# Game 515: the three main mistakes

Map bight; our bot landgrab played as team 1 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost on turn 133.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 54: a retake beside the enemy's stack

Castle 2 (enemy, 13, production 1) stood 5 from castle 3 (enemy, 54, with 46 landing in 3 to 6). We sent 0->2 48 and 4->2 4, took castle 2 on turn 61 with 31 and lost it on turn 66 to castle 3's 42.

**The right move:** No strike: gather (0->4 48).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: 0->4 48.

## 2. Turn 42: the first of the castle 2 retakes

Our 12 was landing on castle 2 (enemy, 7) in 2; 15 enemy landed there in 7. We added 0->2 24; castle 2 changed hands on turns 43, 48, 49, 53, 61 and 66, about 100 soldiers on both sides for a production 1 castle.

**The right move:** 0->4 24, gather.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: 0->4 24.

## 3. Turn 100: two castles saved apart and idle

From turn 66 to 121 we sent nothing: castles 0 and 4 held 184 and 194, while the enemy fed castle 3 to 530 (876 by turn 122). On turn 122 it sent 481 at castle 4 and then 70 and 64; castle 4 fell on turn 131 and castle 0, emptied to help, on turn 132.

**The right move:** One stack with speed: 0->4 184, speed 3 and 4 at castle 4.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: exactly that.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 42: `["send 0->4 x24"]`
- turn 54: `["send 0->4 x48"]`
- turn 100: `["send 0->4 x184"]`
