# Game 580: the three main mistakes

Map bight; our bot was pounce_reach, playing as team 2, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 189.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 32: a far grab of neutral castle 3 that could not hold

Neutral castle 3 (15, production 1) sits at the far side of the map, 5 from enemy castles 2 (11, with 20 landing) and 4 (3, production 3), and 7.6 from castle 1 (an 8-turn walk). Castle 1 (26) sent 17 at it; it was taken on turn 39 with 2 and lost on turn 41. Castle 3 stayed the enemy's for the rest of the game.

**The right move:** Keep the 17 home: buy production at castle 5 (production 2) and gather.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: take>3 with 1->3 17 (and feeds 1->5 9).

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats with the same commands. `neutralOption` prices the hold only against what enemy castles can spare now (`enemySpare`) less the capture's growth; it ignores their growth and the groups landing on them during an 8-turn walk.

## 2. Turn 100: two castles saving apart for 137 turns

From turn 41 to turn 178 we sent nothing. On turn 100 castles 1 (281) and 5 (214) sat apart while enemy castle 2 held 312 and was fed every turn; by turn 178 castle 2 held 1737, and our castles 593 and 526.

**The right move:** Gather castle 1 into castle 5 and buy it speed, so one stack can strike or hold.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: buys speed 3 and 4 at castle 5 and sends 1->5 281. juggernaut_fix does the same (and so on turn 60, 1->5 121).

## 3. Turn 179: help sent to the exact count (decided the game)

Castle 2 sent 910 at castle 5 (530), landing in 5; castle 1 held 597. We sent 361, the count our defence needed, and kept 236; the enemy then sent 138 and 132 more, and our late 130 and 118 (castle 1 left with 4) were not enough: castle 5 fell on turn 187 with the enemy keeping 1, and castle 1 fell on turn 188 to 98.

**The right move:** Send castle 1's whole 597 to castle 5 at once, the castle the enemy had committed its stack to.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: help 1->5 361 and feed 1->5 236, the whole 597. juggernaut_fix does the same.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 32: `["send 1->5 x26"]`
- turn 100: `["send 1->5 x281"]`
- turn 179: `["send 1->5 x361", "send 1->5 x236"]`
