# Game 624: the three main mistakes

Map bight; our bot fullfeed_unfreeze played as team 2 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 121.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 94: castle 5 fed castle 2 and was left with 4 beside the enemy's stack

The enemy was feeding castle 3 (26 on turn 94, 62 on turn 98) with castles 0 and 4. Castle 5 (12) sent its 12 to castle 2 (31), as it had on turns 88 and 91, leaving 4. On turn 99 castle 3 sent 61 at castle 5 (24), which no castle of ours could reach in time (castle 2 is 10 turns away at speed 1, castle 1's 20 was not enough), and castle 5 fell on turn 103.

**The right move:** Keep castle 5's stock home (it is the castle nearest castle 3), and feed it rather than castle 2.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: buys production at castle 2 and feeds it with 1:8 and 5:12, leaving castle 5 with 0. `feedStack` gives a rear castle's whole `safeOut`, which counts only the groups already landing on it.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: buys production at castle 2 and keeps castles 1 and 5 home: castle 3 can land on castle 5 before the stack in castle 2 could help it, so castle 5 keeps a reserve.

## 2. Turn 105: a strike on castle 4 while 47 landed on castle 1

Castle 3 sent 47 at castle 1 (20), landing in 5. Castle 2 (65) sent 26 at enemy castle 4 (4, fed by castle 0) and only 8 to castle 1. Castle 1 held by a single soldier; the 26 took nothing.

**The right move:** Buy speed at castle 2 and send its stock to castle 1 (45 or more), so castle 1 holds well and becomes the stack.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: buys speed at castle 2 and sends 2->1 8 (help) and 2->1 37 (feed).

## 3. Turn 113: the strike repeated while castle 1 fell

Castle 1 (13) had 22 + 5 from castle 5 landing in 2. Castle 2 (43) sent 26 at castle 4 again and 11 to castle 1. Castle 1 fell on turn 114, and the 26 took nothing; castle 2, our last castle, fell on turn 120.

**The right move:** Send castle 2's 43 to castle 1, to retake it at once after the 27 land (it holds 6 then).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: no strike; it sends 2->1 43.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 94: `["send 1->2 x8", "send 5->2 x12"]` (still makes it)
- turn 105: `["up 2 speed", "send 2->1 x8", "send 2->1 x37"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 113: `["send 1->2 x13"]` (avoids the mistake)
