# Game 571: the three main mistakes

Map bight; the league bot was burstguard, playing as team 1, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 189.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 138: saving apart while the enemy stacked (decided the game)

From turn 41 to 178 we sent nothing: castle 0 and castle 4 grew side by side (433 and 366 on turn 138) while the enemy gathered everything into castle 3 (978, more walking in). On turn 178 castle 3 sent 910 at castle 4 (530); our help from castle 0 (361, 130, 118) came in pieces, castle 4 fell on turn 187 and castle 0, emptied, on turn 188.

**The right move:** One stack: buy speed at castle 4 and move castle 0's 433 into it (about 800, then over 1100 by turn 178, against the enemy's 910).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: stack@4, buys speed 3 and 4 at castle 4 and feeds 0->4 433.

## 2. Turn 182: castle 0 emptied to help a castle that could not be held

Castle 4 held 903 with 910 landing in 2 turns and 138 and 132 more behind; castle 0's 130 was on its way. We sent 0->4 118 as well, leaving 4. After the 910 landed castle 4 had 131, and the 270 behind took it on turn 187 with 11 to spare; the enemy then sent 98 at castle 0, which fell on turn 188 with 20.

**The right move:** Keep castle 0's 118 home: castle 4 falls either way, castle 0 then holds against 98.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: help>4 in 4 with 0->4 118. `defend` sizes the help to hold castle 4 and is short by about 10 here, so it empties castle 0 for a castle that still falls.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats the same 0->4 118; the fix does not change `defend`.

## 3. Turn 32: a take of castle 2 that held two turns

Castle 2 was neutral with 15, enemy castle 1 (4, production 4) and castle 3 (11) able to reach it. We sent 0->2 17; castle 2 fell to us on turn 39 with 2 and enemy castle 1's 7 took it back on turn 41. The enemy kept it, and with it the fourth castle that out-produced us for the rest of the game.

**The right move:** No take of castle 2 yet: gather at castle 4 and take it with enough to hold (about 25).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: take>2 with 0->2 17 (and feed>4 0:9).

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats the same take. `neutralOption` holds only against each enemy castle's spare now, less the target's growth on the enemy's way; castle 1's growth during our 7-turn walk is not counted.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 32: `["send 0->4 x26"]`
- turn 138: `["send 0->4 x433"]`
- turn 182: `["send 0->4 x118"]`
