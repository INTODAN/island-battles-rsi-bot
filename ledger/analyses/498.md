# Game 498: the three main mistakes

Map star-of-david-duo; our bot landgrab played team 1 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 109.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 51: a staggered strike at the neutral centre castle 12

Castle 12 (neutral, 40) sits at the centre, ringed by enemy castles 9, 10 and 11. We sent 7->12 39 (speed 2, lands in 3), 6->12 15 and 8->12 18 (speed 1, about 6 turns). The 39 landed alone and only wore the neutral down to 1; the slow 33 then met the enemy's 29, we added 7->12 24 on turn 57, took castle 12 on turn 62 with 21 and lost it on turn 68. About 96 soldiers went into castle 12, and the enemy fed it into a stack of 189 that took castles 6, 8, 7, 1 and 3 between turns 95 and 108.

**The right move:** No strike at castle 12: keep the 72 home and buy production (or one strike that lands together, with what holds it against the three enemy castles beside it).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: take>12 with 7->12 39 and 6->12 15, the same staggered landing (3 and 6 turns).

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: the same two sends. Its capture price counts enemy castles within 4 turns only, and castles 9, 10 and 11 are 6 turns away at their speed then; nor does a take wait for its sources to land together.

## 2. Turn 91: castle 6 left to fall to the enemy stack

Castle 6 held 110; the enemy sent 124 from castle 11 (lands in 5) and 7 from castle 12, whose 189 sat 2 turns away. Castle 7 had 89 and castle 3 had 62; we sent only 3->6 2. The enemy added 8 more from castle 12 on turn 93 and castle 6 fell on turn 95 with 121 in it.

**The right move:** Feed castle 6: 7->6 89 and 3->6 40.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: help 3->6 2 and 1->8 8, and its stack is castle 6: 7->6 89, 3->6 40, 1->6 8.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: the same help 3->6 2 (sized to the bare margin), but its stack moves to castle 7 (1->7 8, 3->7 40) because the new stack rule skips a castle whose landings take it before a feed lands; castle 6 is left with the 2 that did not save it in the game.

## 3. Turn 97: help that held neither castle 1 nor castle 7

Castle 1 held 32 with 42 (lands in 1) and 60 from the enemy stack on it; castle 7 had 86, 3 turns from the enemy stack at castle 12 (135). We sent 7->1 48, leaving 42; castle 1 fell anyway on turn 103 and castle 7 fell on turn 101 to 66 from castle 12.

**The right move:** Keep castle 7 whole and feed it (3->7 64); castle 1 cannot be held against 102.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats in an equivalent form: 7->1 48, 3->1 64 and 7->1 38 hold castle 1 but leave castle 7 empty, 3 turns from the 135 stack.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids the main cost: 7->1 48 and 3->7 64, keeping castle 7 at about 100 against the 66 that came; the 48 help is still spent on castle 1.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 51: `["send 7->6 x39"]`
- turn 91: `["up 3 speed", "send 3->6 x2", "up 1 speed", "send 1->8 x8", "send 1->7 x8", "send 3->7 x40"]`
- turn 97: `["send 7->1 x48", "send 3->7 x64"]`
