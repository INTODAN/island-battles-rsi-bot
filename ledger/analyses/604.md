# Game 604: the three main mistakes

Map claw; our bot batchfront_keep played team 2 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 93.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 79: the centre castle 2 thinned for a strike at castle 6

Castle 2 (115) sits at the centre, 3 to 4 from enemy castles 0, 3, 4 and 5 (28, 48, 63, 48). We sent 2->6 59 at castle 6 (10, fed by the enemy), leaving 56. The next turn castles 3 and 5 sent 86 at castle 2 and castle 4 sent 60 into castle 6; castle 2 fell on turn 83 (72 to 10) and castle 6 stayed the enemy's. Castles 1 and 7 fell on turns 88 and 92.

**The right move:** Keep castle 2 whole (115 and growing against the 86 that came), and feed it from castles 1 and 7.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats, worse: its stack is castle 1 (the castle nearest enemy castle 6), and it sends 2->1 115, emptying castle 2.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: 2->1 59, leaving castle 2 with 56, as in the game. Its rear reserve keeps what the single strongest enemy castle landing before the stack's help could take (castle 3), not the sum of castles 3 and 5 that struck together.

## 2. Turn 59: the same mistake twenty turns earlier

Castle 2 held 76; we sent 2->6 53 at castle 6 (4), leaving 23. Castles 5, 3 and 0 sent 91 at castle 2 on turn 61 and it fell on turn 65; we retook it only on turn 70.

**The right move:** Keep castle 2 whole and feed it (1->2, 7->2).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats, worse: 2->1 76 and 7->1 9, emptying castle 2.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: 2->1 53, keeping 23 (castle 3's 38 less its growth), as in the game.

## 3. Turn 85: a strike at castle 4 that emptied castles 1 and 7

Enemy castle 4 had 4, but the enemy's 121 were landing in castle 6 beside it. We sent 1->4 40 and 7->4 16, leaving 5 in each; castle 4 was fed 30 and held, castle 6 sent 21 at castle 1 (fell on turn 88) and 55 at castle 7 (fell on turn 92, the end).

**The right move:** No strike: gather castle 7 into castle 1 (7->1 17).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: speed at castle 1 and 7->1 17.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: 7->1 17.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 59: `["send 2->1 x53", "send 7->1 x9"]`
- turn 79: `["send 2->1 x59"]`
- turn 85: `["send 7->1 x17"]`
