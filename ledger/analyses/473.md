# Game 473: the three main mistakes

Map claw; the hosted bot was harrier_feedkeep, playing as team 1; lost, eliminated on turn 119.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 86: feeding down beside a growing enemy stack

Enemy castle 4 had 13 with 37 landing within 4 turns (54 the next turn). We bought production at castle 3, struck castle 2 with 8 and fed 0->3 12 and 5->3 12 (as on turn 83), leaving castle 5 at 4. The enemy struck castle 5 with 43 + 12 on turn 87; holding it cost 28 more and it fell on turn 95 anyway.

**The right move:** Buy production at castle 3, feed only 0->3 12, keep castle 5's 12 home, no strike on castle 2.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeated, worse: smash>2 with 21 from its stack castle 3, leaving 21 (13 with the later fix of captures) beside castle 4's 54.

**Proposed fix (not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed):** The stack's guard now counts the groups landing on each enemy castle within 4 turns, not only what it holds; on this board juggernaut keeps 42 in castle 3 and only feeds it.

## 2. Turn 91: no help against the second wave

Castle 5 had 26 with our 22 landing in 1 and the enemy's 55 in 2, so it survived with about 1; a second wave of 12 + 33 landing in 5 was on the board. We sent 7 at castle 2. Castle 5 fell on turn 95 and became the enemy's forward stack, which took castle 3 (turn 110) and castle 0 (turn 118).

**The right move:** Send 0->5 14 and 3->5 19 (both land in time); top up on turn 93.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: help>5 0:14 3:19, and on turn 93 0->5 22 with speed bought at castle 3.

## 3. Turn 49: a long strike at a castle its neighbours refill

Castle 4 (enemy, 7) was 5.8 from castle 0, a 6-turn walk; enemy castles 6 and 1 fed it 11, 9 and 12 meanwhile. We sent 0->4 20 and dripped more until turn 54, 52 in all, captured it with 2 on turn 54 and lost it on turn 55.

**The right move:** Send nothing at castle 4; keep castle 0's 20 for production 4.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: sends nothing. Its smaller strikes on turns 52 and 54 (10 and 14, thin captures) are removed by the capture fix of game 471.
