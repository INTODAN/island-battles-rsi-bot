# Game 561: the three main mistakes

Map star-of-david-duo; our bot pounce_reach played team 1 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 159.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 151: help to castle 1 that emptied castle 8 beside the enemy's 362

349 from the enemy stack were walking to castle 1 (93, 7 turns), and 250 to castle 7 (81). Castle 8 held 327, 4 turns from enemy castle 9 (362). We sent 8->1 229, leaving 98; castle 9 sent 226 at castle 8 the next turn and it fell on turn 155 (114 to 108), castle 1 fell on turn 158 and the game ended on turn 159.

**The right move:** Keep castle 8 whole against castle 9 and gather castle 1 into it (1->8 93); castle 1 could at best just hold 349.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats, worse: help 8->1 229 and a feed 8->7 98, leaving castle 8 empty beside 362.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: help 8->1 229, castle 8 keeps 98. `defend()` sends help from any castle that can land in time without keeping what an enemy castle beside the sender could take; the fix's new reserve applies only to feeds of the stack.

## 2. Turn 113: a retake of castle 6 in two waves beside the enemy's home

Castle 6 had just fallen (enemy 6), 3 turns from enemy castle 0 (133) and fed by four more. We sent 3->6 72, then 3->6 49 on turn 114; the enemy sent 85 into castle 6 on turns 113 to 114 and more each turn after. The 121 were lost and castle 6 grew into the enemy's stack of 659.

**The right move:** No retake: help castle 7 (1->7 98) and gather castles 1, 3 and 8 in one castle.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats in an equivalent form: help 1->7 98 and smash>6 with 3->6 165 in one wave landing in 6, against the same reinforcements (more than 200 by turn 118).

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: help 1->7 98 and gathers everything in castle 7 (1->7 86, 3->7 192, 8->7 175).

## 3. Turn 129: saving apart while the enemy stacked at castle 6

The enemy stack at castle 6 (163, fed 12 at a time by five castles) sent 186 at castle 3 (135, lands in 3). Castles 1 (113) and 8 (239) were too far to help; we sent only 1->7 7 and kept everything apart. Castle 3 fell on turn 131 and the stack reached 659 by turn 147.

**The right move:** Gather: 1->7 113 and 8->7 239 (or into castle 3 to retake it).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: 1->3 113 and 8->3 239.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: 1->7 113 and 8->7 239.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 113: `["send 1->7 x98", "send 1->7 x86", "send 3->7 x192", "send 8->7 x175"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 129: `["send 3->7 x135", "send 1->7 x113", "send 8->7 x239"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 151: `["send 1->8 x93"]` (avoids the mistake)
