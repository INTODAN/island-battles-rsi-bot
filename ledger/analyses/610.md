# Game 610: the three main mistakes

Map star-of-david-duo; the league bot was batchfront_keep, playing as team 2, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 112.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 78: castle 8 emptied into neutral castle 12 beside the enemy's stack (decided the game)

Castle 8 held 56 (production 4); enemy castles 1 (51) and 7 (37) were within reach, and 69 enemy soldiers were landing on castle 9. We sent 8->12 55 at neutral castle 12 (40, production 2), keeping 5. On turn 79 castle 1 sent 48 at castle 8, which fell on turn 84; castle 12, taken on turn 83 with 20, fell the next turn. Castle 9 fell on turn 104 and the rest by 112.

**The right move:** No strike: keep castle 8's 56 home.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: smash>12 in 6 with 42 from castle 8 (and feeds 4->8 13, 10->8 13).

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: sends nothing (stack@10).

## 2. Turn 64: castle 11 emptied into castle 8 beside enemy castle 6

Castle 11 held 23 (production 4); enemy castle 6 held 31, 6 turns away. We sent 11->8 22, keeping 1 (5 after growth); on turn 65 castle 6 sent 34 at castle 11, which fell on turn 70.

**The right move:** Keep castle 11's 23 home (it grows to about 47 by the landing).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: stack@8, feed>8 11:23, emptying castle 11.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats, marginally: feeds 11->8 15 and keeps 8. Its rear-castle reserve is castle 6's stock now less castle 11's growth on the walk (31 - 24 + 1); the enemy waited a turn and sent 34, so 8 kept still falls short by about 2.

## 3. Turn 24: a 10-turn strike at castle 8 that took nothing

Castle 8 was the enemy's with 3 (production 1); enemy castles 7 and 1 could feed it. We sent 10->8 17 (castle 10 left with 5), then 8, 4 and 8 more on turns 26 to 29; the enemy fed castle 8 and it held: 37 soldiers lost while the enemy took castles 1 and 3.

**The right move:** No strike: keep castle 10's soldiers home until they can take a neutral castle on our side (castle 2, 4 or 5, 20 each) in one group.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: smash>8 in 10 with 15.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats the same smash. Its hold counts only enemy castles that land within `guardWalk` (4) turns of the target; castle 7, 12 turns away, is left out although our own walk is 10.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save).
