# Game 472: the three main mistakes

Map claw; the hosted bot was harrier_feedkeep, playing as team 1; lost, eliminated on turn 130.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 89: castle 3 lost to the first strike of the enemy stack (decided the game)

Enemy groups of 45 and 6 from castle 4 landed on castle 3 (38, production 3) in 2 turns: about 44 against 51. Castle 0 had 40 at 2.8 (3 turns at speed 2), castle 5 37 at 4 turns. We sent 5 to harass castle 2 instead; castle 3 fell on turn 90 with 41 and the enemy stacked it to 353.

**The right move:** Buy speed 3 at castle 0 and send it 0->3 20 (lands in 2), castle 5's 37 behind.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: up 0 speed, 0->3 8 + 12 and 5->3 37; on turn 88 it had already fed castle 3 (0->3 36, 5->3 33).

## 2. Turn 36: castle 2 lost while castle 3 attacked

An enemy group of 26 landed on castle 2 (17, production 1) in 4 turns; castle 3 had 19 at 3.6 away. We bought production at castle 5 and sent 3->4 19 and 0->4 4; castle 2 fell on turn 39 and the 19 died in clashes on turns 42 to 44.

**The right move:** Buy speed at castle 3 and send 3->2 6, landing with the enemy's group; no strike on castle 4.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: up 3 speed, 3->2 6 (exact, retakes with 1).

## 3. Turn 122: castle 5 left to fall

An enemy group of 123 landed on castle 5 (108) in 2 turns; castle 0 had 152 at 2.8 (3 turns at speed 2). We sent nothing; castle 5 fell on turn 123 with 112.

**The right move:** Buy speed at castle 0 and send 20 or more to castle 5.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: up 0 speed, 0->5 8 (the exact lack).
