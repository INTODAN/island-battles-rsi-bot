# Game 506: the three main mistakes

Map archipelago; the league bot landgrab played team 1 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 174.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 157: a far strike that three enemy castles could refill (decided the game)

Castle 4 held 198. It sent 123 at enemy castle 3 (7), five turns away, with 20 from castle 0 and 20 from castle 2 already on the way. Enemy castles 7 (58), 11 (79) and 1 (20) are closer to castle 3 and sent 137 on turn 158; the strike took nothing. On turn 158 enemy castle 9 (128, two turns from castle 10) sent 38 at castle 10 (20) and took it on turn 159, then 51 at castle 6. We went from 247 soldiers on turn 155 to 117 on turn 160, and castles 6, 4, 0 and 2 fell by turn 173.

**The right move:** No strike: move castle 4's stock to castle 10, the castle two turns from enemy castle 9 (128).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats in effect: no strike, but `send 0->6 x20`, `send 2->6 x8`, `send 4->6 x198`, `send 10->6 x20`. `stackOf` ties castles 6 and 10 at 5 turns from an enemy and takes the lower id, and `feedStack` empties castle 10 (no enemy group is bound for it yet) two turns from castle 9's 128.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: makes castle 10 the stack (shorter feeds) and sends castles 0, 2, 4 and 6 there, 247 in all. It empties castle 6 (21) though, three turns from enemy castle 11 (79).

## 2. Turn 142: a neutral taken in two landings, a turn apart

Castles 10 and 0 sent 16 each at neutral castle 9 (30), the middle of the map, landing in 2 and 3 turns. The first left it at 14 and neutral; on turn 143 enemy castle 7 (93, two turns away) sent 5, which landed on turn 144 just after our second 16 had taken it with 2. The enemy built castle 9 into the 128-stack that took castle 10 on turn 159.

**The right move:** Leave castle 9 alone while enemy castle 7 (93) is as close as we are, or pay for it from one castle with a margin against castle 7.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: `send 10->9 x16`, `send 0->9 x16` (and feeds the rest to castle 8). `neutralOption` prices the capture at the neutral's 30 plus `neutralExtra` 2 plus what enemy castles could spare, and `enemySpare` counts castle 7's 93 as reserved against our castle 4 (138), so the hold is 0; it lets the payers land on different turns.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: the same two sends to castle 9 (its hold rule is only in `smash`, not in `neutralOption`).

## 3. Turn 138: a neutral taken with 2 beside enemy castle 11

Castles 4, 0 and 10 sent 13, 17 and 2 at neutral castle 8 (30), and took it on turn 141 with 2. Enemy castle 11 (52) is two turns away; it sent 6 and retook it on turn 142. 32 soldiers lost for nothing.

**The right move:** Leave castle 8 neutral; keep the soldiers home.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: takes no neutral and feeds its castles into a stack at castle 6.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 138: `["send 0->10 x17", "send 2->10 x8", "send 4->10 x122"]`
- turn 142: `["send 0->10 x16", "send 2->10 x22", "send 4->10 x138", "send 6->10 x13"]`
- turn 157: `["send 0->10 x20", "send 2->10 x8", "send 4->10 x198", "send 6->10 x21"]`
