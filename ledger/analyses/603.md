# Game 603: the three main mistakes

Map claw; the hosted bot was punisher, playing as team 1, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 91.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 79: castle 3 left to fall by a few soldiers (decided the game)

The enemy had stacked castle 4 to 140 and sent 73 (speed 3) at castle 3, which held 64 and would hold about 68 at the landing in 2. Castle 0 had 44 (speed 2, 3 turns away) and castle 5 had 42. We sent 0->2 16 and 5->2 5 into castle 2; castle 3 fell on turn 80 and the enemy fed it to 81 and struck castle 0 and castle 5 from it.

**The right move:** Buy speed 3 at castle 0 so its help lands in 2 and send it to castle 3 (with castle 5 behind it); better, feed castle 3 from castle 0 on turn 77.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: speed 3 at castle 0, help>3 0:2, then feeds castle 3 with 0->3 22 and 5->3 12 and buys production at castle 5 (on turn 77 it sends 0->3 36).

## 2. Turn 84: castle 0 left to fall

Castle 3, now the enemy's, held 66, 2.8 from castle 0; castle 0 had sent 8 and 11 to castle 2 on turns 82 and 83 and held 29. On turn 84 the enemy sent 48 at it. Castle 5 (23) sent 17 to castle 3 and 5 to castle 2 instead; castle 0 fell on turn 86 with 37 in it.

**The right move:** Send castle 5's 23 to castle 0 (and keep castle 0's 19 home on turns 82 and 83).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: 5->0 23, castle 0 then holds 48.

## 3. Turn 71: feeding the fight for castle 2 while the enemy stacked castle 4

From turn 30 castle 2 (production 2) changed hands sixteen times, each side sending 4 to 14 at a time. On turn 71 castle 3 (40) sent 8 more into it; the enemy fed castle 4 (52) with 12 every three turns from castles 1 and 6, to 140 by turn 77.

**The right move:** Leave castle 2 and feed castle 3 (0->3 12), the castle facing castle 4.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: 0->3 12, nothing into castle 2.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 71: `["send 0->3 x12", "send 5->3 x18"]`
- turn 79: `["up 0 speed", "send 0->3 x2", "up 5 generation_amount", "send 0->3 x22", "send 5->3 x12"]`
- turn 84: `["send 5->0 x23"]`
