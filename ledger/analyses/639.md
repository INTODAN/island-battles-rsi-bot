# Game 639: the three main mistakes

Map delta; punisher played as team 1 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost on turn 251.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 185: castle 4 left to fall (decided the game)

The enemy's stack in castle 3 (766, speed 4) sent 489 to castle 4 (217), landing in 2. Castle 6 (478) was 2 away; castle 0 held 473. We sent nothing; castle 4 fell on turn 186 and the enemy had a second stack (456 by turn 240) beside castles 0 and 6.

**The right move:** Send castle 6's 265 or more into castle 4 (it then holds).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: `send 6->4 x265` (help>4 in 2), then feeds the rest of castle 6 to its stack.

## 2. Turn 240: two strikes that emptied castles 0 and 6

Castle 4 (456) had 356 landing in 1 and 651 more within 9. Castle 6 (600) sent 397 to castle 1 (8) and 25 to castle 7, keeping 182; castle 0 (615) sent 165 to castle 2 and 430 to castle 6, keeping 4. Castle 4 sent 212 to castle 6 (fell on turn 242, retaken, lost again on turn 244) and 153 to castle 0 (fell on turn 245).

**The right move:** No strike: gather castle 0 into castle 6 and hold there.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: `send 0->6 x615` (stack@6); no strike.

## 3. Turn 150: saving apart while the enemy stacks

Castle 3 (654, speed 4) was fed by four castles every few turns; castles 0 (333) and 6 (338) kept their soldiers apart and castle 4 (77) sat beside castle 3. Nothing changed until castle 3 broke castle 4 on turn 185.

**The right move:** Gather: feed castle 6 into castle 4 (or both into one castle) and buy speed.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids saving apart: `up 0 speed` twice, `send 4->0 x77`, `send 6->0 x338`. Its `stackOf` picks rear castle 0 (a tie on walk with castle 4, broken by id), so castle 4 is emptied beside castle 3; juggernaut_fix feeds castle 6's 338 into castle 4 instead.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 150: `["send 0->4 x333", "send 6->4 x338"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 185: `["send 6->4 x265", "send 0->4 x473", "send 6->4 x213"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 240: `["send 0->6 x615"]` (avoids the mistake)
