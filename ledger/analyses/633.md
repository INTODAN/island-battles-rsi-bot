# Game 633: the three main mistakes

Map delta; the hosted bot was fullfeed_unfreeze, playing as team 2, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 96.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 86: two stacks apart beside the enemy's 272 (decided the game)

The enemy had fed castle 5 to 272 (speed 3), 2 turns from our castle 7 (144). Castle 1 held 96, 5 turns from castle 7. We sent nothing; on turn 88 castle 5 sent 167 at castle 7, which fell on turn 89 with 7 left for the enemy, and castle 1 fell on turn 95.

**The right move:** Send castle 1's 96 into castle 7 (about 240 when the strike lands).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: 1->7 96.

## 2. Turn 51: castle 5 emptied to feed the far side

Castle 5 (17, production 4) faced the enemy's castle 2 (41). We sent 5->3 17 and 7->3 12 to castle 3 (10, production 1, the far side). On turn 54 castle 2 sent 54 at castle 5 (4 left); it fell on turn 59 with the enemy keeping 18, and the enemy fed it to 276 as its stack.

**The right move:** Keep castle 5's 17 and feed it with castle 7's 12: about 61 holds the 54.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: stack@3, 5->3 17, 7->3 12 and production at castle 3. Its `stackOf` picks the own castle nearest an enemy castle by walk; castles 3 and 5 are both 5.0 from one, and the tie goes to the lower id.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: its tie-break by distance to the other own castles picks castle 5 and it sends 7->5 12.

## 3. Turn 69: a stream of 9s into the enemy's stack

Castle 7 (89, speed 3) sent 9 at the enemy's castle 5 on turns 67, 69, 71, 73, 75 and 77, while castle 5 grew from 63 to 142: 54 soldiers lost and nothing taken.

**The right move:** Keep them home and feed castle 7 from castle 1 (and buy speed 4).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: up 7 speed and 1->7 28, nothing sent at castle 5.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 51: `["send 7->5 x12"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 69: `["send 1->7 x28"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 86: `["send 1->7 x96"]` (avoids the mistake)
