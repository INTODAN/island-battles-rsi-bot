# Game 587: the three main mistakes

Map estuary; our bot was juggernaut, playing as team 2, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; won on turn 114.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake. The champion played this game, and on each board it gives the commands it really sent.

## 1. Turn 17: a neutral centre castle taken with 2

The stack, castle 1 (27), struck neutral castle 5 (25, production 1), 6 turns away, with 27, and took it on turn 22 with 2, 9 turns from enemy castle 0 (24). Both sides then fed it with small groups until turn 53; the soldier counts stayed even until turn 71.

**The right move:** Keep the 27 and buy production 4 at castle 1 (30) on the next turn.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: `send 1->5 x27` (`smash>5 in 6 need 27`). `smash()` prices a neutral target at its soldiers plus `strikeExtra` (2), with no term for holding it.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: its hold term applies only to enemy-owned targets.

## 2. Turn 40: feeding the stack as it fell

The stack, castle 5, had 2, with the enemy's 12 and 6 landing within 3 turns and our 10 and 8 landing; it would fall. We fed it castle 1's 8 (keeping 4); castle 5 fell on turn 42 and changed hands every turn until turn 53, each side's groups of 8 landing in turn.

**The right move:** Keep castle 1's 8 home and retake castle 5 later with one group large enough.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: `send 1->5 x8` (`help>5 in 3`, no helper in time, then `feed>5`). `stackOf()` keeps the castle nearest the enemy as the stack even when it is about to fall, and `feedStack()` feeds it.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: no commands; its stack is castle 1, since a castle about to fall is never the stack.

## 3. Turn 64: a neutral strike that emptied the stack

The stack, castle 5 (34, production 3), struck neutral castle 7 (25, production 1), 8 turns away, with 27. It took it on turn 71 with 2; enemy castle 0 sent 37 at it, and castle 5 sent 23 and 9 to hold it (turns 72 to 73), leaving 12 to 15 beside enemy castle 2 (52). On turn 74 the enemy sent 86 at castle 5, which held by a few soldiers.

**The right move:** Keep the 27 in castle 5.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: `send 5->7 x27`, `send 1->5 x8` (`smash>7 in 8 need 27`), by the same rule in `smash()`.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: no hold term for neutral targets.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 17: `[]`
- turn 40: `[]`
- turn 64: `["up 5 generation_amount", "send 1->5 x8"]`
