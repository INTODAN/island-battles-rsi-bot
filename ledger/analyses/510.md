# Game 510: the three main mistakes

Map archipelago; the hosted bot was pounce, playing as team 2, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 89.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 77: an all-in strike that emptied every castle (decided the game)

The enemy stack in castle 8 held 85 (speed 4) with 36 landing; we held castle 1 (48), castle 5 (70) and castle 7 (62). We sent 1->8 47, 5->8 70 and 7->8 62, leaving 4 or 5 in each. Castle 8 fell on turn 81 with 24 left; the enemy's rear castles took castle 7 (turn 80), castle 5 (turn 82), castle 8 back (turn 85) and castle 1 (turn 88).

**The right move:** Gather castles 5 and 7 into castle 1 (or castle 5) and defend; the enemy's strike had just spent 34 on castle 3.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: 5->1 70 and 7->1 62, a stack of 180 in castle 1 (the fix sends nothing and keeps 205 at home).

## 2. Turn 75: five castles apart while the enemy stacked

The enemy had fed castle 8 to 105 (speed 3) with 66 more landing; we held castle 1 (40), castle 3 (17), castle 5 (62), castle 7 (54) and castle 11 (5), and sent nothing on turns 71 to 76. Castle 8 struck castle 11 (18) on turn 75 and castle 3 (34) on turn 76; both fell.

**The right move:** Feed one stack: castles 1, 3 and 5 into castle 7 (or castle 11), so the strikes meet one castle of 170.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: 1->7 40, 3->7 17, 5->7 62 (the fix feeds the same 119 into castle 11 and holds it).

## 3. Turn 54: a capture of neutral castle 8 that could not hold

Castle 8 was neutral with 30 (production 1), 2.1 from enemy castle 6 (49) and beside enemy castle 10 (26, with 67 landing). We sent 11->8 13 and 3->8 19, then on turn 57 another 52 (5->8 22, 3->8 24, 11->8 6). We took it on turn 60 with 29 and lost it on turn 63 to castle 6's 37: about 84 soldiers for nothing.

**The right move:** No capture of castle 8: feed castle 1's 95 and the rest into one stack and buy production.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: take>8 11:13 3:19 (it then feeds 95 into castle 9; on turn 57 it sends nothing more to castle 8).

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats (take>8 11:13 3:19). In `neutralOption` the hold counts `enemySpare`, which takes the enemy castle's stock now (castle 10's 67 landing not counted) and assumes it keeps back enough to fear our castle 1; castle 6 spent 62 on castle 8 regardless.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 54: `["send 1->9 x29", "send 7->9 x21", "send 11->9 x13"]`
- turn 75: `["send 1->11 x40", "send 3->11 x17", "send 5->11 x62"]`
- turn 77: `["send 3->8 x15"]`
