# Game 539: the three main mistakes

Map delta; our bot pounce played as team 2 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 231.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 119: 179 from castle 1 at castle 4, in the middle of three stocked enemy castles

Castle 4 (5, production 1) was the enemy's, 2 to 3 turns from enemy castles 6 (110), 2 (188) and 0 (99). Castle 1 (321) sent 179 at it, landing in 5. The enemy put 114 from castle 6 and 61 from castle 2 into castle 4 before it landed; the strike failed by a few soldiers, and castle 4 held 179 on turn 124. Castle 5 fell on turn 120 and castle 7 on turn 131, with no stock left to help them.

**The right move:** No strike into a castle three enemy stocks can reach first; keep castle 1's stock (or gather it in castle 3) and help castles 5 and 7.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: no strike; buys speed at castle 1 and sends 1->7 6 (help), and gathers 285 into castle 3 with speed 4 bought.

## 2. Turn 121: the failed strike fed with 130 more

On turn 121 the board showed enemy groups of 114 and 61 landing on castle 4 one and two turns before our 179. We sent 74 more from castle 1 and 56 from castle 3 after it. Both landed on a castle the enemy now held with about 180; castle 1 was down to 8 on turn 124 and castle 3 to 76. In all about 309 soldiers went into castle 4 for nothing, and the game turned there: 4 castles against 4 on turn 119, 2 against 6 from turn 140.

**The right move:** Send nothing more to castle 4; keep castles 1 and 3 home or gather them in one.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: no strike; feeds castle 7 with 1:114 and 3:120.

## 3. Turn 81: 5 or 6 soldiers at a time into castle 4

From turn 69 to 119 castle 3 sent 5 or 6 soldiers at enemy castle 4 every two or three turns, about 20 groups and 110 soldiers. Castle 4 held 1 to 8, and the enemy's own small groups into it met ours: none of them took it. Meanwhile castle 1 sat on 190 to 321.

**The right move:** Either one strike large enough to take and hold castle 4, or none; on turn 81 gather castle 1's 190 in castle 3 instead of dribbling.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: sends 7->5 9 (help for castle 5), buys speed at castle 3 and gathers 1->3 190.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 81: `["send 7->5 x9", "send 1->5 x112"]`
- turn 119: `["send 5->2 x3", "up 1 speed", "send 1->7 x6", "up 3 speed", "up 3 speed", "send 1->3 x285"]`
- turn 121: `["send 1->7 x114"]`
