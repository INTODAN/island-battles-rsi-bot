# Game 519: the three main mistakes

Map bight; the league bot was speedhelp_fullfeed, playing as team 1, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost on turn 225, down to castle 0 alone from turn 96 (about 580 against 2500 at the end).

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 82: castle 4 emptied into the stack beside a stocked enemy castle

Enemy castle 2 held 36 (production 4) with 56 more walking in; castle 4 held 12 and already had 12 walking to castle 3. We sent 0->3 20 and 4->3 12 (castle 4 left with 4) and kept feeding castle 3 from castle 4. On turn 85 castle 2 sent 50 at castle 4, which fell on turn 89 with 28; castle 0's 16 could not land in time to make up the 24 that had walked away.

**The right move:** Keep castle 4's soldiers home (and castle 0's 20 as its help): buy production at castle 3 only.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: stack@3, production at castle 3 and feed>3 0:20 4:12, exactly our move. `feedStack` sends a rear castle's whole safe stock (`safeOut`), which counts only groups already landing on it, not enemy castle 2's stock 4 turns away.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: buys production at castle 3 and sends nothing; its rear castles keep what an enemy castle that lands before the stack's help would take.

## 2. Turn 92: castle 3 sent away from the front as the enemy stacked beside it

Castle 4 had just fallen; castle 3 held 55 (production 4) with enemy castle 2 (35, 40 more landing) 5 away. Castle 3's 26 was already walking to castle 0 and we sent 3->0 41 more, leaving 18. On turn 93 castle 2 sent 40 at castle 3, which fell on turn 96 with 30; castle 0 then sat alone for 130 turns.

**The right move:** Keep castle 3's 55 home; it holds against castle 2's 40.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats, worse: stack@0, feed>0 3:55, emptying castle 3 completely.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: sends nothing; castle 3 keeps the reserve against castle 2, which lands before castle 0 could help.

## 3. Turn 8: a race for contested castle 3 instead of the safe castle 4

Neutral castles 3 (15) and 4 (18) were both free; castle 3 lies between the two homes, castle 4 on our side only. We sent 0->3 19, and the enemy 13; then 8, 11, 10 and 11 more over turns 10 to 21. Castle 3 fell to us on turn 22 with 4, changed hands five times by turn 49, and castle 4 was taken only on turn 38.

**The right move:** Take castle 4: 0->4 20 (6 turns, no enemy castle near).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: smash>4 in 6 with 20, and no trickle on turn 10.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 8: `["send 0->4 x20"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 82: `["send 0->3 x20", "send 4->3 x12"]` (still makes it)
- turn 92: `["send 3->0 x55"]` (still makes it)
