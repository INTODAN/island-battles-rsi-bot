# Game 556: the three main mistakes

Map claw; the hosted bot was juggernaut, playing as team 2, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost (its last castle fell on turn 63, the game ended on turn 67).

The game is game 553 mirrored (our castles 1, 6, 7 for its 0, 3, 5), move for move to turn 58. Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 49: a strike on castle 2 that could take it but not hold it (decided the game)

Our stack, castle 6 (26, speed 4), sent 19 at enemy castle 2 (11) while enemy castle 4 (13, with 22 more landing on it) stood 3 away and 69 enemy soldiers walked at our castle 7. Castle 2 fell to us on turn 50 with 2; we fed it about 60 from castles 1 and 6 against 23, 30 and 9 from the enemy, and it fell back on turn 58.

**The right move:** No strike: send castle 7 its 4 of help and keep the stack whole in castle 6.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: help>7 6:4 and smash>2 with 19. Its strike price counts only enemy castles whose help lands before the strike (`1+travel <= walk` in `smash`), not castle 4's stock and feeds that retake the capture.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: sends only 6->7 4 and keeps the 19.

## 2. Turn 47: speed 4 bought from the stack beside a fed enemy castle

Castle 6 (32, speed 3) bought speed 4 for 18, keeping 14, while enemy castle 4 (8) 3.2 away had 34 landing on it within 4 turns and castle 2 held 29.

**The right move:** Buy nothing: keep 32 in castle 6.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: up 6 speed and 7->6 8. `build` buys stack speed while the stack keeps its `guard`, which counts the enemy castles' stock but not the groups landing on them.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: only 7->6 8.

## 3. Turn 55: castle 6 emptied into castle 2 beside an enemy castle of 48

The stack had moved to castle 2 (14). Castle 6 (12) sent 12 to it, keeping 4, with enemy castle 4 (48) 3.2 away. The enemy sent 33 from castle 4 at castle 6 the same turn, plus 30 and 9 at castle 2: both fell on turn 58, and castles 1 and 7 on turns 60 and 63.

**The right move:** Send nothing from castle 6. The opponent strikes the castle whose garrison drops; with 12 home castle 6 would not have been the cheap target, and the 12 sent were lost in castle 2 anyway.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: feed>2 6:12. `feedStack` sends a rear castle's whole `safeOut` when no enemy group is yet bound for it, whatever the enemy castle beside it holds.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: sends nothing; a rear castle keeps what an enemy castle that lands before the stack's help could take.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 47: `["send 7->6 x8"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 49: `["send 7->1 x8"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 55: `["send 6->2 x12"]` (still makes it)
