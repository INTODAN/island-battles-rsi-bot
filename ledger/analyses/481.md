# Game 481: the three main mistakes

Map 13 castles, 2 teams; the hosted bot was speedhelp_landlate, playing as team 1; lost, eliminated on turn 139.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 55: a border castle fed away its stock

We sent 8->0 22, a 30-turn walk, leaving castle 8 with 8 beside enemy castle 9 (31, production 4, 5.8 away); castle 9 sent 34 on turn 57 and castle 8 fell on turn 61.

**The right move:** Send nothing from castle 8: its 30 grow to about 48 by the time 34 lands.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeated, another way: bought production at castle 8 with all of its 30, and fed a corner stack (castle 0) on 30-turn walks.

**Proposed fix (not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed):** A rear castle buys an upgrade only while it still holds what the enemy castles could spare against it, and the stack is the castle nearest the enemy with the shortest total distance to the others (castle 6 here, not the corner castle 0); juggernaut now sends only 7->6 8.

## 2. Turn 90: castle 7 given up

Castle 7 had 100 with 113 (in 3) and 74 (in 5) coming; castle 1 had 121 at speed 3, 4 turns away. We logged 'cannot hold' and sent 1->8 17 at the enemy stack; castle 7 fell on turn 94.

**The right move:** Send 1->7 70 or more: castle 7 survives the 113 and the 70 lands before the 74.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids the defence (1->7 66), but also fed its corner stack 1->0 55, 3->0 92 and 6->0 112, emptying castles next to enemy castles of 112 and 120.

**Proposed fix (not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed):** Feeding keeps a reserve against any enemy castle that lands on the feeder before the stack's help could, and the stack is central: juggernaut sends 1->7 66 and feeds only 0->6 53 and 3->6 92.

## 3. Turn 105: castle 3 given up while 320 sat home

Castle 3 had 152 with 204 (in 7) and 97 (in 9) coming; castle 6 had 172 (6 turns away) and castle 0 218. We sent nothing; castle 3 fell on turn 111 about 30 short.

**The right move:** Send 6->3 30 now and about 100 more from castle 6 or 0 before the 97 lands.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids the defence (6->3 25), but fed 6->0 147, leaving castle 6 empty beside castle 11 (180).

**Proposed fix (not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed):** With the stack at castle 6 it sends 6->3 25 and 0->6 53, and castle 6 keeps its soldiers.
