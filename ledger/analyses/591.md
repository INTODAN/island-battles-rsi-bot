# Game 591: the three main mistakes

Map delta; the league bot pounce_reach played as team 2 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; won, the opponent eliminated on turn 216.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 86: a strike at castle 0 across the map

Castle 3 (87) sent 54 at enemy castle 0 (4, production 4) 11 away, and 17 at castle 4 (9). Castle 0 grew and was fed, and neither was taken; castle 5 (12, 43 landing in 4) fell on turn 94.

**The right move:** No strike so far away: keep castle 3's soldiers as the stack beside castles 4 and 6, and feed it from castle 1 (107).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: nothing at castle 0; it smashes castle 4 with 48, buys speed at castle 3 and feeds it 1->3 107.

## 2. Turn 142: 53 at a production-1 castle that did not fall

Castle 3 (61) sent 53 at enemy castle 4 (23, production 1, beside castle 6). It was not taken. Castle 5 (5) was being struck with 17 + 17 the same turns.

**The right move:** No strike: castle 7's 9 to castle 5 and castle 1's 105 into the stack at castle 3.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: help 7->5 9, speed twice at castle 3 and 1->3 105.

## 3. Turn 156: everything thrown at the enemy's stack

Enemy castle 2 held 188 with 28 more landing, and castle 6 (52) and castle 0 (18) beside it. We sent 161 from castle 1, 61 from castle 3, 57 from castle 7 and 45 from castle 5: 324, from different distances. The enemy added 75 more, castle 2 grew to 274, and our groups landed in pieces on turns 163 and 164; it held with 26. Our soldiers fell from 231 (turn 150) to 67 (turn 160).

**The right move:** Stack on castle 3 or 5 and strike only when one landing beats castle 2 with its feeds.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: speed twice at castle 3 and stacks there, 1->3 161, 5->3 45, 7->3 57.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 86: `["send 1->5 x49", "send 3->5 x17", "send 7->5 x22"]`
- turn 142: `["send 7->5 x10", "send 1->5 x105"]`
- turn 156: `["send 1->5 x161", "send 7->5 x57"]`
