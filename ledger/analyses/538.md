# Game 538: the three main mistakes

Map gyre; the league bot was batchfront, playing as team 2, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 58.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 40: the stack at castle 5 sent at neutral castle 8 beside enemy castle 2 (decided the game)

Castle 5 held 48 (production 4); enemy castle 2 held 34 with 28 more landing, enemy castle 4 23. We sent 5->8 40 at neutral castle 8 (25), keeping 12. Castle 2 bought speed and sent 41 at castle 5 on turn 41: castle 5 fell on turn 44 (24 to 9), and castle 8, taken on turn 43 with 15, fell the next turn. From there the enemy rolled up castles 1, 3 and 7.

**The right move:** Keep the stack home: castle 5 with 48 (and 1->5 9) holds against castle 2.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats, worse: stack@3, feed>3 1:9 5:48, emptying castle 5 completely beside castles 2 and 4.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: stack@5, smash>8 in 4 with 27 (castle 5 kept about 21) and 1->5 9. `guard` counts only enemy castles that land within `guardWalk` (4) at their current speed; castle 2 (speed 1, 8 turns) is left out though one speed upgrade brings it to 4, and the neutral target needs no hold in `smash`.

## 2. Turn 48: home castle 1 emptied beside enemy castle 5

Enemy castle 5 (taken on turn 44, 21) was 2 turns from castle 1 (13, production 4). We sent 1->3 12, leaving 5; castle 5 sent 21 at castle 1 on turn 49 and castle 1 fell on turn 51.

**The right move:** Keep castle 1's 13 home and feed it (7->1 13).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats, worse: stack@3, feed>3 1:13 7:13, emptying castle 1. `feedStack` sends a rear castle's `safeOut`, which counts only groups already landing on it.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: stack@1, feeds 7->1 13; castle 1 keeps what castle 5 could take before help.

## 3. Turn 28: a capture of neutral castle 3 that could not hold

Castle 3 was neutral with 12, enemy castle 4 (12, production 3) 3.6 away. We sent 1->3 12 and 5->3 7; castle 3 fell to us on turn 32 with 7 and enemy castle 4 retook it on turn 36. It changed hands five more times; about 120 soldiers went into it.

**The right move:** No capture yet: gather at castle 5 (1->5 13) and buy production.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: smash>3 in 5 with 14 from castle 5, and 1->5 13.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats the same: in `smash`, a neutral target needs only its soldiers plus `strikeExtra`; the hold against nearby enemy castles applies only to enemy-owned targets.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 28: `["send 1->3 x13", "send 5->3 x21"]` (still makes it)
- turn 40: `["send 1->5 x9"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 48: `["send 7->1 x13"]` (avoids the mistake)
