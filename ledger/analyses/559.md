# Game 559: the three main mistakes

Map claw; the league bot was pounce_reach, playing as team 2, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 89.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 80: home emptied into a strike at the enemy's stack

Enemy castle 4 held 112 (speed 4) and had just sent 76 at our castle 6 (2), with 71 more behind. Castle 1 (111) sent 111 and castle 7 36 at castle 4, 4 turns away; castle 4 held (133 when they landed). Castle 6's captors sent 16 at castle 1 (4), which fell on turn 82; castle 7 fell on turn 88.

**The right move:** Give castle 6 up and gather at castle 7 (1->7 111): it outnumbers everything near it.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids the strike, but feeds its stack at castle 6 (1->6 111, 7->6 61) although castle 6 falls the next turn, a turn before they land.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: skips castle 6 as a stack because it falls first, and feeds 1->7 111.

## 2. Turn 70: the stock kept at home while the front fell

Castle 1 held 112; castle 6 (23) had the enemy's 36 landing in 2 and the enemy gathered 69 more at castle 4. We sent 6 to castle 6 and nothing more; castle 6 fell on turn 77 with 21 in it.

**The right move:** Feed castle 6 with castle 1's stock (as juggernaut does).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: buys speed at castle 1 and sends 1->6 92 and 7->6 21.

## 3. Turn 42: a second small wave at a fed castle

Castle 1 sent 25 at castle 4 (7), after 17 on turn 40, keeping 4; the enemy's 8 and 15 were landing on castle 4 in 3 and 5 and it was fed by three castles. Both waves were lost; castle 4 became the enemy's stack.

**The right move:** No strike: feed the front castle 6 (1->6 25).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: feeds 1->6 25.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 42: `["send 1->6 x25"]`
- turn 70: `["up 1 speed", "send 1->6 x6", "send 1->6 x86", "send 7->6 x21"]`
- turn 80: `["send 1->7 x111"]`
