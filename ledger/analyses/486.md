# Game 486: the three main mistakes

Map claw; the hosted bot was juggernaut, playing as team 1, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 67.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 49: a strike on castle 2 that could take it but not hold it (decided the game)

Our stack, castle 3 (26, speed 4), sent 19 at enemy castle 2 (11) while enemy castle 4 (13, with 22 more landing on it) stood 3 away and 69 enemy soldiers walked at our castle 5. Castle 2 fell to us on turn 50 with 2; the enemy sent 23, 22, 16 and more at it, we fed it about 58 from castles 0 and 3 (most lost in clashes), and it fell back on turn 59. Castle 3, drained to 11 to 15, fell on turn 60.

**The right move:** No strike: send castle 5 its 4 of help and keep the stack whole in castle 3.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: help>5 3:4 and smash>2 with 19. Its strike price counts only enemy castles whose help lands before the strike (`1+travel <= walk` in `smash`), not castle 4's stock and feeds that retake the capture right after.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: sends only 3->5 4; the capture's price now counts castle 4's stock, feeds and growth, so it keeps the 19.

## 2. Turn 47: speed 4 bought from the stack beside a fed enemy castle

Castle 3 (32, speed 3) bought speed 4 for 18, keeping 14, while enemy castle 4 (8) 3.2 away had 34 landing on it within 4 turns and castle 2 held 29. After it castle 3 never held more than about 26, and the stack it bought speed for was the castle the enemy took on turn 60.

**The right move:** Buy nothing: keep 32 in castle 3 (and keep feeding it).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: up 3 speed and 5->3 8. `build` buys stack speed while the stack keeps its `guard`, and the guard counts the enemy castles' stock only, not the 34 landing on castle 4.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: only 5->3 8; its guard counts the groups landing on the enemy castles, so the purchase doesn't fit.

## 3. Turn 60: 17 sent to castle 3 the turn before it fell

Castle 3 held 15 and an enemy group of 30 landed on it the next turn. Castles 0 (8) and 5 (9) sent everything to it; it fell anyway, the 17 walked into the enemy's castle and were lost, and on turn 61 the enemy sent 33 at castle 0 and 41 at castle 5, each left with 4; both fell on turn 66.

**The right move:** Send nothing: castles 0 and 5 keep their 17 against the strikes that came next turn.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: 0->3 8 and 5->3 9. `stackOf` names the castle nearest the enemy as the stack even when its landings take it next turn, and `feedStack` empties the rear into it.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: skips a castle about to fall as the stack, names castle 5 and sends only 0->5 8.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 47: `["send 5->3 x8"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 49: `["send 5->0 x8"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 60: `["send 0->5 x8"]` (still makes it)
