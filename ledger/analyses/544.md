# Game 544: the three main mistakes

Map gyre; the hosted bot was landgrab, playing as team 1, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 79.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 56: castle 2 left nearly empty beside the enemy's castle 9

Castle 2 (production 3) held 4, two turns from enemy castle 9 (40, with 58 landing). Castle 6 had 23 and castle 0 had 8 (18 more landing on castle 4). We sent nothing; castle 9 sent 14 and took castle 2 on turn 57. The enemy fed it to 94 by turn 69 and struck castles 4 and 0 from it.

**The right move:** Feed castle 2 (6->2 23, 0->2 8) so 14 cannot take it.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: feeds its stack at castle 2 with 0->2 8 and 6->2 23.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: sends nothing (44 home). Its new rear-castle reserve makes castle 6 keep what castle 9 could land on it (castle 9 reaches castle 6 in 5, the stack's help in 6), so the stack is left at 4.

## 2. Turn 28: a long fight for a production-1 castle

Castle 9 was neutral with 25 (production 1), far from our castles. We sent 2->9 10, 6->9 17 and 0->9 6, then 9, 16 and 12 more by turn 34: about 70 soldiers. The enemy took it on turn 36 with 8, we took it back on turn 37 with 3, and the enemy took it for good on turn 48 and made it a stack.

**The right move:** No capture: buy production (castle 4) and keep the soldiers home.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: up 4 production, nothing sent.

## 3. Turn 70: castle 4's soldiers kept in a castle that could not hold

The enemy sent 121 at castle 0 (32, lands in 6) and 46 at castle 4 (17, lands in 2), then 23 more at castle 0. We sent 6->0 66 and 0->4 1; castle 4 fell on turn 71 with 21 in it, castle 0 held the 121 with 1 left on turn 76 and fell on turn 77.

**The right move:** Send castle 4's 17 to castle 0 (3 turns) as well as castle 6's 66, and nothing to castle 4: castle 0 then holds the 23 behind the wave.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: 6->0 66, 0->4 1 and 6->4 13.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: 6->0 66 and 0->4 1. `defend()` helps each falling castle with whatever arrives in time even when that cannot save it, and never moves a castle that will fall into one that can hold.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 28: `["up 4 generation_amount"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 56: `["send 0->2 x8", "send 6->2 x23"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 70: `["send 4->0 x17", "up 6 speed"]` (still makes it)
