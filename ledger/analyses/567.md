# Game 567: the three main mistakes

Map archipelago; the hosted bot was juggernaut, playing as team 2, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost (its last castle fell on turn 80).

The game is game 564 mirrored up to turn 58. Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 47: the stack spent on the neutral centre castle (decided the game)

Castle 7, the stack (39), sent 32 at neutral castle 9 (30, production 1), 3 turns away, keeping 7, with enemy castle 10 (42) 2.1 from castle 9. It took castle 9 on turn 49 with 2. The same turn the enemy sent 85 at our castle 11 (4), which fell on turn 52; castle 9, fed about 60 more, fell on turn 56.

**The right move:** No strike: keep the 39 in castle 7.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: smash>9 with 32 and feeds 1->7 8, 3->7 8. A neutral target is priced at its soldiers plus `strikeExtra`; enemy castle 10's 42 beside it isn't counted.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats an equivalent: no strike, but its `stackOf` tie-break names castle 11 the stack and feeds it 7:16, 1:8, 3:8, about 36 into the castle the enemy struck with 85 that turn.

## 2. Turn 61: castle 1 emptied into castle 3 beside an enemy castle of 38

Castle 1 (47, speed 3) sent all 47 to castle 3 (34), the new stack, with enemy castle 11 (38) 2.1 away. The enemy bought speed 3 at castle 11 and sent 27 at castle 1, which fell on turn 63; castle 3, our last castle with 80 to 132, never struck and fell on turn 80.

**The right move:** Send nothing: castle 1 keeps 47 against castle 11's 38.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: feed>3 1:47. `feedStack` gives a rear castle's whole `safeOut` when no enemy group is yet bound for it.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: feed>3 1:47. Its rear reserve counts only enemy castles that land before the stack's help (castle 11 in 3 turns, castle 3's help in 2), and the enemy bought speed to land first.

## 3. Turn 58: castle 7 fed the stack beside an enemy castle of 80

Castle 7 (12, speed 3) sent its 12 to castle 1, keeping 0, with enemy castle 10 (80) 3.6 away. The enemy sent 29 at it the same turn and took it on turn 60.

**The right move:** Keep the 12 home (and let castle 3's 50 cover it).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: feed>1 5:8 7:12. `feedStack` gives a rear castle's whole `safeOut` when no enemy group is yet bound for it.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: feed>3 7:12. Castle 10 lands on castle 7 in 4 turns, no sooner than castle 3's help, so it keeps no reserve.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 47: `["send 1->11 x8", "send 3->11 x8", "send 7->11 x16"]` (still makes it)
- turn 58: `["send 5->3 x8", "send 7->3 x12"]` (still makes it)
- turn 61: `["send 1->3 x47"]` (still makes it)
