# Game 499: the three main mistakes

Map star-of-david-duo; the league bot pounce played as team 1 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 133.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 101: three stocks kept apart while the enemy stacked one

Castles 1, 3 and 8 held 164, 145 and 131, each in its own castle, and the front castle 6 held 17. The enemy fed castle 0 from every rear castle (180 at turn 101, 277 at turn 106) and from it took castle 6 on turn 109, then castle 7 on turn 125; our 440 never struck and were lost castle by castle.

**The right move:** Feed one stack at the front (castle 6) from castles 1, 3 and 8, so the enemy stack cannot pick off one castle at a time.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: help 7->6 19, then stack at 6 fed by 1->6 164, 3->6 145, 7->6 10, 8->6 131.

## 2. Turn 111: more soldiers thrown at a retake that could not hold

Castle 6 had fallen on turn 109 (11 in it), but the enemy sent 20, 12, 12, 12 and 17 more into it and castle 0 (120) sat 5 away. On turn 110 castle 3 sent 66, on turn 111 another 39 and on turn 112 another 34: 139 soldiers, castle 3 down from 181 to 54, and castle 6 stayed the enemy's.

**The right move:** Send nothing more at castle 6; keep castle 3's 119 home (or join the stack) and hold castle 7.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: no send at castle 6; it buys speed twice at castle 3 and stacks there (1->3 84, 8->3 171).

## 3. Turn 123: castle 8 left half full beside two enemy stacks

Castle 1 (17) had 95 + 66 + 21 + 9 landing in 5 to 7. Castle 8 (219) sent 126 to it and kept 97, 6 from enemy castle 9 (157) and castle 4 (90). The help held castle 1 for 9 more turns, but castle 8 fell on turn 128 to 55 + 78 with its 84.

**The right move:** The same 126 to castle 1, and castle 8's other 93 into a stack (castle 3 or 7) rather than left where two enemy castles reach it.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: the same help 8->1 126, and 8->3 93 into its stack at castle 3 (speed bought there), so castle 8 is empty and nothing is lost in it.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 101: `["send 7->6 x29", "send 1->6 x31", "send 3->6 x145"]`
- turn 111: `["send 1->7 x84", "send 3->7 x119", "send 8->7 x171"]`
- turn 123: `["send 8->1 x126"]`
