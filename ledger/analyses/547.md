# Game 547: the three main mistakes

Map estuary; our bot pounce, playing as team 2, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 125.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 47: a neutral taken beside an enemy castle about to be filled

Castle 5 (neutral, 25) stood 2 map units from the enemy's castle 4 (6), onto which groups of 40 and 26 were landing in 2 turns. Castle 1 (48) sent 29 at castle 5, which it took on turn 49 with 4. Castle 4 sent 16 and castle 0 12 the same turn, and castle 5 fell on turn 53. On turn 54 the enemy struck castle 1 with 99.

**The right move:** No capture: keep castle 1's 48 and feed castle 3 into it.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: smash>5 in 3 with 27, buys speed at castle 1 and production at castle 7, sends 3->1 10. In `smash` a neutral target is priced at its own soldiers plus `strikeExtra` only; the enemy castles that could retake it are counted only when the target is the enemy's.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats the same commands: its hold-the-capture price sits in the same `owner != neutral` branch, so a neutral beside an enemy castle is still taken thin.

## 2. Turn 106: a strike into the enemy's stack

The enemy's castle 4 held 122 (speed 3) 2 map units from its castle 5 (66, with 22 more landing). Castle 1 (112) sent 100 at castle 5, keeping 12. It landed and left castle 5 with 4, but castle 4 and the rear refilled it (24, 23, 24, 30, 18, 22, 53 over turns 108 to 110). On turn 114 the enemy sent 129 at castle 1.

**The right move:** No strike: gather castle 3's 162 into castle 1 and hold.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: send 3->1 162.

## 3. Turn 109: a second strike at the same castle

Castle 5 (the enemy's, 4, 24 landing in 1) was again full of reach for castle 4 (103), castle 0 (64), castle 2 (53) and castle 6 (40). Castle 3 (174) sent 92 at it, keeping 86, while castle 1 held 12. The strike took nothing; castle 1 fell on turn 122 and castle 3 on turn 124.

**The right move:** No strike: send castle 3's 174 into castle 1.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: send 3->1 174.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 47: `["up 7 generation_amount", "send 3->1 x10"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 106: `["send 7->1 x61", "send 3->1 x162"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 109: `["send 3->1 x174"]` (avoids the mistake)
