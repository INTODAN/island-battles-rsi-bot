# Game 597: the three main mistakes

Map estuary; the league bot was pounce_reach, playing as team 2, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 125.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 106: home castle 1 emptied into a strike on castle 5 (decided the game)

Castle 1 held 112 and castle 3 162; enemy castle 5 held 66 with 22 landing, and enemy castle 4 (122) stood 4.5 from castle 1. We sent 1->5 100, keeping 16, then 12 more on turns 108 and 109 and 3->5 92 on turn 109; castle 5 was never taken. On turn 114 the enemy sent 129 at castle 1, which fell on turn 122 despite castle 3's feeds; castle 3 fell on turn 124 and castle 7 had fallen on turn 108.

**The right move:** No strike: gather castle 3's 162 into castle 1 (about 275 there).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: stack@1, feed>1 3:162.

## 2. Turn 35: a strike that softened neutral castle 4 for the enemy

Castle 4 was neutral with 25 in the middle of the map. We sent 1->4 20 and 3->4 7 (castle 1 left with 4); they wore castle 4 down to about 0, and the enemy's 12 from castle 2 took it on turn 45 with 5. The enemy fed it to 76 by turn 84 and struck from it for the rest of the game.

**The right move:** Leave castle 4 alone until one group can take and hold it; keep castle 1's 20 home.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids castle 4: smash>6 in 8 with 16 from castle 1, at enemy castle 6 (6, production 1).

## 3. Turn 13: a 10-turn walk to take castle 7, held three turns

Castle 7 was neutral with 25; enemy castle 0 (12, production 3) 7 away. We sent 1->7 22 and 3->7 5; castle 7 fell to us on turn 25 with 2, and the enemy's 13 from castle 0 took it on turn 28. It changed hands twice more by turn 34, and the enemy kept it.

**The right move:** No take yet: buy production at castle 1 and take castle 7 later with enough to hold.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: take>7 with 1->7 22 and 3->7 5, exactly our move.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats the same take. `neutralOption` holds against each enemy castle's spare now less the target's growth on the enemy's way; castle 0's growth (3 a turn) during our 10-turn walk is not counted.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save).
