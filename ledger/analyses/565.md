# Game 565: the three main mistakes

Map star-of-david-duo; our bot pounce_reach, playing as team 2, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 128.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 49: the centre struck in two landings 6 turns apart

Neutral castle 12 (40, production 2) is the map's centre, 6 turns from both our castle 10 and the enemy's castle 7. We sent 10->12 36 (lands in 6) and 9->12 6 (speed 1, lands in 12). The 36 left it at 4; the enemy sent 3 + 8 and 4 + 7 and took it on turn 55. The fight for it lasted to turn 64 and the enemy then fed it to 340 and struck castles 9 and 11 from it.

**The right move:** No strike until one landing can take it with a margin over what castle 7 can send; keep castle 10's 36.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: take>12 with 10->12 36 and 9->12 6. `neutralOption` adds up the senders' spares and prices the target at the slowest sender's walk, but the groups land apart, and the first one meets the 40 alone.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats the same sends (neutral captures are unchanged).

## 2. Turn 106: castle 9 helped with exactly what it needed

Castle 9 held 21 with 117 from the enemy's castle 8 landing in 5. Castle 2 (134, speed 3) sent 77, exactly enough; castle 12 added 4 + 4 on turn 108 and castle 9 fell on turn 110 with 114 inside. The enemy then stacked 454 in it.

**The right move:** Send castle 2's whole 134 (it lands in 4), or 77 plus a margin.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids, by its stack: the same help (2->9 77, 10->5 13, 10->11 8) and then, castle 9 being its stack, feeds it 2->9 57 and 10->9 116.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: the same exact help, and the stack is castle 10 (ties by distance to the other own castles), so the 57 go to castle 10 and castle 9 keeps the exact count. The help in `defend` sends what the landings need with no margin for groups sent later.

## 3. Turn 110: castle 10 emptied to help castle 5

The enemy's castle 12 held 200 at speed 4, 3 turns from our castle 10 (104). Castle 5 (9) had 56 + 48 landing. Castle 10 sent 48 to castle 5 and kept 60; castle 12 sent 82 on turn 111 and took castle 10 on turn 113. Castle 5 fell on turn 115 anyway.

**The right move:** Keep castle 10's 104 and send castle 2's 73 into it; castle 5 could not be held.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats, worse: help 10->5 48, then feeds castle 5 (its stack) 2->5 73 and 10->5 56, leaving castle 10 empty beside the 200. `defend`'s helpers may spend their own reserve on the castle they help.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: the same help 10->5 48, leaving 56 in castle 10 against the 82 that lands in 3; it feeds 2->10 73, which lands a turn later.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 49: `["send 10->9 x36"]`
- turn 106: `["up 10 speed", "send 10->5 x13", "send 2->9 x134", "send 10->9 x154", "send 11->0 x13"]`
- turn 110: `["send 10->5 x48", "send 9->8 x59", "send 2->10 x73"]`
