# Game 619: the three main mistakes

Map bight; our bot punisher, playing as team 1, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 146.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 21: one more drip into the race for castle 3

Neutral castle 3 (15) lies as far from our castle 0 as from the enemy's castle 1. Since turn 8 both sides had sent groups of 8 to 19 at it (48 of ours, 44 of the enemy's on the way). On turn 21 castle 0 sent 11 more, keeping 4. We took castle 3 on turn 22 with 4, the enemy's next group took it on turn 26, and the castle changed hands fourteen times up to turn 112, feeding most of both sides' production into it.

**The right move:** Send nothing more into a race the enemy matches; keep castle 0's 12 and take castle 4 (neutral, 18, on our side).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: sends nothing.

## 2. Turn 118: a strike at castle 2 that could not take it

The enemy had just taken castle 3 (3 left, with 85 of its own landing). Castle 4 (39) sent 33 at the enemy's castle 2 (20, with 12 landing), keeping 10 beside castle 3. The strike took nothing.

**The right move:** Keep castle 4's 39 and feed castle 0 into it.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: buys speed 3 at castle 4 and sends 0->4 8 (castle 4 keeps about 19 after the upgrade).

## 3. Turn 126: castle 4 emptied beside castle 3's 67

Castle 3 held 67 with 72 more landing, 5 map units from castle 4 (19). Castle 4 sent 18 at castle 2 (3) and kept 1. Castle 3 sent 51 on turn 128 and 23 on turn 131, and took castle 4 on turn 134; castle 0, alone, fell on turn 145.

**The right move:** Keep castle 4's 19 and feed castle 0's 12 into it.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: sends 0->4 12.
