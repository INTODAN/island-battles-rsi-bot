# Game 522: the three main mistakes

Map bight; the hosted bot was pounce, playing as team 2 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 189.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 32: neutral castle 3 taken with a margin of 2, and the castle count lost for good

Castle 3 (neutral, 15) is 8 turns from our castle 1 and 8 turns from the enemy's castle 0. We sent 1->3 17. It took castle 3 on turn 39 with 2; the opponent's 7 from castle 0, sent on turn 34, took it on turn 41. From then on we held 2 castles to the opponent's 4, and production 8 to its 13 to 16, for the rest of the game.

**The right move:** No take at 2 over the neutral: keep the 17 at castle 1 (or send 1->3 26, castle 1's 26 whole, which holds against the 7).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: the same take>3 1:17 (and feeds castle 5 with castle 1's 9).

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats it too: `neutralOption` takes a neutral that no enemy castle reaches strictly sooner (a tie counts as ours) at its count plus `neutralExtra` (2); castle 0's 4 adds nothing to the hold once the capture's growth over its 8-turn walk is taken off.

## 2. Turn 14: neutral castle 2 taken with a margin of 3

Castle 2 (neutral, 15) is 11 turns from castle 5 and 16 from the enemy's castle 0 (17, production 3). We sent 5->2 2 and 1->2 16. They took castle 2 on turn 28 with 3; the 15 that castle 0 sent the next turn (turn 15) took it on turn 30, and castle 2 became the opponent's hoard (1,737 by turn 178).

**The right move:** No take: keep the 18 home and buy production; castle 2 at 15 cost the opponent as much as it would have cost us.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: the same take>2 (5->2 2, 1->2 16).

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats it too: the same `neutralOption` price, its count plus 2 plus castle 0's spare less the capture's growth over castle 0's 16-turn walk (17 - 16 = 1); it doesn't count that castle 0 grows 3 a turn meanwhile.

## 3. Turn 182: castle 1's last 118 sent into castle 5, which fell anyway

The opponent struck castle 5 (903) with 910, 132 and 138 from castle 2, landing in 2, 3 and 4. With our 130 already on the way, castle 5 fell by about 13 even with castle 1's 118 too. We sent 1->5 118 (and 4 on turn 183), leaving castle 1 with 4; castle 5 fell on turn 187 and castle 2's 98 took castle 1 on turn 188, ending the game.

**The right move:** Keep castle 1's 118 home: castle 5 could no longer be held, and castle 1 with 118 and growing would not have fallen to 98.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: help>5 in 4 1:118.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats it too: `defend()` sends what the helpers that land in time have even when it is less than the castle lacks, so a castle that falls anyway still draws in the last other castle's soldiers.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 14: `[]`
- turn 32: `["send 1->5 x26"]`
- turn 182: `["send 1->5 x118"]`
