# Game 632: the three main mistakes

Map delta; the league bot was batchfront_keep, playing as team 2, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 112.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 60: castle 5 emptied into a strike

Castle 5 (28, production 4) sent 27 at castle 3 (6), keeping 5, with enemy castle 2 (11, 16 more landing) 5 away. Castle 2 sent 30 the next turn and took castle 5 on turn 64; it became the enemy's stack, which grew to 739.

**The right move:** Keep castle 5's 28 and feed it (1->5 9, 7->5 9).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: sends 5->1 28 and 7->1 9 to a stack at castle 1, the far corner. `stackOf` picks the castle nearest an enemy by walk with ties by id: castle 1 (5.4 from castle 3) and castle 5 (5.0 from castle 2) are both 11 turns at speed 1, and castle 1 wins on id; `feedStack` then empties castle 5.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: breaks the tie by the shortest distance to the other own castles and feeds castle 5 (1->5 9, 7->5 9).

## 2. Turn 71: castle 3 left to fall

Castle 3 (14) had the enemy's 17 and 33 landing in 5 and 6. Castle 1 (13) sent 12 to castle 7 instead; castle 3 fell on turn 76 with 17 in it.

**The right move:** Help castle 3 (1->3 13).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: sends 1->3 13 (`defend`).

## 3. Turn 106: castle 1 left to fall beside castle 7's 309

The enemy's 30 was landing on castle 1 (9) in 4; castle 7 held 309, 4.8 away. Castle 1 had fed castle 7 12 every third turn, keeping 5, and got no help; it fell on turn 109, and castle 5's 351 took castle 7 (341) on turn 111.

**The right move:** Help castle 1 from castle 7 (it can buy speed 3 and land in time).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: buys speed at castle 7 and sends 7->1 6, enough by its count.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 60: `["send 1->5 x9", "send 7->5 x9"]`
- turn 71: `["send 1->3 x13"]`
- turn 106: `["up 7 speed", "send 7->1 x6"]`
