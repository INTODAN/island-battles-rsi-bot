# Game 616: the three main mistakes

Map archipelago; the hosted bot was punisher, playing as team 2, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 92.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 85: castle 3 left to fall (decided the game)

The enemy sent 79 from castle 7 (lands in 1) and 125 from castle 5 (lands in 3) at castle 3 (108). Castle 1 held 134, 1.8 away (2 turns). We sent nothing; castle 3 fell on turn 87 and the enemy's 168 took castle 1 on turn 91.

**The right move:** Send castle 1's 134 to castle 3: it lands before the 125 and holds.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: help>3 1:85 plus feed 1:49, all 134.

## 2. Turn 72: castle 7 left to fall

The enemy sent 80 + 11 (land in 3) and 43 + 16 (land in 4) at castle 7 (52). Castle 3 (56) was 2 turns away and castle 1 (82) 4. We sent nothing; castle 7 fell on turn 74 with 27 left for the enemy, which fed it to 238 and struck castles 3 and 1 from it.

**The right move:** Send castle 3's 56 and castle 1's 82 to castle 7: 52 + 138 + growth holds 150.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids on this turn: 3->7 28, enough for the first wave of 91 (the 59 behind it is left for later turns); it keeps castle 1 as its stack (the fix sends 3->7 28 and gathers castle 1 into castle 3).

## 3. Turn 47: castle 11 lost by two soldiers

The enemy sent 75 at castle 11 (10): 29 landing in 4, 8 in 5, 38 in 6. Castle 7 had 42; we bought it speed 2 and sent 32 (4 turns), and castles 5, 3 and 1 sent 10. Castle 11 fell on turn 53 with the enemy keeping 3.

**The right move:** Send all of castle 7 (42, with speed) and castle 3's 11 to castle 11.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats, worse: help>11 1:4, and it feeds castle 7 instead. `defend()` sets the help needed from the first landing that takes the castle (the 29 in 4) and ignores the 46 landing after it.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: the same help of 4, then feeds castle 11 with 39, of which about 31 land before the last wave; the same rule in `defend()`.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 47: `["send 1->11 x4", "send 3->11 x11", "up 5 speed", "send 5->11 x6", "up 7 speed", "send 7->11 x21"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 72: `["send 3->7 x56", "up 1 speed", "send 1->7 x27", "send 1->3 x35"]` (still makes it)
- turn 85: `["send 1->3 x85", "send 1->3 x49"]` (avoids the mistake)
