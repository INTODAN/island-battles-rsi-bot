# Game 568: the three main mistakes

Map archipelago; our bot burstguard played as team 2 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost on turn 75.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 51: a neutral capture that emptied castle 11

Castle 11 (8) and castle 3 (24) faced five enemy castles holding 167, 2 to 4 turns away. We sent 11->9 8 and 3->9 24 at neutral castle 9 (30, production 1). On turn 52 the enemy sent 121 at castle 11; castle 9 was taken by the enemy on turn 54 with our 32 spent on it.

**The right move:** No capture: keep castles 11 and 3 stocked, feed castle 11 from castle 1 (83).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: take>9 with 11->9 8 and 3->9 24 (its senders may spare what threat allows, and threat counts only enemySpare, the stock above a reserve the enemy is assumed to keep against our castles; the enemy kept none).

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: the same capture.

## 2. Turn 54: castle 11 lost by 3

121 enemy landed on castle 11 (12) in 3 to 5 turns. Castle 1 sent 83, but castle 5 bought burst on turns 53 and 54 and kept its 20, 3 turns away. Castle 11 fell on turn 58 by 3, with 83 inside.

**The right move:** 1->11 83 and 5->11 20, no burst.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: exactly 1->11 83 and 5->11 20.

## 3. Turn 62: a retake of castle 11 against 75 landing

Castle 11 (enemy, 3) had 15 landing in 1 and 60 in 4 to 5. We sent 1->11 10, 5->11 25, 3->11 4; they died there and castles 1 and 3, left with 0 to 7, fell on turns 69 and 70.

**The right move:** No retake: gather (5->1 32).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: 5->1 32.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 51: `["send 1->8 x70", "send 3->8 x11", "send 7->8 x18", "send 11->8 x8"]` (still makes it)
- turn 54: `["send 1->11 x83", "send 1->11 x5", "send 3->11 x1", "send 5->11 x20"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 62: `["send 7->8 x13", "send 1->3 x10", "send 5->3 x25"]` (avoids the mistake)
