# Game 643: the three main mistakes

Map gyre; the hosted bot was fullfeed_unfreeze, playing as team 2 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 161.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 80: castle 5 left to fall to 197 while 300 sat beside it

We led 363 soldiers to 171. The opponent emptied castles 4 and 2 into 121 and 76 at castle 5 (99, production 4), landing in 3. Castles 1 (66) and 3 (78) were 3 turns away, castles 7 (66) and 8 (78) 4. We sent 11 from each of the four (44, half of it too late); castle 5 fell on turn 82 with 64 enemy soldiers left, and the opponent's production passed ours (24 to 16) for the rest of the game.

**The right move:** Send castle 1's 66 and castle 3's 78 to castle 5 (they land in time and hold it by about 58), and the rest of castles 3, 7 and 8 behind them.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: help>5 1->5 66 and 3->5 21, then feeds castle 5 with 3->5 57, 7->5 66 and 8->5 78.

## 2. Turn 112: castle 1 left to fall to 424 while castles 3 and 7 held 378

The opponent sent 139, 129 and 156 at castle 1 (183, production 4), landing in 5 to 6. Castle 7 (183) was 4 turns away, castle 3 (195) 5. We sent 8->2 22 and 8->9 28 at the enemy castles the strike had emptied (both were fed and failed), 7->3 58 and 3->5 13, and nothing to castle 1, which fell on turn 117 with 217 enemy soldiers in it. We were down to 3 castles to 7.

**The right move:** Send castle 7's 183 and castle 3's 35 or more to castle 1, which then holds.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: help>1 7->1 183 and 3->1 35, and feeds castle 3 with 8->3 195.

## 3. Turn 116: groups of 8 to 31 thrown at the enemy's stocked castles

From castle 3 (211) we sent 3->2 31 at castle 2 (25, with castle 4's 91 beside it at speed 3), after 8, 8 and 9 at castles 2, 9 and 5 on turn 115; by turn 120 castle 3 had sent 126 soldiers in nine groups at castles 2, 9 and 5 (up to 235), and none took anything. The opponent's stack then took castles 3, 7 and 8 one by one.

**The right move:** No sends at the enemy's castles: gather castles 7 and 8 into castle 3 (7->3 128, 8->3 128).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: exactly 7->3 128 and 8->3 128, nothing at the enemy.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 80: `["send 1->5 x66", "send 3->5 x78", "send 7->5 x66", "send 8->5 x78"]`
- turn 112: `["send 7->1 x183", "send 3->1 x181", "send 8->3 x195"]`
- turn 116: `["send 7->3 x128", "send 8->3 x128"]`
