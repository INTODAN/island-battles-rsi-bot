# Game 640: the three main mistakes

Map gyre; the league bot was fullfeed_unfreeze, playing as team 1, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 110.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 97: saving apart while the enemy stacked (decided the game)

Castle 2 held 173, castle 6 134 and castle 9 146; enemy castle 4 held 314 (speed 3) with castles 0, 3, 5 and 8 (about 400 more) beside it. We sent nothing; the enemy gathered 416 in castle 4 and on turn 99 sent 195 at castle 2, which fell on turn 100 with 185. Castles 6 and 9 fell on turns 103 and 105.

**The right move:** One stack: 6->2 134 and 9->2 146 (about 450 in castle 2).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: stack@2, feed>2 6:134 9:146.

## 2. Turn 67: castle 0 fed into the stack while enemy castle 4 massed beside it

Enemy castle 4 (30) had 114 landing on it; castle 0 held 46, 3 turns from castle 4. We fed castle 2 from everywhere: 0->2 32 (castle 0 left with 14), 6->2 32, 9->2 29, and 2->3 26. On turn 70 castle 4 sent 44 at castle 0, which fell on turn 72 with 34.

**The right move:** Keep castle 0's 46 home; feed castle 2 from castles 6 and 9 only.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats, worse: feed>2 0:46 6:46 9:65, emptying castle 0. `feedStack` sends a rear castle's `safeOut`, which counts only groups already landing on it, not the enemy castle beside it.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: feeds 6->2 46 and 9->2 65 and keeps castle 0's 46.

## 3. Turn 44: the stack spent on neutral castle 9 as the enemy gathered

Castle 2 (the stack) held 43 with 22 landing; enemy castles 3 (23, 34 landing) and 5 (30) stood beside castles 2 and 4 (16). We sent 2->9 30 at neutral castle 9 (25), taking it with 5; the next turn the enemy sent 65 at castle 4, and castle 2 sent 17 at castle 5 on turn 47, keeping 5.

**The right move:** No capture: gather on castle 4 (0->4 8, 2->4 43, 6->4 8).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: smash>9 in 2 with 27 from castle 2.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: stack@4, feed>4 0:8 2:43 6:8.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 44: `["send 0->4 x8", "send 2->4 x43", "send 6->4 x8"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 67: `["send 0->2 x46", "send 6->2 x46", "send 9->2 x65"]` (still makes it)
- turn 97: `["send 6->2 x134", "send 9->2 x146"]` (avoids the mistake)
