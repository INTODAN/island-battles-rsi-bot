# Game 574: the three main mistakes

Map bight; the league bot pounce_reach played team 1 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 189.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 14: the first centre castle taken with a margin of 3 (decided the game)

Castle 0 (19) sent 16 and castle 4 sent 2 at neutral castle 3 (15), about fourteen turns away at speed 1. They took it on turn 28 with 3. Enemy castle 1 had 17; it sent 15 on turn 15, which retook castle 3 on turn 30. Castle 3 became the enemy's stack (1737 by turn 177).

**The right move:** Hold the soldiers (buy speed first) and take castle 3 later with a margin above what castle 1 could land by the capture plus two turns.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: `send 4->3 x2`, `send 0->3 x16`. `neutralOption` prices the capture at the neutral's 15 plus `neutralExtra` 2 plus the enemy castles' `enemySpare` less the capture's growth, and castle 1's 17 is kept as its reserve against our castle 0, so the hold is 0.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats with the same commands; its capture price that holds is only in `smash`, not in `neutralOption`.

## 2. Turn 32: the second centre castle, the same way

Castle 0 (26) sent 17 at neutral castle 2 (15), eight turns away. It took castle 2 on turn 39 with 2; enemy castle 1 (4, production 4) sent 7 on turn 34 and retook it on turn 41. From then on we held castles 0 and 4 only, producing 8 a turn against 16, and the enemy stacked at castle 3.

**The right move:** Keep the 17 home; take castle 2 only with a margin against castle 1's growth over the walk.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: `send 0->2 x17` (and feeds 9 to castle 4). `enemySpare` counts castle 1's soldiers now (4), not what its production 4 adds before the capture.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats with the same commands.

## 3. Turn 182: the last castle emptied into a lost one

Castle 4 (903) had 910 landing in 2, 132 in 3 and 138 in 4; our 130 from castle 0 landed in 2. Castle 0 sent its last 118 (landing in 3) and kept 4. Even with it castle 4 came up a few soldiers short and fell on turn 187; enemy castle 3 (613) sent 98 at castle 0 on turn 184, which took it on turn 188.

**The right move:** Keep castle 0's 118 home: castle 4 falls either way, castle 0 holds against the 98.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: `send 0->4 x118`. `defend` sends the helpers that land in time even when all of them together cannot cover the lack, and its helpers keep only what their own landings need.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats with the same command; the fixes don't touch `defend`.
