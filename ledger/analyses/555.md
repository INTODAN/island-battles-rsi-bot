# Game 555: the three main mistakes

Map claw; the hosted bot was pounce_reach, playing as team 1 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 120.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 58: an all-in at castle 4 while castle 2 was being taken

Castle 2 (ours, 24, production 1) had 34 enemy soldiers landing in 2 to 3 turns. We sent 0->4 64, 5->4 26 and 3->4 4 at the enemy's castle 4 (52, production 4, 12 more landing), and only 3->2 8 to castle 2. Castle 4 was fed 15 and 13 and held; castle 2 fell on turn 63; castles 0, 3 and 5 were left with 4 to 12. From then on we held 3 castles to the opponent's 5, and its hoard at castle 4 (558 by turn 110) took castles 3, 5 and 0 between turns 114 and 119.

**The right move:** Defend and gather at castle 2: 3->2 8 (castle 3 buying speed to land in time) and feed castle 2 from castles 0 and 5; no strike at castle 4.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: help>2 (speed 3 at castle 3, 3->2 8) and makes castle 2 its stack, fed 0->2 64 and 5->2 34.

## 2. Turn 40: a thin strike at castle 4 that the opponent fed

The enemy's castle 4 held 5 (production 1), 6 turns from castle 0. We sent 0->4 17. The opponent answered with 15 and 8 on turn 41 and 17 more on turn 43; our 17, and the 25 that castle 0 sent on turn 42 (leaving it 4), were lost without taking it. With turn 32's 20, castle 4 cost us 62 soldiers for nothing.

**The right move:** No strike: feed castle 3, the castle facing castle 4 (0->3 34), and strike only with what also beats the help castles 1 and 6 can send.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: feeds its stack at castle 3 with 0->3 34.

## 3. Turn 14: neutral castle 4 taken as the opponent could retake it the next turn

Castle 4 (neutral, 10) is as far from our castle 0 as from the enemy's castle 1 (17), 12 turns each. We sent 0->4 15 and 3->4 5, leaving castles 0 and 3 empty. They took castle 4 on turn 25 with 10; the 18 that castle 1 sent on turn 15 took it on turn 26 with 7, and castle 4 became the opponent's front castle for the rest of the game.

**The right move:** No take: keep the 20 and buy production, and leave castle 4 to whoever pays its 10 first, then take it back from it thin.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: the same take>4 (3->4 5, 0->4 15).

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats it too: `neutralOption` takes a neutral that no enemy castle reaches strictly sooner (a tie counts as ours), and counts castle 1 against it only as its spare less the capture's growth over castle 1's 12-turn walk, although castle 1 grows faster than the capture.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 14: `[]`
- turn 40: `["send 0->3 x34"]`
- turn 58: `["up 3 speed", "send 3->2 x8", "send 0->2 x23"]`
