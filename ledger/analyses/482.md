# Game 482: the three main mistakes

Map claw; the hosted bot was pounce, playing as team 2; lost, eliminated on turn 93.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 76: too little to hold castle 6 (decided the game)

The enemy's 95 was landing on castle 6 (82) in 2 turns from its stack castle 4 (163 after the sortie). We bought speed at castle 1 and sent 1->6 6; castle 6 held with exactly 1, we sent nothing on turn 78, and the enemy's 14 took it on turn 79: a production-4 castle and the chance to strike castle 4 after its sortie.

**The right move:** Buy speed at castle 1 and send 1->6 110 and 7->6 95: about 300 beside a castle 4 down to 163.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: up 1 speed, 1->6 6, then 1->6 110 and 7->6 95, and keeps feeding castle 6 on turns 77 to 79.

## 2. Turns 82 to 84: home emptied

On turn 82 we sent 1->3 65 at a castle 8.2 away, and on turn 84 1->3 13, 1->2 49 and 7->2 70, leaving home with 4 while enemy castle 6 (45, speed 3) was 2.8 away. Its 16 took home on turn 86; the 65 and 13 died in clashes.

**The right move:** On turn 84 send nothing from castle 1 (keep 62); send 7->1 127 or let castle 7 alone attack.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: on turn 84 it sends 7->1 127 to home as its stack.

## 3. Turns 32 to 42: castle 4 attacked in small waves

We sent 20, 17 and 25 from castle 1 at castle 4, 6 turns away at speed 2; none captured, as the enemy's 8 and 15 kept landing. Castle 4 became the enemy's stack, which reached 274.

**The right move:** Feed the front castle (1->6 25) and do not attack castle 4.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: feeds castle 6 on turns 32, 40 and 42.
