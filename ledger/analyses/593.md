# Game 593: the three main mistakes

Map estuary; our bot burstguard, playing as team 1, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 109.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 35: a contested neutral fed with what the enemy matched

Neutral castle 5 (25) lies at the centre, as near the enemy's castles 1, 3 and 7 as ours. Castle 0 sent 20 and castle 2 7 at it (keeping 4 and 5); the enemy sent 12 and 5 on turn 37, our 27 left it at 0, and the enemy took it on turn 45. Castle 5 became the enemy's stack against castle 4 and castle 0 for the rest of the game.

**The right move:** No send: keep the 27 home and buy castle 2's production.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: no send at castle 5. It sends 0->7 16, a smash at the enemy's castle 7 (6, production 1) that lands in 8 with about 2 over; castle 1, 10 turns away at its speed, could take it back.

## 2. Turn 74: a strike at castle 4 under the enemy's stack

The enemy's castle 4 held 3 (with 48 of the enemy's landing) and its castle 5 held 58, 2 map units away. Castle 2 (97) sent 54, castle 6 26 and 9, castle 0 16; the enemy answered with 126 (58 from castle 5), took castle 4 back on turn 79 with 27, and castle 2 was left with 47 and then 4 after sending 51 at castle 5 on turn 76.

**The right move:** No strike: gather castles 2 and 6 into castle 0 and keep a stack against castles 4 and 5.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: send 2->0 97 and 6->0 35.

## 3. Turn 97: upgrades instead of helping castle 0

Castle 0 (43) had 58 from the enemy's castle 4 (64, speed 3) landing in 1; castle 4 sent 23 and 25 more on turns 98 and 101. Castle 2 (60) bought speed 3 and burst 2 and castle 6 (50) burst 3, and neither sent anything. Castle 0 fell on turn 102 with 5, and castle 2 (27 after the upgrades) on turn 103.

**The right move:** Send castle 2's 60 and castle 6's 50 into castle 0; buy nothing.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: buys speed at castle 2 but helps 2->0 12, feeds 2->0 28 and 6->0 50 into castle 0 (its stack).

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 35: `["send 0->7 x16"]`
- turn 74: `["send 2->0 x97", "send 6->0 x35"]`
- turn 97: `["up 2 speed", "send 2->0 x12", "send 2->0 x28"]`
