# Game 546: the three main mistakes

Map gyre; the league bot landgrab played as team 2 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 76.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 51: a strike at castle 2 that the enemy fed

Enemy castle 2 held 4. Castle 1 sent all its 33 at it (landing in 3, castle 1 left with 4). The enemy sent 10 from castle 4 and castle 2 was never taken; castle 1 then had 8 when it was needed.

**The right move:** No strike: the enemy was pouring 58 into castle 8, beside our castle 3 (13, production 2). Move castles 1, 5 and 7 into castle 3.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: stacks on castle 3, 1->3 33, 5->3 30, 7->3 13.

## 2. Turn 53: castle 3 emptied for neutral castle 9

Enemy castle 8 had 15 with 26 + 32 + 12 + 16 + 16 landing over 7 turns, 1.4 from our castle 3 (17). Castle 3 sent 16 and castle 7 11 at neutral castle 9 (25). On turn 56 castle 8 sent 15 at castle 3 and took it on turn 57 (9 to 4).

**The right move:** Keep castle 3 and feed it (castle 5's 38 is 1.4 away), and leave castle 9 for later.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: stacks on castle 3, 1->3 8, 5->3 38, 7->3 11.

## 3. Turn 59: 63 thrown at retaking castle 3

Castle 3 was the enemy's (6, 21 more landing in 1 and 24 after), beside enemy castle 8 (46). We sent 24 from castle 1, 30 from castle 5 and 9 from castle 7; the enemy sent 116 more into it over turns 60 and 61 and kept castle 3. Castle 7 fell on turn 67, castle 5 on turn 71, castle 1 on turn 75.

**The right move:** No retake: join castles 1 and 7 into castle 5 (62) and hold.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: stacks on castle 5, 1->5 24, 7->5 24.

- turn 59: `["send 1->5 x24", "send 7->5 x24"]` (avoids the mistake)
