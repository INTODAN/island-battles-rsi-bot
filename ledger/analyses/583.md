# Game 583: the three main mistakes

Map estuary; our bot was juggernaut, playing as team 1, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; won on turn 147.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake. The champion played this game, and on each board it gives the commands it really sent. The game was won, but slowly: until turn 100 our production stayed near the enemy's.

## 1. Turn 57: the stack left empty beside two enemy castles

The stack was castle 4 (5 soldiers, production 1, the centre), 5 turns from enemy castle 1 (27) and 6 from castle 3 (26). Castles 0 (24) and 2 (27), both at production 3 and 3 turns away, kept everything. The enemy sent 46 at castle 4 that turn; our help on turns 58 to 60 (11, 19, 3, 8) came piecemeal, and castle 4 fell on turn 62 (retaken on turn 64).

**The right move:** Send castle 0's 24 into castle 4: it lands in 3, before the 46, and castle 4 holds.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: no commands. `feedStack()` feeds the stack only from castles at production 4 (`topProduction`), and nothing else fills the stack's guard.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: no commands, by the same rule in `feedStack()`.

## 2. Turn 17: a neutral centre castle taken with 2

The stack, castle 0 (27), struck neutral castle 4 (25, production 1), 6 turns away, with 27; it took it on turn 22 with 2, and enemy castle 1 (24, 9 turns away) sent 15 at once. Holding castle 4 took groups of 2 to 19 from castles 0 and 2 nearly every turn until turn 95, and castle 0 stayed at production 3 until turn 101.

**The right move:** Keep the 27 and buy production 4 at castle 0 (30) on the next turn.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: `send 0->4 x27` (`smash>4 in 6 need 27`). `smash()` prices a neutral target at its soldiers plus `strikeExtra` (2), with no term for holding it.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: its hold term applies only to enemy-owned targets.

## 3. Turn 49: neutral castle 6 taken with 2

Castle 2 (27) sent 24 and castle 0 sent 3 at neutral castle 6 (25, production 1), while castle 4 had 1 with 17 landing (castle 0 sent it 13). Castle 6 was taken on turn 55 with 2; the enemy struck it with 34 on turn 63, it fell on turn 70 and was retaken on turn 74, and its defence drew about 140 soldiers by turn 109.

**The right move:** Keep castle 2's 24 and buy production 4 there (30) two turns later; it bought it only on turn 58.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: `send 0->4 x13`, `send 0->6 x3`, `send 2->6 x24` (`take>6`). `neutralOption()` holds the capture only against what the enemy castles could spare now (castles 1 and 3 had just struck, 3 and 4 left), not their production over the next turns.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats, by the same rule in `neutralOption()`.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 17: `[]`
- turn 49: `["send 0->4 x13"]`
- turn 57: `["send 0->4 x24", "send 2->4 x27"]`
