# Game 644: the three main mistakes

Map delta; our bot was punisher, playing as team 2, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 103.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 69: two strikes that took nothing

Castles 5 (45) and 7 (40) faced enemy castle 2 (41, speed 3, 5.0 from castle 5, so 2 turns). Castle 5 sent 25 at castle 2 (fed to 46) and castle 7 sent 19 at castle 4 (22); neither took anything, and castle 5 kept 20.

**The right move:** No strikes: gather castle 7 into castle 5, the castle castle 2 can reach.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats an equivalent mistake: 5->3 45 and 7->3 40, gathering in castle 3, a production-1 castle 30 enemy soldiers land on next turn, and leaving castle 5 with 0 beside castle 2.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: castle 3 falls before a feed lands, so `stackOf` skips it; it sends 7->5 40.

## 2. Turn 70: a retake of castle 3 that emptied castle 7

The enemy had just taken castle 3 (5, production 1). Castle 7 sent 25 at it (keeping 4), castle 5 sent 11 and castle 1 sent 1; 64 soldiers went into castle 3 over turns 70 and 71, and it changed hands four more times. Meanwhile castle 2 sent 44 at castle 5 (20) on turn 75: castle 5 fell on turn 78 and castle 7 on turn 84.

**The right move:** Let castle 3 go: feed castles 1 and 7 into castle 5, which faces castle 2.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats an equivalent mistake: 5->1 24 and 7->1 25, gathering at castle 1 and leaving castle 5 with 0. `stackOf` picks castle 1 over castle 5: both are 11 turns from an enemy at the slowest speed, and the tie goes to the lower id.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: 1->5 8 and 7->5 25, a stack at castle 5 (the tie is broken by the total distance to the other own castles).

## 3. Turn 93: the home castle emptied to help a castle 6 turns away (decided the game)

We held castles 1 (29) and 3 (17). Enemy groups of 42, 16 and 4 were landing on castle 3 in 5 to 6 turns; enemy castles 5 (20) and 7 (7, with 24 landing) were 5 turns from castle 1. Castle 1 sent 22 to castle 3 and kept 7. On turn 98 castle 7 sent 54 at castle 1 (31): castle 3 fell on turn 101 and castle 1 on turn 102.

**The right move:** Keep castle 1's 29 home: castle 3 holds at best by 1, and castle 1 is the castle the enemy can reach.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: help 1->3 22.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: help 1->3 22. In `defend`, helpers keep only what their own landings need (`safeOut`); nothing keeps a reserve against enemy castles that can reach the helper.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 69: `["send 7->5 x40"]`
- turn 70: `["send 1->5 x8", "send 7->5 x25"]`
- turn 93: `["send 1->3 x22"]`
