# Game 495: the three main mistakes

Map claw; our bot was pounce, playing as team 2, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 97.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 58: a 114-soldier strike that took castle 4 but could not hold it

Castle 4 was the enemy's with 79 at production 4, fed every few turns by castles 0, 3 and 5 (24, 9, 24). We sent 63 from castle 1, 36 from castle 7 and 15 from castle 6, leaving 4 to 6 in each. The enemy fed castle 4 with 68 more on turn 59; we took it on turn 63 with 2 and lost it on turn 64 to a group of 52. About 114 soldiers bought nothing, and our three castles were empty beside a stocked enemy.

**The right move:** No strike: gather castles 1 and 7 into one stack (castle 6, the castle facing castle 4) and keep producing.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: feeds its stack at castle 6 (1->6 63, 7->6 48) and sends only 10 from castle 6 at castle 2 (7). juggernaut_fix feeds castle 6 the same way and sends nothing at castle 2.

## 2. Turn 82: castle 6 left with 9 beside the enemy's stack

Castle 6 had just survived a group of 83 and held 9; enemy castle 4, 3.2 away, held 114 at speed 4 (it lands in 2) with 63 more on the way to it. Castles 1 (68) and 7 (81) kept their soldiers. On the same turn the enemy sent 24 from castle 4; castle 6 fell on turn 83 (13 to 24), and our retake on turn 84 lost it again on turn 85.

**The right move:** Move castle 1's 68 (and castle 7's 81) into castle 6, so the enemy's next small group cannot take it.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: 1->6 68 and 7->6 81, a stack of 158 at castle 6. juggernaut_fix sends 1->6 68 and keeps castle 7's 81 home.

## 3. Turn 86: a strike at castle 4 while castle 6 fell (decided the game)

Castle 6 held 2; enemy groups of 69 (lands in 1), 20, 20 and 24 were landing on it. Castle 4 held 100 at production 4 and speed 4. We sent 67 from castle 1 (left 4) and 63 from castle 7 at castle 4. Castle 6 fell on turn 87 with 63 in it; the strike melted on castle 4, castle 1 fell on turn 92 (5 soldiers) and castle 7 on turn 96 (48).

**The right move:** No strike at a 100-soldier production-4 castle: keep castles 1 and 7 stocked (or gather them in castle 7) and answer castle 6's captor from there.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids the strike: it sends 1->6 67 and 7->6 97 to retake castle 6 behind the 69. juggernaut_fix gathers instead: 1->7 67, a 164 stack at castle 7.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 58: `["send 1->6 x63", "send 7->6 x48"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 82: `["send 1->6 x68", "send 7->6 x81"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 86: `["send 1->7 x67"]` (avoids the mistake)
