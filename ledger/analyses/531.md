# Game 531: the three main mistakes

Map delta; the league bot pounce played as team 1 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 240.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 113: a retake of castle 4 that left castle 6 bare

Castle 4 had just fallen (3 in it) with enemy castle 3 (182) beside it. Castle 0 (294) sent 133 to retake it and castle 6 kept 12. The enemy answered with 104 into castle 4 and 112 + 96 at castle 6; we spent 49 + 72 + 32 more to save castle 6 and 52 more on castle 4, took it on turn 119 and lost it again on turn 122. We led 230 to 35 in soldiers on turn 120 and trailed 103 to 113 by turn 130.

**The right move:** No retake beside a stocked enemy castle: move castle 0's stock and castle 2's into castle 6, the front castle the enemy could reach.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: stacks on castle 6, 0->6 294 and 2->6 68.

## 2. Turn 129: soldiers dripped into a production-1 castle

Castle 2 sent 28 at enemy castle 5 (9, production 1, fed with 4 or 5 by castle 7 every two turns), then 15 more on turn 131. From about turn 60 to turn 156 castle 2 sent 6 or 7 there every two turns; castle 5 never fell.

**The right move:** Nothing at castle 5; castle 2's 59 and castle 0's 52 into castle 6, where 94 enemy soldiers were gathering in castle 4 next door.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: stacks on castle 6, 0->6 52 and 2->6 59.

## 3. Turn 140: castle 6 kept thin beside the enemy stack

Enemy castle 4 held 173 with 70 more landing, 2 from castle 6 (52). Castles 0 (96) and 2 (47) stayed apart. On turn 145 the enemy sent 87 and took castle 6 (76) on turn 146, then stacked over 400 in it.

**The right move:** Feed castle 6 from castles 0 and 2 while the help still lands before a strike: 52 + 96 + 47 and growth hold the 87.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: 0->6 96 and 2->6 47.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 113: `["send 0->6 x294"]`
- turn 129: `["send 0->6 x52", "send 2->6 x48"]`
- turn 140: `["send 0->6 x96"]`
