# Game 512: the three main mistakes

Map archipelago; the league bot speedhelp_fullfeed played as team 1 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 73.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 47: a strike at castle 11 that never took it

Castle 6 (40) sent 28 at enemy castle 11 (6, with 5 more coming from castle 1), and over the next six turns another 13, 9, 13 and 11 from castles 6 and 0. The enemy fed castle 11 each time and it never fell; meanwhile 20 from castle 7 took castle 10 on turn 48 and 29 took castle 4 on turn 52.

**The right move:** No strike at castle 11: keep castle 6's soldiers as the stack (or take neutral castle 8, 30, which no enemy group was heading for).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: stacks on castle 6 (0->6 8, 2->6 12, 4->6 16) and smashes neutral castle 8 with 32; nothing goes to castle 11.

## 2. Turn 50: a retake of castle 10 into 42 enemy soldiers

Castle 10 had fallen (8 in it) and 11 + 31 enemy soldiers were landing on it in 6. We sent 19 from castle 6, 6 from castle 2 and 1 from castle 0 to retake it: none of it held, and castle 4 (8, 29 landing in 3) got nothing from castle 0.

**The right move:** No retake; castle 0's 8 to castle 4, and the rest into one stack.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: help 0->4 8, and stacks on castle 0 (2->0 12, 6->0 19).

## 3. Turn 66: three castles apart beside a stack of 141

Enemy castle 4 held 141 with 80 more landing in 1 to 5, castle 10 held 90. Ours were 81 (castle 0), 48 (castle 2) and 52 (castle 6), apart. On turn 67 the enemy sent 83 + 17 at castle 0 (89) and took it, then castle 2 (64) on turn 70 and castle 6 (68) on turn 72.

**The right move:** Join the three in one castle: 181 in castle 0 holds the 100 that came.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: stacks on castle 0, 2->0 48 and 6->0 52.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 47: `["up 6 speed", "send 0->6 x8", "send 2->6 x12", "send 4->6 x16"]`
- turn 50: `["send 0->4 x8", "send 6->2 x19"]`
- turn 66: `["send 0->2 x81", "send 6->2 x52"]`
