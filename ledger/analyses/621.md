# Game 621: the three main mistakes

Map bight; our bot fullfeed_unfreeze played as team 1 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost on turn 125.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 95: a strike that emptied both castles

Castle 2 (enemy, 25, production 2) stood 5 from castle 3 (enemy). We sent 0->2 20 and 4->2 24, leaving castles 0 and 4 with 3 and 4. Castle 2 was taken with 10 on turn 100 and lost on turn 104 to 62 from castle 3; then the enemy struck castle 0 (turn 106), castle 4 (fell on 115) and castle 0 again (fell on 124).

**The right move:** No strike: speed at castle 4 and keep both stocked.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: only speed 3 at castle 4.

## 2. Turn 102: help that lands after the fall

Castle 2 (12, 24 of ours landing in 1) had 49 landing in 3: 42 against 49. Castle 4 is 8 turns away. We sent 4->2 28, leaving castle 4 with 4; castle 2 fell on turn 104 and the 28 landed on an enemy castle.

**The right move:** Keep castle 4's 28 home.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: feeds castle 2, its stack, 4->2 28 (stackOf picks the castle nearest the enemy even when its landings take it).

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: sends nothing, stack in castle 4.

## 3. Turn 74: a retake of castle 3 that couldn't hold

Castle 3 (enemy, 18, production 2) was fed from castles 1 and 5. We sent 4->3 16 and 0->3 20 (and 12 more on turn 77); castle 3 was taken with 2 on turn 81 and lost on turn 83. Castle 0 stayed at production 3 all game.

**The right move:** Wait and build: no strike.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: sends nothing.
