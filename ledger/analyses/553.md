# Game 553: the three main mistakes

Map claw; the hosted bot was juggernaut, playing as team 1, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost (its last castle fell on turn 64, the game ended on turn 67).

The game is move for move game 486 up to turn 54 (the same boards on turns 47 and 49), then differs. Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 49: a strike on castle 2 that could take it but not hold it (decided the game)

Our stack, castle 3 (26, speed 4), sent 19 at enemy castle 2 (11) while enemy castle 4 (13, with 22 more landing on it) stood 3 away and 69 enemy soldiers walked at our castle 5. Castle 2 fell to us on turn 50 with 2; we fed it about 60 from castles 0 and 3 against 23, 30 and 9 from the enemy, and it fell back on turn 58.

**The right move:** No strike: send castle 5 its 4 of help and keep the stack whole in castle 3.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: help>5 3:4 and smash>2 with 19. Its strike price counts only enemy castles whose help lands before the strike (`1+travel <= walk` in `smash`), not castle 4's stock and feeds that retake the capture.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: sends only 3->5 4 and keeps the 19.

## 2. Turn 47: speed 4 bought from the stack beside a fed enemy castle

Castle 3 (32, speed 3) bought speed 4 for 18, keeping 14, while enemy castle 4 (8) 3.2 away had 34 landing on it within 4 turns and castle 2 held 29.

**The right move:** Buy nothing: keep 32 in castle 3.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: up 3 speed and 5->3 8. `build` buys stack speed while the stack keeps its `guard`, which counts the enemy castles' stock but not the groups landing on them.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: only 5->3 8.

## 3. Turn 55: castle 3 emptied into castle 2 beside an enemy castle of 48

The stack had moved to castle 2 (14). Castle 3 (12) sent 12 to it, keeping 4, with enemy castle 4 (48) 3.2 away. The enemy sent 33 from castle 4 at castle 3 the same turn, plus 30 and 9 at castle 2: castle 3 fell on turn 58 (to 17 left in it), castle 2 the same turn, and with them the game (castles 5 and 0 fell on turns 63 and 64).

**The right move:** Send nothing from castle 3. The opponent strikes the castle whose garrison drops; with 12 home castle 3 would not have been the cheap target, and the 12 sent were lost in castle 2 anyway.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: feed>2 3:12. `feedStack` sends a rear castle's whole `safeOut` when no enemy group is yet bound for it, whatever the enemy castle beside it holds.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: sends nothing; a rear castle keeps what an enemy castle that lands before the stack's help could take.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 47: `["send 5->3 x8"]`
- turn 49: `["send 3->5 x4"]`
- turn 55: `[]`
