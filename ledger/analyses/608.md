# Game 608: the three main mistakes

Map star-of-david-duo; our bot fullfeed_unfreeze played as team 1 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost on turn 186.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 82: every castle emptied into castle 12

Castle 12 (38, with 28 of ours coming) had 71 enemy landing in 3 and needed about 10 more; 61 landed on castle 0 (50, production 1) in 3, out of anyone's reach. We sent 1->12 32, 3->12 49, 6->12 58, 7->12 15, 8->12 33. Castle 0 fell on turn 84, castle 8 (left 15) on turn 87 to 13, castle 6 (left 7) on turn 92 to 70.

**The right move:** Speed at castle 7 and 7->12 15; keep the rest home or gather it in castle 6.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats an equivalent: speed and 7->12 15, then feeds castle 0 (its stack) 1->0 32, 3->0 49, 6->0 61, 8->0 44, which falls before any of it lands; castles 6 and 8 are left empty (stackOf picks the castle nearest the enemy even when its landings take it).

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: the stack is castle 6 (speed 3 and 4, 1->6 32, 3->6 49, 8->6 27).

## 2. Turn 154: castle 7 left to fall

293 landed on castle 7 (280, production 4) in 3. Castle 12 (300, speed 3) was 4 turns away. We sent nothing; castle 7 fell on turn 156 with 1 left to the enemy.

**The right move:** Speed 4 at castle 12 and 12->7 2 (castle 7 holds by 1), or more.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: exactly that, then gathers in castle 3.

## 3. Turn 164: small strikes instead of gathering

Castle 12 (340) could not be saved from 359 landing in 3. We sent 3->6 17 at castle 6 (313), 3->7 47 at castle 7 (29; taken on 167, lost on 171) and 1->3 53. On turn 167 the enemy sent about 650 at castle 3 and 295 at castle 8; both fell.

**The right move:** Gather: 3->1 366 and 8->1 238, speed at castle 1.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: exactly that.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 82: `["up 7 speed", "send 7->12 x18", "send 0->6 x50", "send 1->6 x14", "send 3->6 x49", "send 8->6 x10"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 154: `["up 12 speed", "send 12->7 x2", "send 1->7 x270", "send 3->7 x326", "send 8->7 x198", "send 12->7 x268"]` (still makes it)
- turn 164: `["send 12->6 x145", "send 1->8 x292", "send 3->8 x21"]` (still makes it)
