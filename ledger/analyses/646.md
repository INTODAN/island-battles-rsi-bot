# Game 646: the three main mistakes

Map estuary; the hosted bot was punisher, playing as team 2, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; won, the last team standing on turn 280.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 130: 170 turns without a send or an upgrade

From turn 97 to turn 272 we sent nothing and bought nothing. On turn 130 we held five castles (625) against three (377); castle 7 stayed at production 2 and castle 4 at production 3 all game. The enemy gathered into castle 0 and struck first on turn 270; the game was decided only on turn 280 of 300.

**The right move:** Buy production at castles 7 and 4, gather the 136s into one castle and strike the enemy's small castles 6 (65) and 2 (78).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids the idling: production 3 and 4 at castle 7 and 1->4 136, 3->4 136, 5->4 144, 7->4 20 into its stack at castle 4 (it does not strike on this turn).

## 2. Turn 271: castle 4 left to fall to the enemy's 981

The enemy sent 981 from castle 0 at castle 4 (562, lands in 2). Castle 5 held 708, two turns away; castles 1 and 3 had 700 each. We sent nothing; castle 4 fell on turn 272 with 413 left for the enemy. We won afterwards because the enemy had emptied castles 2 and 6.

**The right move:** Send castle 5's 708 to castle 4: 1270 holds 981.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: help>4 5:414, then feeds castle 4 with everything else (the fix sends 5->4 414 and 294).

## 3. Turn 59: feeding the fight for the middle castle

Castle 5 (production 1) sits between the two sides; from turn 29 to 69 it changed hands thirteen times, each side sending 10 to 34 at a time, while neutral castles 4, 6 and 7 (25 each) stayed untaken until turns 79 to 89. On turn 59 castle 5 held 17 with 24 landing in 1 and 22 behind; we sent 1->5 12 and 3->5 12, and it fell on turn 59 and changed hands six more times.

**The right move:** Leave castle 5 and take neutral castle 7 (25) beside castle 1 with castles 1 and 3.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: help>5 and feed 1->5 12, 3->5 12. Its `stackOf` is the own castle nearest an enemy castle, so the stack is the contested middle castle and every feed goes into the fight.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: castle 5 falls before a feed could land, so the fix skips it as a stack and sends 3->1 12.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 59: `["send 3->1 x12"]`
- turn 130: `["send 5->4 x144"]`
- turn 271: `["send 5->4 x708", "up 7 generation_amount", "up 7 generation_amount"]`
