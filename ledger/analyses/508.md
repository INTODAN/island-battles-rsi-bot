# Game 508: the three main mistakes

Map archipelago; our bot was batchfront, playing as team 2, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 82.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 44: a siege of castle 2 that never took it

Enemy castle 2 held 12 at production 4, fed from castles 0, 6 and 10. Castle 11 (47) sent 38 at it, keeping 13 beside enemy castle 10 (33). Over turns 44 to 55 ten waves, about 142 soldiers from castles 1, 3, 5 and 11, landed one by one on castle 2 and never took it. On turn 55 the enemy sent 77 at castle 11 (9), and four castles had to pour 48 into it.

**The right move:** No strike at a production-4 castle that its neighbours feed: gather castles 1 and 3 into castle 11 (or 7) and keep producing.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: gathers a stack at castle 7 (1->7 9, 3->7 13, 11->7 47). juggernaut_fix gathers at castle 11 (1->11 9, 3->11 13) and sends 32 at neutral castle 8 (30), a capture with 2 beside enemy castles 6 and 10.

## 2. Turn 72: castle 7 emptied for a 6-turn strike as castle 11 fell

Castle 11 (15) was falling to 74 enemy soldiers landing in 1 to 2 turns, so the enemy would hold a castle beside castles 1, 3, 5 and 7. Castle 7 (33) sent 32, and castles 3 and 5 sent 8 each, at castle 2 (4), 6.0 away at speed 1: they landed on turn 78 into a castle fed to 37, and took nothing.

**The right move:** Keep castle 7 stocked and gather castles 1, 3 and 5 into it, facing the enemy's new castle 11.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats an equivalent mistake: it feeds castle 7 (1->7 9, 3->7 9, 5->7 9) but sends castle 7's 32 at neutral castle 9 (30), leaving castle 7 with 1 for a capture of 2 beside enemy castles 10 (22) and 11.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats it with the same commands. Its smash prices a capture to hold against nearby enemy castles only when the target is the enemy's; a neutral target is skipped only when an enemy reaches it first (`smash`, the `target.team == neutral && b.sooner(...)` branch), so castle 9 costs 32.

## 3. Turn 74: a second wave at castle 2 beside the enemy's new castle 11

Castle 11 was now the enemy's (23, with 36 landing in 1). Castle 7 sent 20 more and castles 3 and 5 sent 8 each at castle 2 (12, with 17 landing in 1), 36 soldiers on a 5-turn walk, while 48 of ours were still walking there. On turn 75 the enemy sent 63 at castle 7 and 21 at castle 5 (9): castle 5 fell on turn 77, castle 7 on turn 78, castles 1 and 3 on turn 81.

**The right move:** Call nothing more at castle 2: gather castles 3, 5 and 7 into one castle (castle 1 or 3) that castle 11's stack cannot take.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: gathers at castle 1 (3->1 9, 5->1 9, 7->1 21). juggernaut_fix gathers at castle 3 (1->3 17, 7->3 21).

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 44: `["up 11 speed", "send 1->11 x9", "send 3->11 x13"]`
- turn 72: `["up 7 speed", "send 1->7 x9", "send 3->7 x9", "send 5->7 x9"]`
- turn 74: `["send 1->3 x17", "send 7->3 x21"]`
