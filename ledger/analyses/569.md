# Game 569: the three main mistakes

Map archipelago; pounce_reach played as team 1 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost on turn 85.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 69: feeding a strike while castle 10 was about to fall (decided the game)

Five enemy groups (30, 24, 16, 16, 6: 92) landed on castle 10 (16) in 4 to 6 turns. We sent castle 0's 20, castle 4's 13 and castle 2's 8 into enemy castle 8 (2, with 11 landing) and only 5 to castle 10. Castle 10 fell on turn 74 with 21 in it; castles 4 (turn 77), 6 (79), 0 (83) and 2 (84) followed.

**The right move:** Send castle 0's 24, castle 2's 8 and castle 4's 18 into castle 10 (it then holds), no strike at castle 8.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: `send 4->10 x5` (help>10 in 4, which only holds the first landing), then feeds its stack castle 6 (`0->6 x24`, `2->6 x8`, `4->6 x13`), so castle 10 falls to the later landings. `stackOf` picks castle 6 (ties by id), and `defend()` helps only against the first landing that takes the castle.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: the same help, then stack@10 (`0->10 x24`, `2->10 x8`, `4->10 x13`).

## 2. Turn 53: castle 10 emptied for a neutral take

Castle 10 (20) sent 20 and castle 2 sent 12 to the neutral castle 9 (30), keeping 4 in castle 10, 2 to 3 turns from enemy castles 7 (48), 11, 3 and 1. The take fell short; on turn 55 the enemy sent 132 at castle 10, which castle 0 held only by emptying itself (109 sent), and the enemy took castle 9 on turn 56.

**The right move:** No take: keep castle 10's 20 and feed it from castle 0.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: `send 10->9 x20`, `send 2->9 x12` (take>9), and feeds castle 8 with castles 0, 2 and 4 (133).

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids emptying castle 10: it takes castle 9 from castles 2 and 4 (28 and 4) and makes castle 10 its stack (`0->10 x91`, `4->10 x22`).

## 3. Turn 46: a neutral take that could not hold

Castles 10 (18) and 2 (14) sent 32 to the neutral castle 8 (30), 2 from enemy castles 11 (37) and 5 (19). It was taken on turn 49 with 2, and castle 5's 13 and 15 took it back on turn 53.

**The right move:** No take: keep the 32 home and feed the stack.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: `send 10->8 x18`, `send 2->8 x14` (take>8).

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: the same take. `neutralOption` prices the hold against what the enemy castles could spare now (`enemySpare`), not against what they produce while our soldiers walk and after the capture.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 46: `["send 0->10 x63", "send 2->10 x18", "send 4->10 x8"]`
- turn 53: `["send 0->10 x91", "send 2->10 x28", "send 4->10 x26", "send 6->10 x18"]`
- turn 69: `["send 4->10 x18", "send 0->10 x4", "send 0->10 x20", "send 2->10 x8"]`
