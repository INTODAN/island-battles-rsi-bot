# Game 617: the three main mistakes

Map bight; our bot batchfront_keep played team 1 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 220.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 66: a long strike at castle 1 while castles 0 and 4 held 5 each

Castle 3 (59, with 48 more being fed to it) was our front; castles 0 and 4 fed it 12 at a time and kept about 5. We sent 3->1 58 at enemy castle 1 (12), 8 turns away. On turn 67 enemy castle 2 (72) sent 49 at castle 4, which fell on turn 71; the 58 took nothing (castle 1 had 51 when they landed). Castle 4 became the enemy's stack, which reached 2000 and took castle 0 on turn 219.

**The right move:** No strike: keep castle 3 whole and stock castle 4 against castle 2.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: smash>1 with 3->1 50 (8 turns), and feeds 0->3 9 and 4->3 9, leaving castles 0 and 4 empty.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: the same commands. The capture price counts enemy castles within 4 turns of castle 1 (castle 5, 4) but not castle 2, 8 turns away, which struck castle 4 instead; and the rear reserve keeps nothing at castle 4 because castle 2's walk was longer than the stack's help.

## 2. Turn 75: a strike at castle 5 that left castle 3 with 19

Castle 3 held 62; enemy castle 4 (32) was 5 away and castle 2 (30) 5 away. We sent 3->5 47 at castle 5 (4), which the enemy fed in time; castles 2 and 4 sent 50 at castle 3 on turn 76 and it fell on turn 81, leaving us one castle for 139 turns.

**The right move:** No strike: keep castle 3's 62 (it holds the 50).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids the strike but gives up castle 3: 3->0 62.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: sends nothing, castle 3 keeps 62.

## 3. Turn 72: the front castle fed the rear

Castle 4 had just fallen (enemy 20); castle 3 had 73 with 24 more landing. We sent 3->0 19, and 3->0 28 on turn 73, thinning castle 3 to 46 before the turn-75 strike.

**The right move:** Keep castle 3 stocked (97) against castles 2 and 4.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats, more: 3->0 73, emptying castle 3 into castle 0.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: 3->0 9, castle 3 keeps 64 and the 24 landing.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 66: `["send 0->3 x9", "send 4->3 x9"]` (still makes it)
- turn 72: `["send 3->0 x9"]` (still makes it)
- turn 75: `[]` (avoids the mistake)
