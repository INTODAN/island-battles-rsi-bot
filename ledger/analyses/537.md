# Game 537: the three main mistakes

Map delta; speedhelp_fullfeed played as team 1 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost on turn 96.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 51: castle 4 emptied beside enemy castle 3 (decided the game)

Castle 4 (17) fed its 17 to the stack in castle 2 (10, production 1) and kept 4, with enemy castle 3 (41, fed from castle 1) 3.5 away. Castle 3 sent 54 on turn 54; castle 4 fell on turn 59 with 32 in it. The enemy then fed castle 4 every turn (324 by turn 88) and broke castles 2, 6 and 0 from it.

**The right move:** Keep castle 4's 17 (it holds the 54 with castle 6's 12 and its growth) and feed castle 4, the front, instead of castle 2.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: `up 2 generation_amount`, `send 4->2 x17`, `send 6->2 x12`. Its stack is castle 2 and `feedStack` gives the stack all of every rear castle's `safeOut` when no enemy group is bound for it yet, so castle 4 keeps nothing against castle 3.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: stack@4, `send 6->4 x12`; castle 4 keeps its 17.

## 2. Turn 62: castle 2 emptied into castle 6

Castle 2 (32) sent 37 and 10 on turns 60 to 61 and 32 more on turn 62 to castle 6, keeping 4, while enemy castle 4 (26, with 77 landing) was 7 turns away. Castle 4 sent 52 on turn 66; castle 2, refilled only by 17, fell on turn 74.

**The right move:** Keep castle 2's 32 home (it then holds the 52 with its growth) and feed castle 6 only from castle 0.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: `send 0->6 x12`, `send 2->6 x32` (feed>6). The same `feedStack` rule: no enemy group was yet bound for castle 2.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: the same feed. Its reserve counts only enemy castles that land before the stack's help, and castle 4 needs 7 turns to castle 2, as many as castle 6, so castle 2 keeps nothing.

## 3. Turn 69: dribbling into castle 4 while castle 0 saved apart

Castle 6 (89) sent 9 into enemy castle 4 (70, 36 landing in 1), one of six such 9s on turns 67 to 77 (54 soldiers spent for nothing), while castle 0 kept its 28 and grew it to 104 by turn 88 without feeding castle 6. On turn 88 castle 4 (324) sent 165 to castle 6 (152), which fell on turn 89; castle 0 fell on turn 95.

**The right move:** No dribble; feed castle 0's 28 into castle 6 (and keep doing so).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: `up 6 speed`, `send 0->6 x28`.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 51: `["send 6->4 x12"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 62: `["send 0->6 x12", "send 2->6 x32"]` (still makes it)
- turn 69: `["send 0->6 x28"]` (avoids the mistake)
