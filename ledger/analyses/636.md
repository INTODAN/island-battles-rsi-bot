# Game 636: the three main mistakes

Map estuary; our bot batchfront_keep, playing as team 1, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 167.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 71: a strike at castle 7 that the enemy's stack refills

Castle 4 (90) stood 2 map units from the enemy's castle 5 (47, speed 3). It sent 50 at castle 7 (14, with 60 of ours already walking there), keeping 44; castle 5 had sent 30 into castle 7 and sent 51 more on turn 72. The strike took nothing, and the same happened on turns 63, 77, 93 and 95 (about 230 soldiers spent).

**The right move:** No strike: keep castle 4's 90 against castle 5 and feed castles 0 and 2 into it.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats an equivalent mistake: smash>6 with 27 at neutral castle 6 (25), 4 turns from castle 5 as from castle 4, buys speed at castle 4 and feeds 0->4 13, 2->4 13. In `smash` a neutral target is priced at its soldiers plus `strikeExtra`, with no enemy castle counted that could take it back.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats the same commands: the neutral branch of `smash` is unchanged.

## 2. Turn 87: saving apart while the enemy stacks

Castles 0, 2 and 4 each held 57. The enemy's castle 5 held 105 (with 23 landing) at speed 3, 2 turns from castle 4. Each castle kept its own; on turn 89 castle 5 (136) sent 74 at castle 4 (65), which fell on turn 90 with 1 over. The enemy then stacked castle 4 to 249.

**The right move:** Send castles 0's and 2's 57 into castle 4 (3 turns), so it holds 170.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: send 0->4 57 and 2->4 57.

## 3. Turn 93: a 7-turn strike, and castle 2 emptied

Castle 4 was the enemy's (11, with 83 landing). Castles 0 (67) and 2 (63) sent 39 each at castle 7 (12), 7 turns away; castle 5 sent 43 into it and it held. On turn 95 castle 2 sent 31 at castle 5 and kept 5. Castle 2 never refilled past 25; castle 4 sent 110 at it on turn 112 and it fell on turn 116.

**The right move:** No strike: gather castle 2 into castle 0 and hold one stack of 130.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats an equivalent mistake: buys speed at castle 0 and takes neutral castle 6 (25) with 31, 4 turns from castle 4, whose 94 soon land; feeds 2->0 63. Its stack guard counts the enemy castles' stock but not the groups landing on them.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: only feeds 2->0 63 (its guard counts the 83 landing on castle 4).

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 71: `["up 4 speed", "send 0->4 x13", "send 2->4 x13"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 87: `["send 0->4 x57", "send 2->4 x57"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 93: `["send 2->0 x63"]` (avoids the mistake)
