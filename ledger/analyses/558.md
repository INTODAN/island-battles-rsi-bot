# Game 558: the three main mistakes

Map star-of-david-duo; the hosted bot was juggernaut, playing as team 1, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost (its last castle fell on turn 89, the game ended on turn 101).

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 61: the stack emptied on speed 4

Castle 6, the stack (30, speed 3), bought speed 4 and kept 4. Enemy castle 11 (18) was 6 turns away and castle 9 (38) beside our castle 8. The next turn castle 9 sent 42 at castle 8, and the stack had only 16 to send it; castle 8 fell on turn 67.

**The right move:** Buy nothing: keep 30 in the stack, enough to answer castle 9's strike.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: speed4@6 and 7->6 8. `build` keeps only the stack's `guard`, which counts enemy castles within `guardWalk` (4 turns); on this map the enemy castles are 6 turns away, so the guard is about 0.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats the same commands: its guard adds the groups landing on enemy castles, but still only within 4 turns.

## 2. Turn 62: castle 8 fed its last 8 to the stack beside an enemy castle of 42

Castle 8 (8) sent 8 to castle 6, a 20-turn walk at speed 1 (four such groups of 8 from it were already walking), while enemy castle 9 (42) stood 5.8 away. Castle 9 sent 42 at castle 8 the same turn; castle 8, with 20 and the stack's 16, fell on turn 67.

**The right move:** Keep the 8 home (and stop the 20-turn feeds): castle 8 would have held about 44 against the 42.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: feed>6 3:8 8:8 (and production2@1). `feedStack` sends a rear castle's whole `safeOut` at any distance when no enemy group is yet bound for it.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: its rear reserve counts only enemy castles that land before the stack's help (castle 9 lands in 6, the stack in 5), and the stack it counts on had just spent itself on speed.

## 3. Turn 67: a 5-turn strike that left the stack with 2

The stack (32) sent 30 at enemy castle 10 (8, production 4), 5 turns away, with enemy castles 9 (20) and 11 (42) 6 turns from both castle 10 and castle 6. The strike took nothing; on turn 71 the enemy sent 101 at castle 6, which fell on turn 76.

**The right move:** No strike: the stack keeps its 32 against castle 11's 42.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: smash>10 with 30 and 7->6 8. `smash` adds to the price only enemy castles that land before the strike, and the stack's `guard` only those within 4 turns, so castles 9 and 11 count for nothing.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: its retake price also stops at `guardWalk`, 4 turns.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 61: `["send 7->6 x8"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 62: `["send 3->6 x8", "send 8->6 x8"]` (still makes it)
- turn 67: `["send 7->6 x8"]` (avoids the mistake)
