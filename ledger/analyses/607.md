# Game 607: the three main mistakes

Map star-of-david-duo; the hosted bot was batchfront_keep, playing as team 1, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 100.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 56: castle 6 emptied beside enemy castle 11 (decided the game)

Castle 6 (production 4) held 17 after the strike of turn 55; enemy castle 11 held 20, 6 turns away. We sent 16 from castle 6 to castle 0 on the map's edge, keeping 5 (with 12 landing from castle 7). On turn 60 castle 11 sent 34 and took castle 6 on turn 65 (25 there). Kept home, castle 6 would have held over 50.

**The right move:** Keep castle 6's 17 home and feed it from castle 7.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: its stack is castle 0 (production 2, on the edge, nearest an enemy by walk with ties to the lowest id), and it sends 6->0 17 and 7->0 13, emptying both. The rule is `stackOf()`'s tie-break plus `feedStack()`, which empties every rear castle with no enemy group bound for it.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: its stack is castle 6 and it feeds 7->6 13.

## 2. Turn 55: 43 thrown at castle 4, fifteen turns away

Castle 6 held 44; enemy castle 4 (5, production 1) was 15 turns away. We sent 43 from castle 6 to castle 4; it never fell, and castle 6 was left with 1 before mistake 1.

**The right move:** Keep castle 6's 44 home.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids the strike, but sends 6->0 44 and 7->0 9 to its edge stack (mistake 1's error).

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats an equivalent: it strikes castle 11 with 42 (its exact price for the landing in 6), keeping 2 in castle 6. Enemy castle 10 (12) is 6 turns from castle 11 and can retake it right after; the hold pricing counts only enemy castles within `guardWalk` (4 turns) of the target.

## 3. Turn 70: castle 1's 50 thrown at castle 9

Castle 1 held 51; enemy castle 9 held 8, 12 turns away. Castle 7 held 13 beside enemy castle 8 (46). We sent 50 from castle 1 to castle 9 (24 more on turn 73), keeping 5; the strike failed, castle 8 sent 41 at castle 7 on turn 74 and took it on turn 79, and castle 1 fell on turn 91.

**The right move:** Send castle 1's 51 into castle 7.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats in part: no strike, but it buys speed at castle 0 and sends 1->0 51 and 7->0 13, leaving castle 7 bare beside castle 8, by the same `stackOf()` and `feedStack()` rules.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: its stack is castle 7 and it feeds 0->7 23 and 1->7 51.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 55: `["send 7->6 x9"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 56: `["send 8->1 x31", "send 7->6 x13"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 70: `["send 0->7 x23", "send 1->7 x51", "send 3->7 x19"]` (avoids the mistake)
