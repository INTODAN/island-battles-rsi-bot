# Game 552: the three main mistakes

Map gyre; our bot speedhelp_fullfeed played as team 2 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 70.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 47: front castle 3 emptied beside enemy castle 2

Castle 3 (18) was our centre castle, 4.5 units from enemy castle 2 (26) and 3.6 from castle 4. We sent 17 of it at castle 4 (8, with 12 more landing in 2), leaving 1. The strike took nothing; on turn 48 castle 2 bought speed and sent 21 at castle 3, which fell on turn 50. Castle 5 (1 after holding a 65-soldier attack) fell on turn 51, and from then the enemy had 7 castles to our 3.

**The right move:** Keep castle 3's 18 home (with growth it holds 21); no strike.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: it sends 3->8 18, feeding its stack in castle 8 and leaving castle 3 with 0 beside castle 2's 26. `feedStack` gives a rear castle's whole `safeOut`, which counts only the groups already landing on it, not what a stocked enemy castle next to it can send.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: also 3->8 18. Its reserve counts only an enemy castle that lands before the stack's help could: castle 2 walks 5 turns at its speed, castle 8 4, so it keeps nothing, although castle 8 held 6 and castle 2 bought speed 3 the next turn and landed in 3.

## 2. Turn 52: a strike on castle 5 with castles 1 and 7

Castle 5 (4, production 4) had just fallen to the enemy, with castles 2 and 3 beside it to refill it. We sent 10 from castle 1 and 12 from castle 7 at it, leaving 6 and 4; the enemy put 10 into castle 5 first and the strike took nothing.

**The right move:** No strike; keep castles 1 and 7 home and feed castle 8.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: feeds castle 8 (1:12, 7:12), no strike.

## 3. Turn 54: the strike on castle 5 renewed

We sent 7 from castle 1 and 8 from castle 8 at castle 5 (8, with 10 more enemy landing), leaving castle 1 with 7. Again nothing was taken. Castle 7 fell on turn 61 to a 41 that beat it by 1, castle 8 on turn 64 to a 58 that beat it by 1, and castle 1 on turn 69: the 37 soldiers spent at castle 5 on turns 52 to 54 would have held both.

**The right move:** No strike; keep the soldiers home.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: no strike; feeds castle 8 (1:10, 7:8).

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save).
