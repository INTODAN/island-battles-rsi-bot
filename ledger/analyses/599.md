# Game 599: the three main mistakes

Map gyre; the league bot pounce_reach played team 1 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 202.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 181: the front castle emptied into a hopeless strike (decided the game)

Enemy castle 9 held 9 with 94 enemy soldiers on the way (16 landing in 1, 56 in 5, 22 in 7). Castle 4 (98) sent 16 and 52 at it, and 27 and 7 more on turn 182, keeping 4; castle 2 sent 4. Castle 4 is two turns from enemy castle 5 (258, speed 4). The strike took nothing; castle 5 sent 16 that took castle 2 (4) on turn 182 and 16 that took castle 4 on turn 184. We went from four castles to two by turn 190.

**The right move:** No strike: keep castle 4's 98 (and send castle 0's stock forward to castles 4 and 2).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats in effect: no strike, but `send 0->2 x389`, `send 4->2 x98`, `send 6->2 x97`. `stackOf` makes castle 2 the stack, and `feedStack` empties castle 4 into it, as no enemy group is bound for castle 4 yet, although castle 5 lands there in 2 turns and castle 0's feed takes 5 to reach castle 2.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: `send 0->2 x146`, `send 6->2 x97`; castle 4 keeps its 98 as the reserve against castle 5, which lands before the stack's help.

## 2. Turn 170: the stock kept at the back

Castle 0 held 452 at the back of the map; castles 4 and 9, the front, held 8 each, two turns from enemy castle 5 (216, speed 4). Castle 6 sent 101 at castle 2 (taken on turn 172 with 109), castle 0 sent nothing. Castle 5 took castle 4 on turn 173 (retaken by castle 0's 107 on turn 176) and castle 9 with 37 on turn 178.

**The right move:** Send castle 0's stock to the front, to castles 9 and 4.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids for castle 9: `send 0->9 x452`, `send 4->9 x8`, `send 6->9 x158` (castle 9 is its stack); it leaves castle 4 empty.

## 3. Turn 186: a strike on a castle the enemy was filling

Castle 0 (306) sent 122 at enemy castle 2 (8), five turns away, with 42 enemy soldiers already on the way there and castle 5 (161) two turns off. It took nothing; castle 2 held 162 by turn 197, and castle 0, down to 54 after more strikes on castle 8, fell to 75 from castle 2 on turn 199.

**The right move:** No strike: keep castle 0's stock home (buy speed) and hold castles 0 and 6.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: buys speed 3 and 4 at castle 0 and feeds castle 6's 19 to it.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 170: `["send 0->9 x251"]`
- turn 181: `["send 0->2 x146", "send 6->2 x97"]`
- turn 186: `["up 0 speed", "send 6->0 x19"]`
