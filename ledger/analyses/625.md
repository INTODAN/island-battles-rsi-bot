# Game 625: the three main mistakes

Map circle (one castle each, 4 units apart, no neutrals); the league bot punisher played team 1 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; a draw on turn 300, one castle each.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake. The three are one loop seen at three turns.

## 1. Turn 10: a strike that the target outgrows (decided the game)

Castle 0 (26, production 3, speed 1) sent 25 at enemy castle 1 (25, production 3), eight turns away. Castle 1 grew 24 on the way and held about 49 when the 25 landed. The opponent never sent a soldier; we sent the same 25 every eight or nine turns, 35 times, losing each one whole, and both castles stayed at production 3 for 300 turns.

**The right move:** No strike: keep the soldiers and buy production 4 at 30 (then speed), and strike only with more than the target will hold on landing.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: sends nothing (stack at castle 0).

## 2. Turn 52: production 4 never bought

Castle 0 held 27 with nothing landing; with production 4 (30) it would grow a soldier a turn faster than castle 1 and could later pay for speed and a strike with a margin. It sent 25 again on turn 52 and never held 30 the whole game.

**The right move:** Wait one turn and buy production 4.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: sends nothing, and its `build` buys production 4 as soon as the castle can pay for it.

## 3. Turn 285: a late strike that could change nothing

With 26 at home, castle 0 sent its 25 at castle 1 once more; it landed on turn 293 on about 49. No capture was possible before turn 300 (the strike sent on turn 293 never even landed), so the game stayed a draw.

**The right move:** Keep the soldiers home.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: sends nothing.
