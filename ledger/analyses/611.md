# Game 611: the three main mistakes

Map star-of-david-duo; our bot punisher played as team 2 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 120.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 84: four strikes at once on the enemy's side

We held 7 castles to the enemy's 6. On one turn we sent 34 from castle 9 at castle 0 (41, production 3), 41 from castle 10 at castle 6 (44), 41 from castle 4 at castle 1, and 32 + 20 from castles 2 and 5 into contested castle 12, after 25 from castle 4 at castle 8 (60) the turn before. Castles 9, 10 and 4 were left with 9 to 17. The strikes took nothing; the enemy took castle 9 on turn 88, castle 4 on turn 95, castle 11 on turn 96 and the rest by turn 119.

**The right move:** No strike into castles that hold more than the strike; gather into one castle and hold castle 12.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: no strike; stack@4, it gathers 2:32, 5:20, 9:39, 10:42 and 11:28 into castle 4.

## 2. Turn 64: neutral castle 12 contested against a bigger enemy group

Neutral castle 12 (40, production 2) is the centre of the map. The enemy had 55 + 12 on the way to it, landing in 5. We sent 20 from castle 10, 7 from castle 9 and 6 from castle 11 at it anyway, then more each turn: castle 12 changed hands eight times between turns 68 and 84, and we sent about 250 soldiers at it and the enemy about 350.

**The right move:** Leave castle 12 to the larger group and keep the soldiers home (or gather them), then retake it once the enemy has spent itself on the garrison.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: stack@4, it gathers 2:8, 5:12, 9:8, 10:20 and 11:36 into castle 4, nothing at castle 12.

## 3. Turn 53: 33 from castle 9 at castle 8, 12 turns away

Castle 8 (4) was the enemy's, beside castle 7 (19). Castle 9 (43, speed 1) sent 33 at it, a 12-turn walk, leaving 3. The enemy put 10 into castle 8 on turn 55; the strike took nothing, and 7 more on turn 56 also failed.

**The right move:** No strike so far at speed 1; buy production 4 at castle 9 and send help to castle 4.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: buys production at castle 9 and feeds 9->4 13 and 10->4 12 (help for castle 4).

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 53: `["up 9 generation_amount", "send 10->9 x9"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 64: `["send 2->9 x8", "send 5->9 x12", "send 10->9 x20", "send 11->9 x36"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 84: `["send 2->9 x32", "send 4->9 x41", "send 5->9 x20", "send 10->9 x42"]` (avoids the mistake)
