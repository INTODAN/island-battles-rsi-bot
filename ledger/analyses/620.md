# Game 620: the three main mistakes

Map archipelago; the hosted bot was fullfeed_unfreeze, playing as team 2, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 92.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 72: castle 1 left to fall (decided the game)

Castle 1 held 100; the enemy sent 106 from castle 5 and 49 from castle 11, landing in 2. Castle 3 held 68 and castle 7 65, 2 turns away. We sent nothing on turn 72; castle 1 fell on turn 73 with 47 enemy soldiers left in it, and the enemy built it to 220.

**The right move:** Send castle 3's 48 or more (and castle 7's 65) to castle 1.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: `defend()` sends 3->1 48, and it feeds 3->1 20 and 7->1 65.

## 2. Turn 84: saving apart beside the enemy's stacks

We held castles 3 (116) and 7 (113), 2 turns apart; the enemy held castles 1 (220), 5 (205) and 11 (155) within 2 to 3 turns of them. We kept both; on turn 87 the enemy sent 143 at castle 3 and 232 at castle 7, took castle 3 on turn 88 and castle 7 on turn 91.

**The right move:** Gather in one castle: send castle 7's 113 to castle 3 (about 230 there holds against 143).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: sends 7->3 113.

## 3. Turn 56: a retake of castle 5 that couldn't hold

Castle 5 (enemy, 8) had our 14 landing in 1, and four enemy groups of 20 were bound for it from castles 0, 2, 4 and 6. Castle 1 sent 18 more; we took castle 5 on turn 57 with 2 and lost it on turn 59.

**The right move:** No retake: keep castle 1's 43 home.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: `smash()` sends 1->5 12 (its price for the landing in 3), buys speed at castle 1 and feeds it. The price counts the groups bound for castle 5 only up to its landing (one of 20), not the 60 that land 3 to 5 turns after it.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: no strike; it gathers in castle 3 (1->3 43, 7->3 12).

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 56: `["send 1->3 x43", "send 7->3 x12"]`
- turn 72: `["send 3->1 x68", "send 7->3 x65"]`
- turn 84: `["send 7->3 x113"]`
