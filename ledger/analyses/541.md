# Game 541: the three main mistakes

Map delta; the hosted bot was speedhelp_fullfeed, playing as team 2 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 96.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 51: castle 5 emptied into castle 3 beside the enemy's stocked castle 2

Castle 5 (17, production 4) faced the enemy's castle 2 (41), 4 turns away. We sent 5->3 17 to feed castle 3, leaving castle 5 with 4. On turn 54 castle 2 struck castle 5 with 54; on turn 55, when the strike was visible and castle 7's 16 could still land before it, castle 7 sent its 16 to castle 3 instead. Castle 5 fell on turn 59 with 18 enemy soldiers left, and the opponent fed it to 325.

**The right move:** Keep castle 5's 17 home (feed castle 5 from castle 7 instead: 7->5 12); on turn 55, 7->5 16.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: makes castle 3 its stack, buys production there and feeds it 5->3 17 and 7->3 12. (On turn 55 it does send the help, 7->5 16.)

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: its stack is castle 5, fed 7->5 12, and castle 5 keeps its 17.

## 2. Turn 65: castle 3 emptied into castle 7 beside the enemy's castle 5

The enemy's castle 5 (38) had 49, 16 and 36 more landing on it within 5 turns. We sent 3->7 32 (castle 3 kept 8; it had already sent 37 and 10 on turns 60 and 61). On turn 66 castle 5 struck castle 3 (12) with 52, then 12 more; castle 7's 17 on turn 67 could not hold it, and castle 3 fell on turn 74.

**The right move:** Keep castle 3's 36 home: with its growth it would hold 68 against the 64 that came.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats, worse: feeds its stack at castle 7 with 3->7 36 and 1->7 12.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats it too: `feedStack` makes a rear castle keep a reserve only against an enemy castle that lands on it sooner than the stack's help; castle 5 and the stack at castle 7 are both 7 turns from castle 3, so castle 3 keeps nothing.

## 3. Turn 77: groups of 9 thrown at the enemy's castle 5, then castle 7 lost by 6

Castle 7 (117) sent 7->5 9 at the enemy's castle 5 (126, production 4, with 98 more landing), as it had on turns 67, 69, 71, 73 and 75: 54 soldiers in all, each wiped out. On turn 88 castle 5 struck castle 7 (156) with 166 and took it on turn 89 with 6 left; any one of those 9s kept at home would have held it.

**The right move:** No send at castle 5; feed castle 7 from castle 1 (1->7 60).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: feeds castle 7 with 1->7 60 and sends nothing at castle 5.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 51: `["send 7->5 x12"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 65: `["send 1->7 x12", "send 3->7 x36"]` (still makes it)
- turn 77: `["send 1->7 x60"]` (avoids the mistake)
