# Game 648: the three main mistakes

Map gyre; the league bot punisher played as team 2 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 132.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 81: small strikes that never took anything

Castle 5 (32) sent 10 at enemy castle 4 (16) and 13 at castle 2 (20), and castle 3 had sent 21 at castle 2. From turn 52 to 102 castles 5, 3 and 7 sent over 30 groups of 8 to 21 at castles 2, 4 and 6; none was taken, and our castles stayed under 40 each while the enemy's grew.

**The right move:** No strike smaller than the target: feed castle 5 and let it grow into a stack.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: stacks on castle 5, 1->5 12, and strikes nothing.

## 2. Turn 114: castle 5 left to fall

45 from castle 9 and 38 from castle 4 landed on castle 5 (74) in 2; on turn 116 castle 4 sent 42 more. Castles 1 (76), 7 (64) and 3 (54) sent nothing to it: castle 1 sent 15 at castle 6, then castle 1 21 at castle 2 and castle 7 29 at castle 4 on turn 117. Castle 5 fell on turn 118 (9 in it), and the enemy stacked in it.

**The right move:** Speed at castle 1 and help castle 5, then feed it from castles 1, 3 and 7.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: buys speed at castle 1, help 1->5 2, production at castles 3 and 8, and feeds castle 5 with 1->5 54, 3->5 24, 7->5 64.

## 3. Turn 121: castle 1 left to fall

65 from castle 9 landed on castle 1 (46) in 3 and 57 from castle 4 in 4. Castle 7 (46) sent 21 at castle 6 and 25 at castle 3, and castle 3 (49) sent 42 at castle 2. Castle 1 fell on turn 123, castle 3 on 127, castle 7 on 128 and castle 8 on 131.

**The right move:** Speed at castle 3 and its 49 to castle 1 (lands in 3), castle 7's 46 to castle 1 (lands in 4): about 150 against 122.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats in part: buys speed at castle 3 and sends help 3->1 8, which holds the first landing by one, then makes castle 3 the stack and sends castle 7's 46 there instead of to castle 1, so the 57 landing a turn later takes castle 1. `defend()` sizes help to the first landing that takes the castle (it stops at that landing), and `feedStack` then spends the helpers' rest.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: the same commands; the fix does not change `defend()`.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 81: `["send 1->5 x12"]`
- turn 114: `["up 1 speed", "send 1->5 x2", "up 3 generation_amount", "up 8 generation_amount", "send 1->5 x54", "send 3->5 x24", "send 7->5 x64", "send 8->5 x18"]`
- turn 121: `["up 3 speed", "send 3->1 x8", "up 8 generation_amount", "up 8 generation_amount", "send 7->3 x46"]`
