# Game 514: the three main mistakes

Map bight; the hosted bot was batchfront, playing as team 2, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 100.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 72: castle 2 emptied into a far strike beside enemy castle 3 (decided the game)

Castle 2 (the centre) held 57; enemy castle 0 held 12, 8 turns away, and had already absorbed our 40, 20 and 16 sent on turns 64 to 67. Enemy castle 3 held 53 at speed 3, 2 turns from castle 2. We sent 56 from castle 2 to castle 0, keeping 5. Castle 0 never fell; on turns 73 and 74 castle 3 sent 26 and 7 and took castle 2 on turn 76. From then on we held only castles 1 and 5.

**The right move:** Keep castle 2's 57 home (buy speed there) and feed it from castle 5: a strike at castle 0 must beat 4 soldiers a turn of growth over 8 turns plus castle 3's help.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: buys speed 3 at castle 2 and feeds it 5->2 14; no strike, since `smash()` prices castle 0 at its stock at the landing plus the enemy castles that can help it.

## 2. Turn 47: 56 soldiers thrown at castle 4, twelve turns away

Castle 5 held 61; enemy castle 4 held 4 but was 12 turns away at speed 2 and grows 4 a turn, and castles 0 and 3 stood beside it. We sent 56 from castle 5 to castle 4; it never fell and the 56 were lost.

**The right move:** Send castle 5's soldiers to the central castle 2 (4 turns) and build there.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: feeds its stack at castle 2 with 5->2 61.

## 3. Turn 83: castle 5's 44 thrown at castle 3

Castle 5 held 45 and castle 1 only 5; enemy castle 3 held 4, 8 turns away, with enemy castle 2 (35) beside it. We sent 44 from castle 5 to castle 3, keeping 5; the strike failed, and castle 5 fell on turn 94 to 62 from castle 2.

**The right move:** Keep the 45 home in castle 5 and buy speed there.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: buys speed 3 at castle 5 and sends nothing.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 47: `["send 5->2 x61"]`
- turn 72: `["up 2 speed", "send 5->2 x14"]`
