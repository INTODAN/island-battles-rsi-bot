# Game 485: the three main mistakes

Map claw; the hosted bot was pounce, playing as team 2; won, the last team standing on turn 106.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 78: a siege in small waves while home sat idle

We sent 4->5 16 and 7->5 22 at castle 5 (20, production 4), 4 turns away, then more waves on turns 83, 85, 87, 89, 91 and 93; castle 5 fell only on turn 96, while castle 1 held 67, then 119, unused and enemy castle 3 grew from 38 to 84.

**The right move:** Gather one stack: 1->4 67, 6->4 16, 7->4 22, then one strike.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: exactly those three feeds, and again on turns 83 and 89.

## 2. Turn 91: a long wave at the enemy's home

We sent 4->0 4 and 6->0 30 at castle 0 (4, production 4), 8.2 away and 6 turns, about 28 at landing; it was worn down in clashes and trickles followed on turns 93 to 100. Castle 0 fell only on turn 105.

**The right move:** No attack: buy production at castle 2 and feed castle 4 (1->4 119, 6->4 39, 7->4 9).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: exactly that.

## 3. Turn 46: a second wave that could not hold castle 4

Our 32 was capturing castle 4 (20) in 1 turn; we also sent 1->4 17, landing in 6. The enemy's 16 from castle 0 (in 4) and castle 3's 11 retook castle 4 on turn 49, and the 17 landed on an enemy castle.

**The right move:** Feed the front instead (1->6 26), no second wave.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: feeds castle 6 (1->6 26, 7->6 12).
