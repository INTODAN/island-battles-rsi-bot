# Game 485

**Map:** `claw`. **The bot that played:** `pounce` (the hosted bot then; its code is in git at `3f95877^:bots/pounce/main.go`, deleted in iteration 47), as team 2 (Red). **Result:** won as the last team standing on turn 105, 8 castles and 315 soldiers to 0. Every one of its 105 turns was accepted; the slowest took 264 ms.

Both sides took their near neutrals by turn 14 (castles 6 and 7 for the hosted bot). The hosted bot then lost the race for the middle: it held castle 4 for one turn (25 to 26) and let the opponent take castle 2 on turn 48, and it was out-produced from turn 21 to 61 (13 to 10 a turn, then 16 to 14). It took castle 2 on turn 54 and castle 4 on turn 69 with a strike from three castles, and led from there (18 to 12 a turn by turn 71). It still needed 35 more turns to finish, because its strikes on castles 5 and 0 were sized to the exact count and bounced off the opponent's reinforcements one after the other. Meanwhile its home castle grew to 175 soldiers and sent nothing after turn 64.

## Mistakes

### 1. Strikes sized to the exact count, then trickled after them (turns 32 to 38, 49 to 54, 78 to 105)

- **Castle 5, turns 78 to 96:** seven strikes, `pounce>5 in 4 need 38 4:16 7:22` (turn 78), `need 24` (83), `need 12` (85), `need 39` (87), `need 32` (89), `need 13` (91), `need 12` (93). In all, 170 soldiers for `turn 96: capture: castle 5 Blue (6 soldiers) -> Red (2 soldiers)`. The first strike missed by 2: a group of 4 the opponent sent from castle 0 on turn 79 clashed with the hosted bot's 16 (16 to 12, turn 81), and castle 5 was left with 2. Over those turns the opponent fed castle 5 about 77 soldiers in groups of 1 to 26.
- **Castle 0, turns 91 to 105:** `pounce>0 in 6 need 34 4:4 6:30`, then follow-ups of 3, 23, 3, 4 and 24. That was 91 soldiers for `turn 105: capture: castle 0 Blue (10 soldiers) -> Red (2 soldiers)`. The 30 lost 4 in a clash on turn 94, and castle 0 had grown from 4 to 30 with help from castle 3.
- **Castle 2, turn 49:** `pounce>2 in 5 need 44 6:10 7:14 1:20`. Its three groups walked 4, 4 and 5 turns and landed apart. The opponent fed castle 2 with 11 and 8 on turn 50, and the castle was left with the opponent at 0, then 1. A follow-up of 5 took it on turn 54.
- **Castle 4, turn 32:** `pounce>4 in 6 need 20 1:20`. On turn 33 the opponent bought speed 2 at castle 3 and sent 3, which left castle 4 at 1 for the opponent on turn 38. All 20 were lost.

**Cause.** `strikeOption` prices a target as its count at landing plus `strikeExtra` (2) plus `helpFor`. `helpFor` sums each enemy castle's `enemySpare`, which assumes every enemy castle keeps the main line's reserve against the hosted bot's largest castle, wherever that is. The idle home stack (61 to 175) therefore made every enemy castle look pinned, with a spare of 0. Yet castle 0 sent 12 on turn 65 and 4 on turn 79, and castle 3 sent 7 and 3 to castle 0 after turn 91. `helpFor` also uses each castle's current speed, so it missed castle 3's speed purchase on turn 33. `sendFrom` then sends exactly `need`.

**What would have avoided it:**
- Count an enemy castle's help as what it holds above about 4 (this opponent spends castles down to 4), within its walk.
- Count only the castles that can actually reach the enemy castle when working out the reserve it must keep.
- Send a real margin, or everything the castles that land in time can spare. Castle 6 held 36 to 52 on turns 83 to 87 and gave 0 to 17.

### 2. The middle lost: castle 4 through a split, slow payment, castle 2 never tried (turns 14 to 48)

- **Castle 4.** On turn 14, `take>4 6:5 1:15` paid castle 4 with 5 from castle 6 (a 7-turn walk) and 15 from home (5.8 units at speed 1, 12 turns). The 5 landed alone around turn 20 and cut the neutral to 5. The opponent sent 18 on turn 15, which was on the board from turn 16 and was never answered. `turn 25: capture: castle 4 neutral (5 soldiers) -> Red (10 soldiers)` was followed by `turn 26: capture: castle 4 Red (10 soldiers) -> Blue (7 soldiers)`, logged `help>4 in 1` with no helper.
- **Castle 2.** It stayed neutral at 10 until the opponent took it on turn 48. The hosted bot logged waiting on turns 28 to 31 and 35 to 40 with 48 to 73 soldiers at home. The neutral's price (10 + 2 + `hold`, where `hold` was 23 on turn 31 and 32 on turn 32) was more than two payers could give. From turn 33, when the opponent bought speed 2 at castle 3, the rule `sooner(enemies) < sooner(own)` marked it as the opponent's side. Castles 6 and 7 walked at speed 1 (8 turns to the centre) until turns 46 and 47, because `nextUpgrade` buys production 4 before any speed.
- **Cost.** Taking castle 2 back cost 49 soldiers, against the 12 the neutral needed. Castle 4 cost 89 soldiers in turns 14 to 52 for 4 turns of holding.

**Cause.** `neutralOption` allows `neutralPayers` = 2 payers whatever their walk, and returns nil once any of its own groups is bound for the target, so a pending capture is never re-priced or topped up. `defend` covers only castles the hosted bot already owns.

**What would have avoided it:**
- Pay a middle neutral from one near castle, or time the groups to land together.
- Re-price a pending neutral when an enemy group heads for it.
- Buy speed 2 in castles 6 and 7 before production 4.

### 3. The home stack never fought after turn 64 (turns 64 to 105)

Home castle 1 grew from 15 (turn 65) to 79 (turn 81), 119 (turn 91) and 175 (turn 105), over half of the final 315. Its last send was 50 on turn 64. The hosted bot had the most soldiers from turn 71 (262 to 118 by turn 91) and still took 34 more turns to win.

**Cause.** `punishWalk` = 6 allows only strikes that land within 6 turns. At speed 2, home is 9 turns from castles 3 and 5 and 10 from castle 0. `topSpeed` = 2 in `nextUpgrade` never buys speed 3, which would make those walks 6, 6 and 7 turns. `defend` uses only helpers with `travel <= fall`, and the bot stages nothing, so no rule moved the stack forward to castle 2. The idle stack also caused mistake 1, through `enemySpare`.

**What would have avoided it:** a castle with nothing left to buy feeds the castle nearest the enemy, or buys speed 3 when every target is beyond `punishWalk`.

### 4. Captures that fell, or held only by a tie (turns 41 to 52, 69 to 73)

- **Turn 46.** `pounce>4 in 6 need 32 1:32` took castle 4 with 11. The opponent's 16 and 11 retook it on turn 49 (`capture: castle 4 Red (13 soldiers) -> Blue (13 soldiers)`), while the logs `help>4 in 3`, `in 2` and `in 1` named no helper. Castle 6 was still at speed 1 and 4 turns away.
- **Turn 69.** Castle 4 was taken with 2, after 12 of the 76 sent died in a clash. On turn 70 the bot sent those 2 away to help castle 2 (`help>2 in 3 4:2 6:8 7:7`), with an enemy group of 12 walking at castle 4. The castle held only by a tie: on turn 73 it showed 0 soldiers.

**Cause.** `strikeOption` prices only the capture at its landing, not holding it after. `defend` lets helpers spend their `safeOut` down to a margin of 0.

**What would have avoided it:** price a capture to hold through the enemy help that lands after it, and keep a margin of at least 1 in a helper.

### 5. Border castles fed down beside a stocked enemy castle (turns 56 to 63; not punished)

- **Turn 56.** `help>2 in 3 speed3@6 6:3 speed3@7 7:3` spent 40 soldiers on speed 3 to send two groups of 3, both of which died in clashes on turn 58. Castle 6 fell from 23 to 4 beside castle 4, which held 48 for the opponent.
- **Turn 59.** Castle 6 sent all 12 it had to castle 2.
- **Clashes.** Of the 126 soldiers sent to castle 2 in turns 56 to 74, 121 died in clashes. They traded one for one, and castle 2 held to the end.

**Cause.** In `defend`, helpers ignore their own `threat`: a castle that would certainly fall outweighs one that may be struck.

## What it did well

- It had no rejected or late turn.
- It bought production 3 at home on turn 1.
- Its three-castle strike on turn 64 took castle 4, and it held castle 2 from turn 54 to the end under many waves, with `defend` counting the landings exactly.
- It finished with strikes from several castles at once (`pounce>3 in 5 need 69 4:13 5:6 6:26 7:24`, castle 3 on turn 103).
- Its home was never threatened.

## Against the ledger's lessons

- **The middle castles lost early: confirmed.** Castle 4 was lost on turn 26 after a 12-turn walk, and castle 2 was never tried before the opponent took it on turn 48.
- **Trickled attacks: confirmed, strongly.** There were seven strikes on castle 5 and six on castle 0, plus the turn-14 neutral payment of 5 that landed alone.
- **Captures with few soldiers fall again: confirmed in part.** Castle 4 fell 3 turns after it was taken with 11, and held by a tie after it was taken with 2. The later captures with 2 held only because the opponent was collapsing.
- **Long walks at speed 1: confirmed.** See turn 14, and castles 6 and 7 at speed 1 until turns 46 and 47.
- **A border castle left thin beside a stocked enemy castle: seen.** Castle 6 was left thin on turns 56 and 59, though it was not punished.
- **Saving apart while an enemy stack grows: seen from this side.** The idle home stack of up to 175 is the hosted bot's own hoard. It also distorted the bot's estimate of the opponent's spare soldiers.
- **Out-produced: confirmed** for turns 21 to 61. It turned only after the middle was taken.
- **Help in small groups dying in clashes: confirmed.** It cost tempo here, not castles.
