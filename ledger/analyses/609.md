# Game 609: the three main mistakes

Map star-of-david-duo; punisher played as team 1 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost on turn 161.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 130: castles 6 and 8 left to fall by a few soldiers (decided the game)

After fifty quiet turns the enemy's stack in castle 12 (298) and castle 11 sent 249+44+4 to castle 6 (272) and 263+4 to castle 8 (242), landing in 5. Castles 1 (228), 3 (252) and 7 (255) were ours. We sent nothing; both castles fell on turn 134 by about 10 each (288 to 5 and 258 to 5).

**The right move:** Buy speed at castles 3 and 1 and send a few soldiers each to castles 6 and 8, which then hold.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: `up 3 speed`, `send 3->6 x6`, `up 1 speed`, `send 1->8 x6` (help>6 and help>8 in 5).

## 2. Turn 136: a spray over five castles

The enemy had 122 landing on castle 0 and 464 on castle 1. Castle 3 sent 165 to castle 1 (right), but castle 7 (279) sent 17, 27, 27, 29 and 33 to castles 12, 6, 8, 9 and 4. Only castle 8 was retaken (with 2, on turn 139) and lost again on turn 147.

**The right move:** No spray: help castle 1 with castle 7 too, or take castle 6 (9) alone with what it needs.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: `send 7->1 x177` (help>1), the rest fed to its stack; no spray.

## 3. Turn 150: castle 7 left to fall

Castle 7 (127) had 111 (from castle 12), 29 and 4 (from castle 6) landing in 3. Castle 3 (147, speed 3) needed 4 turns, or 3 with speed 4. We sent nothing to castle 7 (8 from castle 3 went to castle 0); castle 7 fell on turn 152 with 135 in it.

**The right move:** Buy speed 4 at castle 3 and send it to castle 7 (a few soldiers hold the landing).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: `up 3 speed`, `send 3->7 x6` (help>7 in 3, speed4@3).

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 130: `["up 3 speed", "send 3->6 x6", "up 1 speed", "send 1->8 x208", "send 0->7 x98", "send 3->7 x226"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 136: `["send 7->1 x177", "send 0->3 x104", "send 3->7 x276"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 150: `["up 3 speed", "send 3->7 x6", "send 1->7 x50", "send 3->7 x111"]` (avoids the mistake)
