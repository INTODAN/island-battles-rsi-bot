# Game 509: the three main mistakes

Map bight; the league bot was batchfront, playing as team 1, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 101.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 47: a 12-turn strike while the enemy took the middle

Castle 4 (61) sent 56 at castle 5 (4, production 4), 12 turns away. The same turn the enemy's 52 landed on castle 2 (16), beside our castle 3 (4); castle 2 sent 27 at castle 3, which fell on turn 52, and the strike was worn down and never took castle 5.

**The right move:** Move the 61 into castle 3, the castle facing castle 2.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: feeds 4->3 61 (its stack is castle 3).

## 2. Turn 72: castle 3 emptied into a strike beside castle 2's 54

Castle 3 (57) sent 56 at castle 1 (17), after 20 and 16 on turns 66 to 67, keeping 5; enemy castle 2 (54, speed 3) was 5 away. Castle 2 sent 30 the next turn and castle 3 fell on turn 76; castle 1 was never taken.

**The right move:** Keep castle 3's 57 (and feed it 4->3 14); it outnumbers castle 2 and holds.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: keeps the 57 and feeds 4->3 14.

**juggernaut_fix:** Avoids: keeps everything home.

## 3. Turn 84: castle 4 emptied into a strike beside a stocked castle 3

Castle 4 (41) sent 40 at castle 2 (4), then 4 to 16 more on turns 85 to 87, keeping 5; enemy castle 3 (40, with 52 more landing in 3 to 7) was 5.1 away. Castle 3 sent 42 on turn 87 and took castle 4 on turn 92; castle 0, our last, fell on turn 100.

**The right move:** Keep castle 4's 41 and feed it from castle 0 (as the 12 already walking there).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: buys speed at castle 4 (its stack) and keeps the rest home.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save).
