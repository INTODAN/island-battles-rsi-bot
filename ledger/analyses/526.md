# Game 526: the three main mistakes

Map estuary; the league bot was batchfront, playing as team 1, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 77.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 60: the front emptied for a neutral beside the enemy's 54

Castle 4 (53, production 4) sent 52 at neutral castle 6 (25) and castle 0 sent 5 more, keeping 5; enemy castle 5 (54, with 36 more landing) was 2 away. Castle 5 sent 30 the next turn and took castle 4 on turn 62; castle 6 was taken on turn 63 with 27 but fell on turn 69.

**The right move:** Keep castle 4's 53 and feed it (0->4 13, 2->4 13): it faces castle 5's stack.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: feeds 0->4 13 and 2->4 13 to its stack at castle 4.

## 2. Turn 49: a small strike at a castle the enemy was stacking

Castle 0 sent 12 at castle 5 (4), after castle 2's 8 on turn 48, while the enemy's 20 and 24 were landing on castle 5 in 2 and 4 turns. Both were lost and castle 5 grew to 54.

**The right move:** Feed the front castle 4 (0->4 17) instead.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: feeds 0->4 17.

## 3. Turn 66: a 24-soldier strike while the home castles were next

Castles 0 and 2 sent 12 each at castle 7 (2, production 2), then 24 more on turns 68 to 69, while enemy castle 4 (28, with 43 more landing) sat 2.8 from castle 0. Castle 7 was never taken; castle 4's 25 took castle 0 (13) on turn 72 and castle 2 fell on turn 76.

**The right move:** Keep the 24 home: gather at castle 0 (2->0 13), which then holds against 25.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: feeds 2->0 13 to its stack at castle 0.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 49: `["send 0->4 x17"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 60: `["send 0->4 x13", "send 2->4 x13"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 66: `["send 2->0 x13"]` (avoids the mistake)
