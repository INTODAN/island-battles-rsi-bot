# Game 614: the three main mistakes

Map archipelago; our bot was punisher, playing as team 1, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 92.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 54: a trickle at castle 10 that never retook it

The enemy had just taken castle 10 (3) and was sending 47 more into it. Castle 6 (46) sent 21 at it; from turn 54 to 59 six groups, 71 soldiers from castles 0 and 6, landed one by one and never retook castle 10, which held 57 by turn 62 and became the base of the enemy's attacks.

**The right move:** No retake: gather castles 2, 4 and 6 into one castle and keep producing.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: gathers at castle 0 (2->0 8, 4->0 16, 6->0 46). juggernaut_fix gathers at castle 2 (0->2 8, 4->2 16, 6->2 46).

## 2. Turn 72: castle 6 left to fall with its 52

Castle 6 (52) had 80 and 11 landing in 3 and 43 and 16 in 4, 150 in all. Castles 0 (82) and 2 (56) could not hold it. We sent nothing: castle 6 fell on turn 74 with its soldiers, and the enemy then had 187 there, 1.8 from castle 2.

**The right move:** Pull castle 6's 52 back into castle 2 (2 turns) and keep castles 0 and 2 together; castle 6 cannot be held.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: it leaves castle 6's 52 there and sends 2->6 28 help, which holds the first wave by 1 and falls to the second; it also feeds 2->0 28.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: 2->6 28 help and 0->2 82. `defend` sizes the help against the landings up to the help's arrival (3 turns) and ignores the 59 landing one turn later.

## 3. Turn 85: no help for castle 2 (decided the game)

Castle 2 (108) had 79 landing in 1 and 125 in 3; castle 0 (134) is 1.8 away (2 turns). We sent nothing; castle 2 fell on turn 87 (84 left to the enemy) and castle 0 fell on turn 91 to 168.

**The right move:** Send castle 0's 134 to castle 2: it lands between the two waves and castle 2 holds about 50 after both.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: 0->2 85 help and 0->2 49 feed, all 134. juggernaut_fix does the same.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 54: `["send 0->2 x8", "send 4->2 x16", "send 6->2 x46"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 72: `["send 2->6 x56", "up 0 speed", "send 0->6 x27", "send 0->2 x35"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 85: `["send 0->2 x85", "send 0->2 x49"]` (avoids the mistake)
