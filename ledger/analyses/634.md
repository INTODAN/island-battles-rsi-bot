# Game 634: the three main mistakes

Map circle (two castles, 4 apart); our bot punisher played team 2 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; a draw at turn 300.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

The opponent never sent a soldier and stayed at production 3. We sent 25 at its castle every 8 or 9 turns, 35 times; each group walked 8 turns at speed 1 and met the 25 or so it had held plus the 24 it grew on the way, so no group came near taking it. All our production went into those groups, castle 1 never reached the 30 that production 4 costs, and a game we could out-grow ended in a draw.

## 1. Turn 10: the first throw, 25 at a castle that would hold 49

Castle 1 had 26, castle 0 had 25 (production 3, speed 1, 8 turns away). We sent 1->0 25; castle 0 had about 49 when it landed and kept 24.

**The right move:** Send nothing: keep growing to 30 and buy production 4 (then speed), and strike once castle 1 holds more than castle 0 will at the landing.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: sends nothing (its stack saves for production and speed before a smash).

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: sends nothing.

## 2. Turn 18: the second throw, after the first had visibly failed

Castle 0 had 24 after the first group, castle 1 had 25. We sent 1->0 25 again, with the same result; the cycle then ran for 280 turns.

**The right move:** As on turn 10: no send, save for production 4.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: sends nothing.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: sends nothing.

## 3. Turn 285: still throwing with 15 turns left

Castle 1 had 26, castle 0 had 25. We sent 1->0 25 (and once more on turn 293); the game ended with castle 0 on 45 and castle 1 on 21.

**The right move:** No send; nothing could be taken in 15 turns, and keeping the soldiers costs nothing.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: sends nothing.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: sends nothing.
