# Game 474: the three main mistakes

Map claw; the hosted bot was feedkeep_speedhelp, playing as team 1; lost, eliminated on turn 109.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 96: castle 3 lost by one soldier (decided the game)

The enemy's 98 from castle 4 landed on castle 3 in 2 turns; castle 3 had 89 + 8 growth = 97. Castles 0 and 5 had about 92 each beside it. We sent nothing; castle 3 fell on turn 97 and the enemy's stack took castle 5 (turn 105) and castle 0 (turn 108).

**The right move:** Buy speed at castle 0 and send 0->3 10 (lands in 2); any 2 would do. Better, feed castle 3 from both rear castles from turn 80 on instead of saving apart.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: up 0 speed, help 0->3 2, then feeds 0->3 70 and 5->3 92 (on turn 95 it already feeds 88 + 88).

## 2. Turn 69: a deep strike that can't hold

We bought speed at castle 3 and sent 3->7 26 at castle 7, 7.2 away, which had 4 because it had just sent 28 to castle 4. We captured it with 2 on turn 73 and lost it on turn 74; follow-ups on turns 75 and 77 were lost too: about 110 soldiers and a speed level.

**The right move:** No strike; keep the 54 in castle 3 and keep feeding it.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Equivalent mistake: smash>4 with 39 landing in 4, while the enemy's 28 + 28 bound for castle 4 land in 5. On turns 75 to 77 it also made the falling castle 7 its stack and fed it 103.

**Proposed fix (not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed):** A capture is priced against what nearby enemy castles will hold by the landing, incoming groups counted (it buys speed 3 and 4 at castle 3 instead), and a castle that falls before any feed could land is never chosen as the stack (it gathers at castle 3).

## 3. Turn 23: a neutral paid in drips

We sent 5->2 5 and 3->2 9 at neutral castle 2 (10, an 8-turn walk), leaving castles 3 and 5 with 3 and 2, and kept dripping on turns 25 and 27: 28 soldiers against the enemy's 8 + 7 + 7. Castle 2 was taken with 4 on turn 30, lost on turn 31, and changed hands about eight times by turn 38, while castle 4 got no help and fell on turn 35.

**The right move:** Send nothing; let castles 3 and 5 grow, and help castle 4 from castle 3 by turn 29.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: sends nothing on turns 23 and 25, buys production on 27, and helps castle 4 on 29.
