# Game 478: the three main mistakes

Map claw; the hosted bot was speedhelp_landlate, playing as team 2; won, the last team standing on turn 116.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turns 75 to 104 (turn 83 checked): strikes at a castle its neighbour refills

We sent 4->3 26 at castle 3 (8, production 4, about 24 at landing) while enemy castle 0 (16) was 2.8 away; the strike left castle 3 at 3. We repeated it on turns 75, 76, 78, 85, 89, 91, 93, 96, 98, 100 and 102.

**The right move:** No strike while castle 0 can reinforce castle 3; keep castle 4 as the stack and buy speed there.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: buys speed at castle 4 (its price counts castle 0's whole stock).

## 2. Turn 44: a strike that left castle 4 without a helper

We sent 6->2 13 at castle 2 (3), leaving castle 6 at 5; the enemy saw castle 4 at 9 and sent 21 + 9 at it. Castle 6 could no longer cover the 10 castle 4 lacked and it fell on turn 50; castle 2 fell back on turn 51.

**The right move:** Send nothing; castle 6 keeps 16 as castle 4's reserve.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: sends nothing on 44, helps castle 4 on 46 (6->4 7) and 47 (6->4 9, 1->4 8).

## 3. Turn 88: a capture with 2 beside stocked castles

We sent 4->2 15, taking castle 2 with exactly 2 next to enemy castle 5 (12, production 4, 3 turns away) and castle 0 (16); they sent 7 + 13 and retook it on turn 91. Castle 2 changed hands six times from turn 86 to 91.

**The right move:** Keep the 15 in castle 4 (it had 34), or send about 30.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeated: smash>2 with 15.

**Proposed fix (not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed):** The capture's price counts castles 5 and 3 by what they will hold at the landing; juggernaut buys speed at castle 4 and keeps the soldiers.
