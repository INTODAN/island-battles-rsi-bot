# Game 490: the three main mistakes

Map claw; batchfront played as team 1 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost on turn 87 (castle 0, the last, fell on turn 85).

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 71: castle 2 emptied into the enemy home (decided the game)

Castle 2 (the stack, 57) had sent 52 and 64 into castle 1 on turns 63 and 67, each time refilled by the enemy. On turn 71 it sent 52 more into castle 1 (69) and kept 9, with enemy castle 4 (85) 3 away. The enemy sent 26 from castle 4 to castle 2 and 63 to castle 5 on turn 72: castle 2 fell on turn 74, castle 5 on turn 80, castle 0 on turn 85.

**The right move:** No strike: keep the 57 in castle 2 (it holds the 26 easily) and let castles 0 and 5 feed it.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats in another form: `send 2->0 x57`. Its stack is the own castle nearest an enemy castle (`stackOf`); since the enemy took castle 3, that is castle 0, so the whole of castle 2 is fed back to castle 0 and castle 2 is left empty beside castle 4's 85.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: sends nothing; the feed keeps what an enemy castle that lands before the stack's help would take, so castle 2 keeps its 57.

## 2. Turn 65: castle 3 left to fall

An enemy group of 34 from castle 4 landed on castle 3 (9, growing 4 a turn) in 3 turns. We sent only 4 from castle 0; castle 3 fell on turn 67 with 17 in it, and the enemy kept it.

**The right move:** Help with about 14: castle 0's 5 and 9 from castle 2 (with speed bought there), as castle 3 then holds with about 35 against 34.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: `send 0->3 x5`, `up 2 speed`, `send 2->3 x9`, `send 5->2 x9` (help>3 in 3).

## 3. Turn 59: a strike on the enemy home within reach of three stocked castles

Castle 1 (the enemy home) held 20, but enemy castles 6 (67), 4 (59) and 7 (36) were all within about 4 turns of it. We sent 53 from castle 2 (106) plus 12 from the rear; the enemy fed castle 1 26 on turn 60 and 44 on turn 64, and three more waves (52, 64, 52) on turns 63 to 71 never took it, while castle 2 kept thinning.

**The right move:** No strike: keep castle 2's 106 at home and let the rear feed it.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: sends nothing (stack@2, 121 home).

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 59: `[]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 65: `["send 0->3 x5", "up 2 speed", "send 2->3 x9", "send 5->2 x9"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 71: `["send 2->0 x57"]` (still makes it)
