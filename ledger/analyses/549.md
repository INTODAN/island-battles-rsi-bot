# Game 549: the three main mistakes

Map gyre; our bot pounce played as team 1 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost on turn 149.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 110: a rear hoard and small retakes of castle 9

Castle 0 held 212 and never sent from turn 58 to 124. Castles 2, 4 and 6 sent 2->9 10, 6->9 31 and 4->9 25 at castle 9 (enemy, 4, with 44 landing in 4 to 6); it was taken with 3 on turn 114 and lost on turn 117. Castle 9 changed hands 10 times between turns 79 and 120.

**The right move:** Gather in castle 2: 0->2 212, 4->2 25, 6->2 37.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: exactly those feeds.

## 2. Turn 125: a retake of castle 4 that couldn't hold

Castle 4 had just fallen (enemy, 4); the enemy's castle 5 held 113. We sent 0->4 84, then 35, 15 and 14; castle 4 was retaken with 2 on turn 133 and lost on turn 135 to 99 from castle 5.

**The right move:** No retake: 0->2 272, 6->2 19.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: exactly that.

## 3. Turn 133: castle 2 left beside the enemy stack

Castle 2 (24) stood 1.8 from castle 9 (enemy, 125, with 78 landing in 1); castle 0 had 156 and castle 6 43. We sent nothing; castle 9 sent 34 and castle 2 fell on turn 135, then 164 took castle 6 and castle 0 fell on turn 148.

**The right move:** 0->2 156 and 6->2 43 (castle 6's 43 lands in 3, with the 40 the enemy sent on turn 134).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: exactly those feeds.
