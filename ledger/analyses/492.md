# Game 492: the three main mistakes

Map claw; our bot pounce played as team 1 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 120.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 58: a strike on castle 4 while castle 2 was left to fall

Castle 2 (24, production 1, just taken) had enemy groups of 28, 4 and 2 landing in 2 to 3 turns. We sent only 8 from castle 3 to it, and struck castle 4 (52, 12 more landing in 1, four enemy castles feeding it) with 64 from castle 0, 26 from castle 5 and 4 from castle 3. The strike left castle 4 with 15 and took nothing; castle 2 fell on turn 63. About 94 soldiers spent for no castle, and the centre castle lost for good.

**The right move:** Hold castle 2: 3->2 8 plus about 25 from castle 0 (lands in time), and keep the rest home.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: help>2, buys speed at castle 3 and sends 3->2 8, 0->2 64, 5->2 34 (all its stock into the threatened castle, which becomes its stack).

## 2. Turn 90: three castles saving apart while castle 4 stacked

From turn 79 the enemy fed castle 4 (adjacent to our castle 3) with every rear castle: 218 on turn 90, 370 on turn 100, 578 on turn 112. We kept 63 in castle 0, 33 in castle 3 and 121 in castle 5, apart, and grew them apart until turn 113, when castle 4 split 134 into castle 3 (129) and 229 plus 100 into castle 5; castle 3 fell on turn 114, castles 0 and 5 on turn 119.

**The right move:** One stack: feed castle 3 (the front castle facing castle 4) from castles 0 and 5, so no enemy split can take a castle.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: stack@3, sends 0->3 63 and 5->3 121 (and the same on turn 100: 0->3 103, 5->3 161).

## 3. Turn 65: a long strike on castle 7 that emptied the front

Castle 7 (4) was on the far side, 5 to 6 turns away, with castles 1 and 6 beside it. We sent 15 from castle 5 and 28 from castle 3 (leaving castle 3 with 4, next to castle 4). The enemy put 16 into castle 7 before they landed; the strike took nothing, and on turn 69 castle 4 sent 33 at castle 3.

**The right move:** No strike so far away; keep castle 3's 28 home and gather castle 0's and 5's soldiers into it.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: stack@3, sends 0->3 28 and 5->3 36.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 58: `["up 3 speed", "send 3->2 x8", "send 0->3 x64", "send 5->3 x34"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 65: `["send 0->3 x28", "send 5->3 x36"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 90: `["send 0->3 x63", "send 5->3 x121"]` (avoids the mistake)
