# Game 638: the three main mistakes

Map estuary; our bot batchfront_keep played as team 2 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost on turn 114.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 90: saving apart beside the enemy stack (decided the game)

Castles 1, 3 and 5 held 69 each; the enemy's castle 4 held 140 at speed 3, 2 turns from castle 5. We sent nothing; on turn 90 castle 4 sent 78 at castle 5, which fell on turn 91 by 1.

**The right move:** Gather in castle 5: 1->5 69, 3->5 69 (207).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: exactly 1->5 69 and 3->5 69.

## 2. Turn 95: more soldiers at a castle we could not take

Castle 6 (enemy, 37, production 1) had our 40 landing in 4 and 81 enemy landing by then. We sent 1->6 41 and 3->6 41, leaving 30 and 26; all of it died at castle 6. On turn 100 the enemy sent 65 at castle 3 (fell on 104) and on turn 111 82 at castle 1 (fell on 113).

**The right move:** Gather: 3->1 67.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: 3->1 67.

## 3. Turn 71: a strike at a castle the enemy was feeding

Castle 6 (enemy, 14) had 30 enemy landing in 2 and 60 of ours dribbling in groups of 12. We sent 5->6 50 from castle 5 (90); castle 6 stayed the enemy's.

**The right move:** smash neutral castle 7 (25, far from the enemy) with 27, feed castle 5 (1->5 13, 3->5 13).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: exactly that.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 71: `["up 5 speed", "send 1->5 x13", "send 3->5 x13"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 90: `["send 1->5 x69", "send 3->5 x69"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 95: `["send 3->1 x67"]` (avoids the mistake)
