# Game 550: the three main mistakes

Map gyre; pounce played as team 2 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost on turn 101.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 84: castle 3 helped too little (decided the game)

Enemy groups of 82 (from castle 4) and 21 (from castle 2) landed on castle 3 (13, production 3) in 3 to 4 turns. We sent 28 from castle 7 and 33 from castle 1 (which kept 42), then 14 and 4 on turn 85; castle 3 fell on turn 86 with 47 in it, the first castle we lost.

**The right move:** Send everything that lands in time: castle 7's 28, castle 1's 75 and castle 5's 28 into castle 3.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: `send 7->3 x28`, `send 1->3 x33` (help>3 in 3), then feeds castle 3 with `1->3 x42` and `5->3 x28`.

## 2. Turn 86: two strikes that took nothing

With castle 3 falling, castle 5 sent 26 to enemy castle 4 (8, fed by castle 0) and 10 to castle 2, and castle 1 sent 24 to castle 2 (12). Neither castle was taken; castle 5 was left with 4 and castle 1 with 16, beside the enemy's castles 3 and 8.

**The right move:** No strike: keep castles 5 and 1 stocked, or gather them into one.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: no strike; feeds 36 from castle 1 and 36 from castle 5 toward castle 3 (to retake it as its landings thin).

## 3. Turn 90: saving apart beside castle 8

Enemy castle 8 held 77 (48 more landing) 3 away from castle 7 (20) and 2 from castle 5 (16); castle 1 held 28. We sent nothing but 2 from castle 1. Castle 8 sent 38 to castle 7 (fell on turn 92), then 53 to castle 5 (36), which fell on turn 95; castle 1 fell on turn 100.

**The right move:** Gather castles 1 and 7 into castle 5 (64 there, which holds the 53).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: `send 1->5 x28`, `send 7->5 x20` (stack@5).

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 84: `["send 7->3 x28", "send 1->3 x75", "send 5->3 x20", "send 5->3 x8"]`
- turn 86: `["send 1->5 x36"]`
- turn 90: `["send 1->5 x28", "send 7->5 x12"]`
