# Game 513: the three main mistakes

Map bight; our bot pounce, playing as team 1, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 189.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 14: neutral castle 3 taken too thin to hold

Castle 3 (neutral, 15) lay 11 turns from our castle 4 and 16 from the enemy's castle 1 (17). We sent 0->3 16 and 4->3 2; the capture landed on turn 28 with 3. The enemy sent 15 on turn 15, which landed on turn 30 and took castle 3 with 10. The castle beside our castle 4 was the enemy's for the rest of the game.

**The right move:** Take castle 3 with 15 more (wait two to three turns of production), or leave it for a retake after the enemy pays for it.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: send 4->3 2 and 0->3 16. In `neutralOption` the capture must hold what each enemy castle could spare less `rate(target)*travel(enemy, target)`: the capture's growth over the enemy's whole 16-turn walk, although the enemy's group can leave at once and land 2 turns after ours.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats the same sends: the fix reprices strikes at enemy castles, not neutrals.

## 2. Turn 32: neutral castle 2 taken too thin to hold

Castle 2 (neutral, 15) was 8 turns from our castle 0 and 8 from the enemy's castle 1 (4, growing 4 a turn). We sent 0->2 17; it landed on turn 39 with 2, and the enemy's 7, sent on turn 34, took it on turn 41. From then on we had 2 castles producing 8 a turn against 4 producing 16.

**The right move:** No capture of a neutral the enemy reaches as soon as we do; keep the 17 home (or send enough to hold 4 turns of castle 1's production).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: send 0->2 17 and 0->4 9 (the same `neutralOption` price; a tie of walks counts as our side).

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats the same sends.

## 3. Turn 138: saving apart while the enemy stacks

The enemy had just emptied castles 1, 5 and 2 (4, 4 and 6 left) into castle 3 (978, with 209 more landing). Our castle 0 held 433 and castle 4 366, both at speed 2, and stayed apart and still for 40 turns. Castle 3 grew to 1737, struck castle 4 with 910 on turn 178, and our help from castle 0 went in three pieces (361, 130, 118), leaving castle 0 with 4: castle 0 fell on turn 188 and castle 4 the turn before.

**The right move:** Merge castle 0 into castle 4 and buy speed there, so one stack of 799 lands on castles 5 or 1 before castle 3's answer.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: buys speed 3 and 4 at castle 4 and sends 0->4 433.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save).
