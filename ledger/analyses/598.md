# Game 598: the three main mistakes

Map gyre; our bot burstguard played as team 2 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 83.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 63: a strike on castle 2 that emptied castles 1 and 3

Castle 2 (28, production 4) was the enemy's, beside castles 4 (20) and 6, which refilled it with 15 and 6 before our groups landed. We sent 36 from castle 1 and 14 from castle 3 (leaving 0 and 4); the strike took nothing. Castle 3 fell on turn 68 to 20 from castle 8, and castle 1 never recovered its stock.

**The right move:** No strike; gather castles 1, 5 and 7 into castle 3, the castle facing the enemy's castle 8.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: stack@3, feeds 1:36, 5:15 and 7:58 into castle 3.

## 2. Turn 66: 30 soldiers on burst potential in castle 7

Castle 7 (70) was our largest castle; the enemy was feeding castle 8 (57) next to castles 3 and 5. We bought burst potential level 3 at castle 7 (10 + 20 soldiers), after levels 2 and 3 at castle 5 on turns 60 and 65 (30 more). No burst was ever ordered: the 60 soldiers bought nothing.

**The right move:** Feed castle 3 with castle 7's 70 (and castle 1's 12), so that castle 8's 20 and 38 cannot take castles 3 and 5.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: no burst upgrade; stack@3, feeds 1:12 and 7:70 into castle 3.

## 3. Turn 68: burst level 4 bought while castles 3 and 5 fell

Castle 8 sent 20 at castle 3 (14, landing in 1) and 38 at castle 5 (15, landing in 2). We bought burst potential level 4 at castle 7 (30 more, leaving about 18) and sent 16 from castle 1 to castle 5. Castle 3 fell on turn 68, castle 7 on turn 76 to 40 + 5 + 7 + 8, castle 5 and castle 1 on turn 82.

**The right move:** No upgrade; send castle 1's 16 to castle 5 and castle 7's 48 to retake castle 3 (or to castle 5).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: help>5 1:16, and feeds 7->3 48 behind the 20 that takes castle 3.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 63: `["send 1->3 x36", "send 5->3 x15", "send 7->3 x58"]`
- turn 66: `["send 1->3 x12", "send 7->3 x70"]`
- turn 68: `["send 1->5 x20"]`
