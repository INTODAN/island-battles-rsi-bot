# Game 630: the three main mistakes

Map delta; the hosted bot was batchfront_keep, playing as team 1 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 90.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 68: castle 6 not helped against a second wave, while castles 0 and 2 struck castle 5

Castle 6 (21, production 4) had 48 from the enemy's castle 3 landing in 2 and 26 more in 4; our 20 from castle 4 landed in 1, enough for the first wave only. Castle 4 (25) was 3 turns away. We sent 0->5 8 and 2->5 9 at the enemy's castle 5 (12, fed) and nothing to castle 6, which fell on turn 71 by about 17.

**The right move:** Help castle 6: 4->6 18 (it then holds by about 1), and no strike at castle 5.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: help>6 4->6 18, and feeds castle 2 (0->2 9).

## 2. Turn 71: castle 4 sent 24 at castle 5, then fell to 45

Castle 4 (49, production 4) faced the enemy's castle 3 (54). It sent 4->5 24 at castle 5 (18, production 2, fed by the opponent), keeping 29; the strike, like every group sent at castle 5 since turn 52 (about 150 soldiers), failed. On turn 75 castles 3 and 6 sent 22, 18 and 5 at castle 4 (then 22); castle 4 fell on turn 78 and castle 0 on turn 81.

**The right move:** Keep castle 4's 49 home (feed it from castle 0); with the 24 it would have held the 45.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats in effect: it doesn't strike castle 5, but feeds its stack at castle 2 with 4->2 49, leaving castle 4 empty beside castle 3.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: its stack is castle 4, fed 0->4 13, so castle 4 keeps its 49.

## 3. Turn 55: the first strike at castle 5, while castle 2 fell

The enemy had just taken neutral castle 5 (10, production 1); its 41 and 5 were taking our castle 2 (21) in 2 to 3 turns, past any help. We sent 0->5 12 and 4->5 22 (with 5 and 8 sent on turns 52 and 54), 8 turns' walk; the opponent fed castle 5 29 on turn 56 and all of it was lost. Castle 2 fell on turn 57.

**The right move:** No strike at castle 5: gather (0->4 13, 6->4 13) and retake castle 2 after it falls.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: no strike; it feeds castle 2 (0->2 13, 4->2 47, 6->2 13) to retake it.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 55: `["send 0->4 x13", "send 6->4 x13"]`
- turn 68: `["send 4->6 x18", "up 2 generation_amount", "send 0->4 x9"]`
- turn 71: `["send 0->4 x13"]`
