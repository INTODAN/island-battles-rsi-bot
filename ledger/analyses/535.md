# Game 535: the three main mistakes

Map gyre; our bot batchfront played as team 1 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost on turn 60.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 40: a neutral capture that emptied the front (decided the game)

Castle 4 (48, production 4) stood 2.2 from the enemy's castle 3 (34, with 28 landing). We sent 4->9 40 at neutral castle 9 (25), leaving castle 4 with 12; castle 9 was taken with 15 on turn 43 and lost on turn 44 to castle 5's 20, and castle 3 sent 41 at castle 4 on turn 41, which fell on turn 44.

**The right move:** Keep castle 4's 48 home (and 0->4 9).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats an equivalent: 4->2 48 and 0->2 9, emptying castle 4 into its stack castle 2 (feedStack gives a rear castle all its safeOut, which counts only groups already bound for it, not castle 3 two turns away).

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats an equivalent: smash>8 with 27 at neutral castle 8 (25), 1.8 from castle 3; smash prices a neutral target at its soldiers plus 2, without the enemy castles that can retake it.

## 2. Turn 48: castle 0 emptied into a falling castle

Castle 2 (26) had 36 enemy landing in 1 to 2 and fell on turn 49; castle 0 (13) stood 3 from the enemy's castle 4 (21). We sent 0->2 12, landing after the fall; castle 4 sent 21 at castle 0 on turn 49 and it fell on turn 51.

**The right move:** 6->0 13, keep castle 0.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: 0->2 13 and 6->2 13 into castle 2, its stack, which falls first (stackOf).

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: 6->0 13.

## 3. Turn 47: a strike at castle 9 from the castle that needed its soldiers

Castle 9 (enemy, 6) had 24 enemy landing; castle 0 (21) stood beside the enemy's castle 4 (17). We sent 0->9 12, leaving 13.

**The right move:** Send nothing from castle 0.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats an equivalent: buys speed 3 at castle 0 (about 20) to send castle 2 one soldier of the 7 it lacks; defend's slow-help rule buys speed whenever the helper can still give one, however short the help falls.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Repeats: the same speed and 0->2 1.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 40: `["send 0->4 x9"]`
- turn 47: `["send 6->0 x9"]`
- turn 48: `["send 6->0 x13"]`
