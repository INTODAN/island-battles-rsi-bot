# Game 520: the three main mistakes

Map delta; our bot batchfront played as team 1 in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 98.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 68: a strike on castle 5 while castle 2 was left to fall

An enemy group of 37 from castle 3 landed on castle 2 (21, production 2) in 5. We sent 36 from castle 4 and 12 from castle 0 at castle 5 (24, fed by castles 3 and 7) instead. The strike took nothing, and castle 2 fell on turn 72 with 29 in it.

**The right move:** Hold castle 2: buy speed at castle 4 and send its 49 (or part of it) and castle 0's 13 to castle 2.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: help>2, buys speed at castle 4 and sends 4->2 7, then feeds castle 2 with 0:13, 4:22 and 6:13.

## 2. Turn 74: castle 4 emptied beside castle 3

Castle 4 (37) was the front castle, 5 units from enemy castle 3 (57). We sent 24 from it at castle 5 and 12 at castle 2, leaving 5. Castle 3 sent 30 at it on turn 76, and castle 4 fell on turn 79; the 36 and 24 sent at castle 5 on turns 68 to 74 took nothing.

**The right move:** No strike; keep castle 4's 37 home and feed it from castles 0 and 6.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats in kind: it stops the strikes but gathers into castle 0, sending 4->0 37 and 6->0 13, which leaves castle 4 empty beside castle 3's 57. `stackOf` takes the own castle nearest an enemy castle by walk at speed 1 and breaks the tie (castles 0 and 4 are both 11 turns away) by the lowest id.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: stack@4, feeds 0->4 13 and 6->4 13; its tie goes to the castle nearest the other own castles.

## 3. Turn 55: the strikes on castle 5 begin

Castle 5 (10, production 1) was the enemy's, beside castles 7 (30) and 3 (39), which kept feeding it. On turn 55 we sent 22 from castle 4 and 12 from castle 0 at it, after 5 and 8 on turns 52 to 54; between turns 52 and 75 about 220 soldiers went at castle 5 in groups of 4 to 36 and it never fell.

**The right move:** No strike on a castle two enemy castles can refill; gather castles 0 and 6 into castle 4 and hold castle 2.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: no strike; it gathers 0:13, 4:47 and 6:13 into castle 2.

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games of this set were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended (strikes and neutral takes priced to hold against nearby enemy castles at the speeds they can afford, a neutral paid only by castles that land together, upgrades kept back when an enemy castle could buy speed and land, a castle meeting an attacking group with half its size, no help sent to a castle it cannot save). Not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed. On the same boards it plays:

- turn 55: `["send 0->4 x13", "send 6->4 x13"]`
- turn 68: `["up 4 speed", "send 4->2 x7", "send 0->4 x13", "send 6->4 x13"]`
- turn 74: `["send 0->4 x13", "send 6->4 x13"]`
