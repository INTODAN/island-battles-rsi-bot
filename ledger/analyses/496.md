# Game 496: the three main mistakes

Map star-of-david-duo; the league bot was batchfront, playing as team 1, in a server match against the opponent's hosted bot; lost, eliminated on turn 100.

Each mistake is pinned to the turn where a different set of commands, on that turn alone, was clearly better. Each board was then given, alone, to the league's champion juggernaut (one turn, the same board), to see whether it makes the same mistake.

## 1. Turn 55: a 15-turn strike from the front while castle 8 faced the enemy's 55

Castle 6 (44, production 4) sent 43 at castle 4 (5, production 1), 15 turns away; the group was worn down on the way and never took it. The same turn castle 9 (55) sent 53 at castle 8 (28), which fell on turn 60 with 43 in it.

**The right move:** Keep castle 6's stock home as the front's reserve and feed castle 8 from castle 7; strike only within a few turns.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids the strike: sends 6->0 44 and 7->0 9 to its stack at castle 0 and buys production there. That keeps the soldiers but moves them to the far corner (see mistake 2).

## 2. Turn 59: castle 6 emptied into the corner beside castle 11's 32

Castle 6 (25) sent 24 to castle 0 (13, production 2), keeping 5, with enemy castle 11 (32) 6 turns away. Castle 11 sent 34 the next turn and castle 6 fell on turn 65 with 25 in it; nothing could land in time.

**The right move:** Keep castle 6's 25 (it holds against 34 with its growth) and feed it from castle 7 (7->6 13).

**juggernaut on the same board:** Repeats: feeds 6->0 25 and 7->0 13. `stackOf` picks the castle nearest an enemy by walk with ties by id: castle 0 (5.77 from castle 11) ties castle 6 (5.78) and wins on id, so `feedStack` empties castle 6, the front, into a production-2 corner. `feedStack`'s `safeOut` counts only groups already bound for the castle, not castle 11's stock.

**juggernaut_fix (proposed, not yet measured nor deployed):** Avoids: breaks the tie by the shortest distance to the other own castles, gathers at castle 6, buys speed there and feeds 7->6 13.

## 3. Turn 70: a 50-soldier strike 11 turns long

Castle 1 (51) sent 50 at castle 9 (8), 11 turns away, past enemy castle 8 (46); it followed with 24 on turn 73. Both were lost on the way, and castle 3 (turn 77), castle 7 (37, turn 79), castle 0 (turn 85) and castle 1 (turn 91) fell.

**The right move:** Keep the 50 and gather it with castle 0's 23 at castle 7, the castle that castle 8's 41 hit on turn 74.

**juggernaut on the same board:** Avoids: buys speed at castle 0 and feeds 1->0 51, 7->0 13.

**juggernaut_fix:** Avoids: feeds castle 7 (0->7 23, 1->7 51).

## The final proposed fixes on these boards

juggernaut_fix above was an early version. After all the games were studied, the champion's proposed changes were extended and each of these boards was judged again (one turn on the same board; not yet measured in a tournament nor deployed). The proposed champion plays:

- turn 55: `["send 7->6 x9"]` (still makes it)
- turn 59: `["send 8->1 x40", "send 7->6 x13"]` (avoids the mistake)
- turn 70: `["send 0->7 x23", "send 1->7 x51", "send 3->7 x19"]` (avoids the mistake)
