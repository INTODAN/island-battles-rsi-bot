## Findings

The four real games were all played by the hosted bot of that time, `speedhelp_landlate` (the `ori` line), on `claw` (477, 478, 480) and a 13-castle map (481); 477 and 478 against one opponent, 480 and 481 against another player's hosted bot. Each was read by its own subagent on Haiku, and so were six of the champion's league games (loch, causeway and estuary against `batchfront_keep`, haven against `growtrickle_feedkeep`, breakwater against `punisher`, and a 300-turn win on bight against `ori_mimic`). The decisive lines below were checked again (`digest --turns`) or in the candidates' rematches.

1. **Help in pieces dies on the road** (`trickled_attack`, `turning_point`). Real game 477, turns 60 to 66: groups of 4 into castle 7 met the opponent's groups of 1 to 7 and died. estuary (`32a5477a…`) turns 55 to 61: `help>4` of 2 to 10 a turn, two groups of 2 and 4 destroyed by one group of 6 on turn 60, castle 4 lost on 62; causeway (`cae8e91f…`) turns 53 to 57, groups of 4 to 8 against groups of 8 to 12, castle 4 lost on 57; loch (`30087a3a…`) turns 46 to 52.
2. **A neutral's payers land apart** (`trickled_attack`). estuary turn 34, `take>4 0:20 2:13`: the 20 (speed 2, 3 turns) landed alone against 25 and lost, the 13 (speed 1, 8 turns) took it on turn 42; turn 40 `take>6 0:24 2:4`, the same. causeway turn 37, `take>5 0:17 8:9`: the 9 landed on 46 alone, the 17 on 50. Iteration 41 saw it on bayou.
3. **Captures it can't hold** (`turning_point`). haven (`97b4517d…`): castle 5 changed hands nine times (turns 23 to 57), each `pounce>5 ... need 10` leaving 2 soldiers that an enemy castle 4 turns away retook. Real game 478, turns 86 to 91: exact strikes took the centre castle 2 with 2 beside two stocked castles, retaken the next turn, six times.
4. **An upgrade empties the only helper of a thin castle** (`turning_point`, `thin_border`). breakwater (`edb7c93f…`) turn 23: `production4@0` took castle 0 from 30 to 4 while border castle 2 held 2; it fell on 26 with `help>2` finding no helper. Real games 478 (turn 45) and 480 (turn 48): production 4 at home while a border castle was attacked, lost 5 and 9 turns later. haven turn 20 looked the same, but the rematch showed the helper was 7 turns away at speed 1 either way.
5. **The stock stands where it can't strike** (`long_walk`; iteration 41's finding). loch turns 26 to 46: 40 to 50 soldiers in castles 10 to 11 turns from every enemy castle, `waiting`, first strike on turn 55. bight against `ori_mimic`, turns 60 to 75: 206 to 386 at home, `waiting`, won only at the limit. Real game 481, turns 81 to 101: the `ori` bot held 400 to 640 soldiers in five castles, `saving`, while the opponent, feeding one hub castle of 200 and more and striking from it with groups of 90 to 110, went from 6 castles to 11 and from production 20 to 44.
6. Also seen: a strike whose senders land 8 and 12 turns out (bight turn 270, 742 then 163, the first lost alone); a take that leaves the castle a reserve that made the enemy's spare 0 (breakwater turn 41: 36 sent from castle 0, after which enemy castle 6's 14 was spare and took castle 2); `speedhelp_landlate`'s `attacked` tests enemy groups one by one, never their sum (real game 477, turn 67); its feed sent 22 on a 30-turn walk to a rear castle from a border castle that then fell (481, turn 55).

No detector was added: each finding is a kind the detectors already flag (`trickled_attack` covers groups landing alone, `thin_border` and `home_emptied` the thin castle).

## Ideas not tried

- A strike's senders land apart: bight against `ori_mimic`, turn 270, 742 sent 8 turns out and 163 sent 12 turns out at castle 5; the first lost alone. `pounce_together`'s rule for strikes (`strikeOption` joins every sender with a walk up to the landing turn).
- A take or send that leaves a castle thin also frees the enemy's spare against its neighbours: breakwater turn 41, 36 sent from castle 0 to a neutral, after which enemy castle 6's 14 counted as spare and took castle 2 on turn 44. Price what a send does to `enemySpare` against the other own castles.
- In haven, the only helper of castle 5 was 7 turns away at speed 1 (turns 20 to 23): speed before production for a castle whose neighbour depends on it.
- estuary turns 29 to 34: `pounce` lost the production race because its upgrade check counted its whole threat, not what lands before the level pays back.
- The `ori` line (for its place): `attacked` judges enemy groups one by one, never their sum (real game 477, turn 67, four groups of 1 to 14 totalling 27 at home castle 1); its feed sends to a rear castle on a 30-turn walk from a border castle that then falls (real game 481, turn 55); reserves against every enemy castle froze 400 to 640 soldiers (481, turns 81 to 101), as iteration 37's idea of one shared reserve said.
- The ideas of iterations 40 and 41 stand.
